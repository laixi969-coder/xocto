## Design Context

### Users

x-octo serves globally distributed AI product builders, operators, investors and
curious decision-makers. They arrive to scan what changed, decide what deserves
attention and, when needed, read the evidence behind a product judgment. Chinese
and English are equal editorial editions, not a primary site and its translation.

### Brand Personality

Calm, sharp and credible. The product should feel like an independent global
product-intelligence desk: it saves readers time without performing urgency or
turning every signal into a trend.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use The Information as the structural reference for professional information
density, and Stratechery as the reading reference for analysis. Avoid portal-like
navigation, generic startup card grids, regional-tech-media cues, decorative
effects and visual noise. Light mode is the editorial default; dark mode remains
an equally considered reading environment.

### Design Principles

1. Editorial judgment before inventory: show what matters before showing all data.
2. Make Chinese and English editions structurally equal and independently useful.
3. Give scanning, comparison and long-form reading distinct layouts and rhythms.
4. Use type, rules and spacing to establish hierarchy; containers are exceptional,
   not the default.
5. Every visual emphasis must communicate evidence, confidence or an actionable
   next step.
