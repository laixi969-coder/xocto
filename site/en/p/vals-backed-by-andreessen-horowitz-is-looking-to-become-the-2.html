Why this would be needed

Start inside the user's day · Public facts + observable behavior · 2026-10-07

Use case

A model evaluation engineer or materials research team, when judging whether a model can handle a specific task such as materials screening, works with the model's outputs on that task to produce comparable, citable evaluation conclusions.

Teams build their own evaluation scripts, consult public leaderboards, or choose based on trial impressions; environmental cost is roughly estimated from token price.

Public material shows teams need to compare token usage and environmental cost across models and task types, yet token price is an imperfect proxy for energy use and limited infrastructure disclosure constrains measurement; without a unified standard, selection and acceptance conclusions are hard to align.
