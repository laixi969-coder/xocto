The work as it happens

Selecting target audiences and keywords when brand merchants run product ads on e-commerce platforms

Media buyers preset tags from experience, manually combine related keywords and audience packages, and expand reach through repeated trial and error

In a saturated market, label-based audience selection around renovation and furniture tags stops delivering growth, and high-intent but non-obvious audiences like esports players get missed

xOcto's call

Demand is evidenced

Trend: ad targeting is shifting from human-selected labels to AI reasoning directly over consumption intent, pulling growth judgment away from media buyers' experience. Entry: build intent-based audience reasoning for channels outside Alibaba's ecosystem—DTC sellers, local service merchants—where targeting is still manual, and charge for placement outcomes rather than selling seats.

Adoption motive

Why someone would keep it in the workflow

It turns audience selection from experience-based guessing into intent-chain reasoning with concrete placement suggestions, giving growth-pressured merchants a direct reason to switch
