The work as it happens

Developers and enterprise teams open Claude while coding, drafting documents, or analyzing materials; it takes code, documents, or questions and returns code, rewritten text, and analysis conclusions; users receive ready-to-use code or drafts, review them, and deliver.

Competing frontier models from OpenAI and Google, self-hosted open-source models, and human outsourcing or simply hiring more staff.

Software and writing work is expensive and slow; outsourcing and template tools deliver unstable quality, and urgent requests cannot wait in queue.
