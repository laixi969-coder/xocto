Use case

A heavy Mac user or person with motor impairments who frequently switches windows, scrolls and clicks takes gesture signals from the Meta Neural Band and must achieve hands-free control of the Mac.

Keyboard shortcuts, trackpad gestures, voice control or assistive switch devices; some rely on dedicated hardware such as foot pedals.

Public material contains only a one-line feature description and does not state the concrete burden, frequency or user complaints caused by keyboard-and-mouse work, so pain intensity and rigidity cannot be confirmed.

xOcto's call

Problem identified, demand strength unclear

The trend is input shifting from keyboard and mouse toward wearable EMG signals, starting with heavy users and people with motor impairments. An entry point could be accessibility or hands-free operation in specific occupations such as surgical teaching or labs, sold as device plus software; but public material is only one feature line and community attention, not enough to tell a stable product from a technical demo.
