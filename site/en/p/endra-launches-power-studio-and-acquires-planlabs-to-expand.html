Why this would be needed

Start inside the user's day · Public facts + commercial validation · 2026-10-06

Use case

Electrical engineers in the MEP design phase take load lists, circuit assignments and distribution requirements as inputs and must produce a deliverable electrical design scheme and calculation documentation, synced into coordinated workflows such as Revit.

Engineers today build schemes by hand in general CAD, spreadsheets and standalone calculation software, review them item by item, and manually keep models and drawings consistent in Revit.

Load calculations, circuits and distribution schemes are long built and code-checked item by item by hand; the work is repetitive and a single parameter change ripples through the whole calculation and drawing set, making rework costly.

xOcto's call

Demand is evidenced

Trend: regulation- and calculation-heavy engineering steps such as MEP are being split into vertical agents that generate schemes. Entry: start from the specific node of load calculation and distribution design in electrical engineering, sell to design institutes or MEP contractors, and charge per project or per scheme output rather than per generic seat.

Why users would choose it
