Why this would be needed

Use case

Motion-graphics or short-video post-production people, after receiving rough AI-generated footage, use this agent skill to process that footage so it looks like it came from a motion designer, delivering a cut closer to professional motion-design quality.

Public material does not describe how users currently work around this problem, so the old alternative (such as manual clip-by-clip adjustment in editing and motion software, or discarding AI footage and building from scratch) cannot be confirmed.

Public material contains only a one-line product description, with no input format, processing steps, human intervention points, or delivery results, so it cannot be confirmed whether the pain of substandard AI footage look actually exists or how strong it is.

xOcto's call

Problem identified, demand strength unclear

Trend: the bottleneck in AI video is moving from whether it can generate to whether the output looks professionally made, and taste plus post-production conventions are being packaged into reusable processing flows. Entry point: target short-video and ad production teams taking client work, and sell polishing AI footage into client-ready cuts per video, rather than building yet another generation tool.
