Why this would be needed

Public facts + workflow reasoning · 2026-08-30
The work as it happens

Office workers need to handle repetitive tasks across applications and files, such as organizing materials, filling forms, and generating reports.

Currently, tasks are done manually or with RPA/macros, which are complex to configure and maintain.

Manually switching between multiple software and copy-pasting data is time-consuming and error-prone, reducing productivity.

xOcto's call

Demand is evidenced

Trend: AI evolves from conversational assistants to agents that operate software, with office scenarios as a key battleground. Entry: target repetitive vertical processes like finance or HR, charge per outcome, but watch competition from existing ecosystems like DingTalk and Feishu.
