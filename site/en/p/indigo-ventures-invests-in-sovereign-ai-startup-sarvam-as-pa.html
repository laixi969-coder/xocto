Why this would be needed

The work as it happens

Provide AI models tailored to Indian languages and regulations for enterprises and government, reducing dependence on foreign models.

Using international general models (e.g., GPT) or building in-house teams, which is costly and less effective.

Current international models perform poorly on Indian languages, and there are data sovereignty and compliance risks.
