Use case

A non-emergency call taker at the Boulder Police Department answers calls about noise, lost property or minor disputes that need no patrol response, and must capture the details by hand and decide whether to dispatch or refer.

Today a human call taker answers, writes down, classifies and routes the call, or residents use web forms and municipal hotlines. The candidate material does not describe the existing process.

The candidate material states no specific pain point for call takers or residents; it can only be inferred from the role that non-emergency volume is high, repetitive and consumes police capacity while callers wait. This is inference, not a reported fact.

xOcto's call

Useful problem, weak urgency

The trend is that public agencies are handing the low-value, high-occupancy non-emergency call flow to AI before touching the 911 emergency line. An entry point is small and mid-size police departments or municipal 311 lines with tight budgets and high dispatcher turnover, priced per call handled or per share diverted. Misrouted escalation and liability must be solved first, or procurement will not sign.
