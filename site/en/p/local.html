The work as it happens

Mac users want to run AI locally to protect privacy and reduce latency.

Currently users rely on cloud AI services or manually configure local models; the former has privacy risks, the latter has high technical barriers.

Cloud AI poses data privacy risks and network latency; users are concerned about data security.

Demand is evidenced

The trend is local AI moving from developer tools to mainstream users, with data privacy and offline capability as selling points. Entry could start with specific professional scenarios like legal or medical document processing, emphasizing data staying on device.
