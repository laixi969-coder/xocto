Why this would be needed

Start inside the user's day · Public facts + commercial validation · 2026-10-08

Use case

A Bay Area restaurant, when a customer expresses an ordering intent through an AI agent, must expose menu, availability and orderable data to the agent and finally confirm and fulfill an order.

Customers pick items one by one in DoorDash-style menus, and restaurants confirm orders manually through platform backends.

The available evidence only says Bites does agentic ordering with about 300 restaurants signed up; it does not say which step blocks customers or restaurants, how often, or what is lost without it.

xOcto's call

Problem identified, demand strength unclear

The trend is that the ordering entry point may shift from humans browsing menus to AI agents placing orders, which re-prices platform commissions and traffic allocation. A wedge is restaurant-side order intake and menu structuring for small restaurants unwilling to depend on a single platform's cut; pricing is undisclosed and not assumed.
