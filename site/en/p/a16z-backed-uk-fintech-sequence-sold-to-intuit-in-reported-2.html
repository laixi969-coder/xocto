Why this would be needed

Start inside the user's day · Public facts + commercial validation · 2026-10-17

Use case

Finance or operations staff at startups, at month-end close or collection points, handle contracts, usage records and payment flows to issue invoices, chase payment and recognize revenue, ending with a reconcilable billing and revenue view.

Finance staff manually invoice, reconcile and chase payment with spreadsheets plus a payment gateway dashboard, or use legacy billing software with manual re-entry.

When usage-based and subscription billing mix, manual line-by-line reconciliation between spreadsheets and payment dashboards is error-prone, collection cycles stretch, and revenue data lags behind decisions; this is a structural pain inferred from product positioning and the legacy workflow, not user testimony.
