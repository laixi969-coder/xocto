Why this would be needed

Start inside the user's day · Public facts + workflow reasoning · 2026-10-18

Use case

Analysts at private credit or lending firms, when receiving internal questions, must compare valuations across loans and read credit agreements clause by clause to produce a reply.

Previously analysts paged through PDF agreements by hand, compared valuations row by row in spreadsheets, and replied by email or verbally.

Agreements are long with scattered clauses, so manual line-by-line comparison is slow and error-prone, while follow-ups carry reply deadlines.
