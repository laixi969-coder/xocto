Why this would be needed

Start inside the user's day · Public facts + commercial validation · 2026-10-20

Use case

Marketing teams or creators lacking filming resources but needing batch short-video assets before a campaign or post hand text or image prompts to Kling to generate clips, then manually select and edit them into publishable videos.

The old route is self-filming, buying stock video or outsourcing an editing team, with high per-clip cost and long lead times, and late changes require re-organizing shoots.

Filming and stock footage are costly and take days, and script changes force reshoots, while campaign and publishing demand for video assets is continuous and high-volume; missing assets directly delays launch.

xOcto's call

Demand is evidenced

Trend: video generation is moving from demos into ad-asset production, with competition shifting to usable-output rate and cost. Entry: avoid building a general video model; start from high-frequency, fixed-format assets such as e-commerce product videos or local-store clips, selling finished videos per piece rather than generation credits.

Reason to use it

Why users would choose it

Inference: versus filming or sourcing stock, Kling turns 'getting a shot first' from organizing a shoot or purchase into generating candidate clips from a prompt, so users only select and edit among outputs, skipping filming and sourcing; marketing teams and creators without filming resources would choose it when racing to produce assets.
