The work as it happens

Directors or storyboard artists plan shots, maintain continuity, and generate usable generation prompts in AI filmmaking.

Currently, they may use general AI chat tools or manually write prompts, lacking a dedicated planning tool for film production workflows.

Manual shot planning and continuity maintenance are time-consuming and error-prone; generation prompts are scattered across tools and hard to manage.

xOcto's call

Demand is evidenced

The trend is AI filmmaking moving from single-point generation to industrialized workflow management. The entry point is building professional tools for directors and storyboard artists, not a generic prompt platform; paid templates or team collaboration features around shot planning and continuity checks could be viable.

Why someone would keep it in the workflow
