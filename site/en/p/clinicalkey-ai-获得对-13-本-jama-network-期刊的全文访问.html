Why this would be needed

Start inside the user's day · Public facts + commercial validation · 2026-10-08

Use case

Before rounds or while writing notes, clinicians need to search authoritative medical journals for evidence on a specific clinical question and verify its provenance; medical librarians configure institution-wide full-text access.

Clinicians previously used PubMed search, institutional subscription databases, or manually paging through journals; librarians negotiated and configured access database by database.

Medical evidence is scattered across subscription databases and journal platforms; searching each, reading each article, and confirming provenance is slow, while clinical decisions demand high evidence reliability and errors are costly.

xOcto's call

Demand is evidenced

The trend is that professional publishing content is being wired into clinical question-answering tools, making content licensing itself the moat; an entry point is other verticals with exclusive full-text corpora, such as case law or engineering standards, charging for authoritative searchable content rather than model capability.

Why users would choose it

Compared with searching each database, ClinicalKey AI reads licensed journal full texts directly and returns sourced answers, removing the cross-database lookup and provenance-checking step; by inference, evidence-oriented clinicians would choose it before care decisions, though no clinician usage or retention evidence is public.
