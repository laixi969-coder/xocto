2026-10-09 · Switzerland · Pre-seed

Visorway

Undisclosed

Tech.eu Funding Explorer ↗ AI-native enterprise services / procurement advisory automation

Visorway is a Zurich-based AI-native procurement advisory service that codifies expert sourcing knowledge into on-demand AI services for corporate procurement teams and consulting firms.

Work it replaces

Replaces traditional external procurement consultancies, freelance sourcing experts, and the practice of hiring and training internal procurement staff.

Business judgment

Visorway's core business judgment is that the bottleneck in procurement advisory is the scarcity and non-scalability of expert knowledge, not a lack of tools; replacing traditional consulting and freelancers with AI-native services could create a new delivery category between software and consulting.
