Real-demand verdict

Real-demand verdict

Real demand

Pet businesses all looked alike with the same palettes and pre-made logos, so a niche studio offering distinct branding found paying clients. Revenue grew from about $4K to $12K a month, peaking at $22K, which suggests real demand.

How it makes money

Pet brands pay per project for branding, packaging, web and social visuals; payments run through PayPal/Stripe, with revenue growth coming mainly from raising prices over time.

What old behavior it replaces

Pet businesses previously used generic pre-made logos and templated visuals, or hired generalist designers who did not know the industry.

02

Where the first customers came from

The founder was already embedded in Grand Rapids' dog community, and her first clients were dog walkers and pet sitters.

03

Tactics you can copy

  1. 01Pick a vertical you are already part of and use that network to land your first clients.
  2. 02Post design tips weekly in the Facebook groups where your target clients hang out instead of running hard ads.
  3. 03Turn the requests you keep getting into fixed service packages, then raise prices as demand steadies.
Moving it to an AI business

The core playbook is vertical positioning plus community-led content. For AI products, pick a niche you know, publish concrete use cases where that niche gathers, and package the most repeated needs into fixed tiers.
