How We Launched A Notebook For Chefs That Went Viral On Kickstarter -

Real demand

A UK design team built a waterproof, greaseproof stone-paper notebook for chefs, seeded it to famous chefs, launched on Kickstarter, and now does roughly $40K a month with 45,000+ units sold.

01

Real-demand verdict

Real-demand verdict

Real demand

It replaces ordinary notebooks that slide on steel counters and get ruined by water and grease — a concrete, recurring problem. Organic chef advocacy plus 45,000 units sold shows people keep paying.

How it makes money

Chefs and kitchen professionals buy the notebooks directly, mostly via the website and Instagram ads; the Kickstarter campaign pre-sold about $250K in one run.

What old behavior it replaces

Ordinary paper notebooks that slide on kitchen surfaces and can't survive water or grease.

02

Where the first customers came from

Sent prototypes to about 80 respected chefs for feedback, then spent three months gifting product to top chefs and industry figures before the Kickstarter launch.

03

Tactics you can copy

  1. 01Gift the product to the most credible people in the niche and let them post about it instead of buying reach.
  2. 02Put a high-production product video on the site next to an email signup form to convert viewers into leads (that video pulled 5,000+ leads).
  3. 03During the Kickstarter, keep engaging backers with small incentives so they feel part of the journey and share it.
Moving it to an AI business

The 'seed industry authorities first, let their content drive demand' playbook transfers directly to AI tools: give accounts to credible practitioners in a niche in exchange for real usage feedback and public endorsement — cheaper and more credible than paid feeds.
