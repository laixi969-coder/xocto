How it makes money

Franchisees pay for the hardware plus franchise fees; the company earns from selling equipment, software and ongoing support, while franchisees charge event hosts per booking.

Tactics you can copy

  1. 01Design the hardware to break into a few pieces so a single franchisee can move it in and out of venues
  2. 02Run a cloud backend for event graphics, green screens and borders so franchisees can customize remotely
  3. 03Sell through a franchise model instead of one-off equipment sales, locking in local operators who keep running events
Moving it to an AI business

Not an AI business itself, but the pattern of splitting hardware into portable modules plus a cloud backend for remote customization transfers to AI hardware or on-site AI services: shrink the heavy asset to lower the operator's barrier, and centralize customization in the cloud.
