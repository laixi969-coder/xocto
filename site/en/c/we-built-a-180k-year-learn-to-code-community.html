01

Real-demand verdict

Real-demand verdict

Real demand

It replaces the inefficient habit of hunting for practice projects or faking portfolios from Dribbble shots; learners want real projects they can show employers. The paywall is clear: career-changers and upskillers pay to skip the project-hunting friction.

How it makes money

Learners pay a recurring PRO subscription for premium challenges, design source files for free challenges, and extra platform features.

What old behavior it replaces

Self-sourcing practice projects and assembling portfolios from Dribbble mockups

02

Where the first customers came from

The founder's own General Assembly students, plus his LinkedIn network and the GA Slack channel

03

Tactics you can copy

  1. 01Ship a free resource list first to validate demand, then layer a paywall on top instead of charging from day one
  2. 02Run an early Slack community and treat daily conversations as your product roadmap
  3. 03Delay the Product Hunt launch until retention and community are solid, then push once
Moving it to an AI business

For AI tool businesses: lead with a free, shareable artifact (here challenges; in AI, templates, prompt libraries, or example workflows), then sell a time-saving premium tier as a subscription, using community chatter as the roadmap.
