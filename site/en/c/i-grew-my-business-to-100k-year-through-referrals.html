I Grew My Business To $100K/Year Through Referrals

Real demand

A Boston-area full-service digital marketing agency serving small businesses with SEO, web development and social media, grown mainly through referrals and its own SEO, self-reporting $167K in monthly revenue.

Real-demand verdict

Real-demand verdict

Real demand

Small businesses have recurring demand for websites and lead generation; the agency substitutes for owners doing marketing themselves or hiring full-time staff. The founder claims retention beyond the industry's six-month average, suggesting repeat payment.

How it makes money

SMB clients pay monthly retainers or project fees for SEO, web development, social media and email marketing; later expanded into sales consulting and a restaurant POS product.

What old behavior it replaces

Owners doing their own web and marketing work, or being unable to afford full-time marketing staff.

02

Where the first customers came from

Through the founder's network; the first sales came from his friend Nathan Salem's cleaning company, in exchange for maintaining that company's site, blog and SEO.

03

Tactics you can copy

  1. 01Start with one free or barter client inside your network, turn it into a showcase case, then expand outward.
  2. 02Run your own SEO and social accounts as a showroom, using rankings and engagement as proof of capability.
  3. 03Buy Bing Ads instead of Google; the founder claims roughly 30% lower cost per click.
Moving it to an AI business

For AI founders: the cold-start path of serving one friendly client into a case study, then using your own product as a growth showcase, transfers directly to vertical AI tools aimed at SMBs.
