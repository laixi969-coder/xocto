How We Designed A $200K/Month Idea Management Software

Real demand

London-based Idea Drop sells enterprises and public-sector bodies a SaaS for collecting and ranking employee ideas, landing deals through a customer-success team running a 12-step rollout; the founder self-reports $200K in monthly revenue.

01

Real-demand verdict

Real-demand verdict

Real demand

It replaces email, spreadsheets and offline meetings for gathering employee suggestions; large organizations need a trackable, ranked pipeline that turns ideas into projects. Covea Insurance reportedly saved £2.5M over five years from one implemented idea, giving buyers a quantifiable cost-saving case.

How it makes money

Enterprises pay a SaaS subscription, bought by HR, innovation or operations leads; revenue comes from sales plus a customer-success team driving org-wide adoption and renewals.

What old behavior it replaces

Email, spreadsheets and offline meetings used to collect employee suggestions

02

Where the first customers came from

The founder first ran a prototype inside his own consultancy, Yellowball, gathering 50+ ideas from about 20 staff in two weeks; he then landed Lear Corporation and Kent Police as first external clients through network outreach and pitching.

03

Tactics you can copy

  1. 01Prototype inside your own company first and use real usage data to validate demand before selling.
  2. 02Bundle a 12-step implementation plan into delivery and let customer success drive adoption inside the client org.
  3. 03Host industry events around innovation topics to acquire customers, without pitching at them.
  4. 04Embed the product in Slack and MS Teams that employees already keep open, lowering the barrier to participate.
Moving it to an AI business

For AI app teams: enterprise AI tools also stall on adoption rather than features, so the transferable parts are bundling implementation and customer success into the subscription and validating demand through internal dogfooding. Its ranking is rule-based scoring, not an AI product, so the value is mostly on sales and delivery.
