Real-demand verdict

Real-demand verdict

Real demand

Offers a nostalgic, affordable quilt that replaces expensive traditional bedding, satisfying emotional demand.

How it makes money

Customers order custom quilts via Shopify at about $750 each, paid per order.

What old behavior it replaces

Expensive traditional quilts and hand‑made patchwork.

02

Where the first customers came from

First customers came via a Grommet video and Groupon Grassroots promotion.

Acquisition channels Groupon GrassrootsGrommet videoShopify + emailFacebook/Google ads

03

Tactics you can copy

  1. 01Use Groupon Grassroots to get free exposure and large orders.
  2. 02Pay $2,500 for a Grommet video to reach its audience.
  3. 03Build a Shopify store and use Klaviyo for email marketing to drive repeat sales.
Moving it to an AI business

Although not an AI business, its low‑cost production, online channels, and viral marketing can be adapted to AI product go‑to‑market and pricing.
