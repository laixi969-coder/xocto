How I Make $85K/Month Selling Tools To Music Producers

Real demand

Modern Producers is a Shopify-based storefront that resells third-party drum kits, loops, presets and VSTs to music producers, earning commission on each sale plus a subscription membership; it reported $85K/month and 60,000 customers in 2019.

01

Real-demand verdict

Real-demand verdict

Real demand

It replaces the scattered, one-off way producers used to hunt down and buy sound packs from individual creators, consolidating selection and payment into one store. The founder's 15 years of industry relationships brought both suppliers and repeat buyers, which points to a real, recurring need rather than a one-time novelty.

How it makes money

Third-party producers and labels supply the products; the platform takes a commission and pays partners monthly, with a second revenue line from the subscription membership The Platinum Circle.

What old behavior it replaces

Producers buying sound packs one by one from individual creators and handling each download and payment separately.

02

Where the first customers came from

The first sales came within a week of launch by reaching out to music producers one-on-one on social media.

03

Tactics you can copy

  1. 01Run contests as content marketing: release a pack of exclusive sounds, ask users to make a beat using only those sounds, and award cash and gear as prizes to pull traffic and buzz back to the store.
  2. 02Treat Facebook Messenger as the primary push channel: the founder reports email open rates of 10-15% and click rates of 2-5%, versus 80-90% opens and 10-20% clicks on Messenger broadcasts to the same list.
  3. 03Don't build the catalog yourself: outsource most sounds and tools to third-party producers and labels and keep the team focused on curation, sales and payouts.
Moving it to an AI business

The core play is other people's products plus your own distribution and checkout. Ported to AI, that means running a marketplace for AI tools, models or prompt packs, monetizing via commission plus membership, and cold-starting with community contests and high-open-rate messaging channels. Note the products here are not AI applications; what transfers is the channel and supply-organization model, not the product itself.
