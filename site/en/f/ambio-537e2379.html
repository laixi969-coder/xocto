Ambio

Ambio is a Helsinki-based AI-native end-to-end procurement platform for recycled plastics, supplying verified recycled polymer materials with structured quality data and supply chain services to European plastic converters.

Work it replaces

Replaces traditional recycled plastics trading workflows that rely on static datasheets, manual quoting, and fragmented suppliers, as well as converters' internal efforts to screen and validate recycled material quality.

Business judgment

Ambio's core commercial bet is that the bottleneck for recycled plastics adoption is not the material itself but quality predictability and procurement trust, so it replaces static datasheets and fragmented trader networks with AI-driven batch testing data and a matching/quoting system.

01

What the product does

Ambio offers an AI-native end-to-end procurement solution for recycled plastics, covering grades such as rLDPE, rHDPE, rPP, rPVC, rPA, rABS, rPS, and rPC. Core capabilities include translating converter requirements into material properties, matching qualified recycled grades, testing each delivery against production requirements, and providing structured batch history quality data. The company also operates a recycler partner network, connecting recyclers with recurring demand from European plastic converters. Its website includes detailed technical documentation for rPA, covering physical, mechanical, and chemical properties, applications, and FAQs.

02

Users, buyers and demand

Users are European plastic converters who need to procure recycled polymers for injection molding, blow molding, and extrusion; payers are the same converters, paying for the recycled materials they purchase. Recyclers are supply-side partners who gain customers, contracts, and freight services through Ambio. The core pain point is high batch-to-batch quality variability in recycled plastics, lack of reliable quality data, and fragmented procurement with high trust costs, making it difficult for converters to integrate recycled materials into stable production.

03

The actual workflow

The process has four steps: first, converters submit production requirements, and Ambio translates them into material properties and screens qualified materials for the best fit. Second, Ambio presents best-matching grades with price and quality data, allowing customers to choose one or test several. Third, Ambio sets a test plan for each delivery and arranges freight, ensuring material reaches the plant on schedule and as agreed. Fourth, customers can buy on a spot basis or move to contract purchasing to build long-term supply relationships. Ambio acts as the single accountable party from requirement input to delivery.

04

Pricing and unit economics

Ambio generates revenue by selling recycled materials to plastic converters, who can purchase on a spot basis or sign long-term contracts. Specific pricing, markup, or commission structures are not disclosed on the website. Unit economics inference: revenue may come from material sales margin or service fees, but the exact model is undisclosed and should be labeled as inference. Recyclers may participate through demand matching and logistics services, but this is also undisclosed.

05

Adoption evidence and gaps

The company discloses sales coverage in DACH, Scandinavia, Finland & Baltics, and France, with named sales contacts per market. The website lists grades converters most often order and shows an example rLDPE batch history. On funding, the company raised €5.1 million in a seed round in October 2026, with investors including Lifeline Ventures, b2venture, and Montis Capital. Missing evidence: no customer count, revenue, retention rate, specific delivery volumes, or case studies are disclosed.

06

Competition and defensibility

Compared to general-purpose AI models, Ambio's moat lies in its accumulated batch-level quality data, qualified recycler network, and procurement relationships with converters. General models cannot provide tested recycled material performance data or assume supply chain responsibility. Compared to legacy processes, Ambio replaces static datasheets with dynamic batch data, reducing converters' validation costs. Moat conditions: data network effects require sufficient delivery batches and recycler participation, and it must continuously prove the accuracy of its quality predictions.

07

How to read this round

The seed round indicates investor recognition of applying AI to recycled plastics procurement, with funds likely intended for expanding material categories, increasing sales coverage, and building data infrastructure. However, the use of funds is undisclosed, and the next milestone may be proving customer retention, scaling delivery volumes, or entering new markets. Funding itself does not constitute demand validation or evidence of product success.

08

Where it could fail

First, recycled plastics quality variability may exceed the control of AI matching and test plans, causing production disruptions or returns and damaging trust. Second, recycler network expansion may be limited by the scarcity and geographic dispersion of qualified recyclers, leading to supply instability. Third, converters may continue using virgin plastics due to price or inertia, slowing recycled material adoption. Fourth, if batch data cannot consistently reduce customer validation costs, customers may revert to traditional traders.

09

What you can take from it

Ambio's approach shows that in materials supply chains, AI's value is not replacing the material itself but structuring non-standard quality information to reduce trust costs for both parties. Entrepreneurs can learn to embed data services into physical transactions and become a single accountable party, rather than just an information matchmaker.

10

What to watch next

Verifiable next indicators include: customer retention and contract purchasing share, delivery batch volume and growth rate, number of recycler partners, and whether specific customer cases or revenue data are disclosed. The key question is whether Ambio's batch data can consistently and accurately predict material performance on customer production lines.
