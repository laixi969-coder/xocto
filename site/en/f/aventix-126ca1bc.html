FUNDING DESK · Sweden · Life science laboratory instrumentation and bioanalytics data quality tools.

Aventix

Aventix is a Swedish life science instrumentation company whose first product, Scarlett 01, is a benchtop optical volume measurement system for microplates, designed to provide high-precision, traceable data for AI and bioanalytics.

01

What the product does

Aventix's product is the Scarlett 01 Volume Analysis System, built on its patent-pending Aventix Chromatic Core Technology (ACCT), which uses polychromatic light dispersion and spectral analysis for non-contact liquid surface height measurement, then applies AI models to convert height to volume. It supports 96-, 384-, and 1536-well plates plus deep-well plates, with volume ranges from 3 µL to 300+ µL and scan times from 30 to 360 seconds per plate. The companion SensVue browser-based software provides heatmap visualization, anomaly flagging, CSV/JSON/Excel export, and REST API plus WebSocket interfaces. The company claims independent validation by VTT MIKES, Finland's National Metrology Institute, showing all measured values within ±5% of the calibrated volume for 10 µL DMSO in a 384-well plate.

02

Users, buyers and demand

Target users are scientists and lab managers in pharmaceutical, chemical, agricultural, and cosmetic R&D labs performing high-throughput screening, compound preparation, NGS library prep, or cell-based assays. Payers are likely lab heads or procurement, and the core pain is pipetting errors causing failed experiments, reagent waste, and irreproducible data, which visual checks cannot catch early. The website does not disclose specific customer names, payment scale, or purchase frequency, so pain frequency and budget ownership cannot be assessed.

03

The actual workflow

A user places a microplate into Scarlett 01, which scans the entire plate non-contact, capturing liquid surface height point cloud data via ACCT. SensVue's AI model analyzes meniscus geometry, plate type, and surface tension in real time, converting height to volume and generating color-coded heatmaps with anomaly flags. Users view results in a browser, export CSV, JSON, or Excel files, or integrate data into lab automation via REST API. The process requires no consumables or calibration liquids and completes in tens of seconds to minutes per plate.

05

Adoption evidence and gaps

The company reports that Scarlett 01 has launched an Early Access Program and established a non-exclusive distribution partnership with Wilten Instrumenten for Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg. The website also presents a VTT MIKES independent validation summary, but does not disclose installed customer count, revenue, repeat purchase rates, or active usage data. The funding announcement states the round will scale the Early Access Program rollout but provides no specific adoption metrics.

06

Competition and defensibility

Aventix's potential moat lies in its ACCT optical measurement technology, AI volume conversion models, and third-party validation by VTT MIKES, which differentiate it from generic AI models or pure software solutions. However, the hardware instrumentation space has established competitors, and patents are still pending. Whether the moat is sustainable depends on maintaining measurement accuracy across more liquids and plate types, rapidly expanding distribution, and whether customers will pay a premium for data quality.

07

How to read this round

This is a €1.2 million funding round with investors including Kost Capital, Voima Ventures, and Bodil Sidén, classified as Other stage with no specific stage disclosed. Funds will scale the Early Access Program for Scarlett 01, but detailed allocation and next milestone metrics are not public. The round indicates an early commercialization stage but does not prove market validation or revenue growth.

08

Where it could fail

First, if target labs are unwilling to pay separately for volume measurement hardware or consider existing pipette calibration sufficient, Scarlett 01 may face insufficient demand. Second, if ACCT accuracy degrades with other liquids (e.g., high-viscosity, volatile, or surfactant-containing samples) or extreme plate types, the scope of independent validation narrows, affecting customer trust. Third, if distribution partnerships fail to convert into actual orders, the Early Access Program may remain a pilot.

09

What you can take from it

Aventix's case suggests that in the AI bioanalytics boom, solving upstream physical data acquisition precision may be more leveraged than training larger models. Entrepreneurs can learn from its use of third-party metrology validation to build trust, non-contact hardware to lower adoption barriers, and software export plus APIs to embed into existing workflows.

10

What to watch next

Next, watch whether Scarlett 01 discloses specific customer cases, installed base, or revenue data; whether pricing and software licensing models are revealed; and whether ACCT technology gains independent validation across more liquid types and plate formats.
