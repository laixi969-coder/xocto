龙版传媒

After six straight limit-up sessions driven by AI-video hype, Chinese listed publisher Longban Media clarified that its AI videos drew about 120 million views but earned only 80 yuan in a month. It is a rare hard data point on monetization: view counts do not convert into revenue, and platform revenue sharing alone cannot sustain an AI-content business; teams making AI videos need to anchor revenue in e-commerce, commissioned advertising or brand services rather than views themselves.

AI shopping is becoming a structural shift in e-commerce: adoption heated up during the 2026 back-to-school season, with Amazon and Google leveraging their existing ecosystems to take a first-mover advantage. Industry discussion also notes that actual consumer adoption of AI shopping is slower than most brands expect, so brands need to recalibrate the pace and expected returns of their AI channel investments.

Astra

Abusive AI crawler traffic

Abusive web crawlers, largely AI-driven scraping traffic, have become a structural burden on large-scale web infrastructure: the operators of the official Linux kernel Git repository report that CPU cycles spent rendering commits for scrapers now exceed all legitimate access including git clones, requiring 14 CPU cores across five geo-distributed nodes. The shift implies significantly higher operating costs for content and code hosting services and may accelerate anti-crawler measures, access restrictions, and paywall policies across the industry.

AI jobs boom

Fears that AI would trigger mass unemployment are being undercut by the latest labor observations: the initial effects of AI technology on employment look positive, the predicted jobs apocalypse is postponed, and an AI jobs boom is under way. This structural shift in the labor market may ease market concerns about AI-driven labor displacement and influence corporate hiring strategies and the pace of AI investment.

vLLM

vLLM is an open-source LLM inference engine; this update enables speculative decoding on AMD GPUs to speed up inference. It's developer infrastructure, not an end-user product.

EU Smartphone Repairability Requirements

The EU's repairability requirements for smartphones are now in force, but new reporting shows manufacturers broadly fail to meet them. The gap between regulation and industry practice could trigger enforcement actions and force changes in spare parts availability, disassembly design and repair support.

Alberta solar panel punitive tax

Alberta, Canada imposes a punitive tax on solar panels, further suppressing renewable energy development, potentially raising local photovoltaic costs and affecting market share.
