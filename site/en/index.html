How I Solved My Own Problem And Turned It Into A Side Business -

How We Started The First Ever Catholic Meditation App

Leveraging My Experience As A Doctor To Create A Digital Health App -

Founder interview, self-reported

Patients returning to the hospital to fill out paper surveys after surgery, and doctors reviewing paper medication logs after the fact.
