How I Started A $1.8M/Year Vintage Watch Company

Founder interview, self-reported

Buying vintage watches on eBay or from local dealers and judging authenticity and condition yourself.

What it is

A 23-year-old founder buys vintage watches from dealers and collectors worldwide and retails them through his own site, using YouTube content to build trust at scale; the shop runs about $150K in monthly revenue.

Real-demand verdict

Real demand · It replaces the old path of hunting vintage watches on eBay or through local dealers where authenticity and condition are hard to judge; the founder says the business was profitable from day one and significantly profitable for the past two years, meaning buyers keep paying a premium for a trusted seller and curated inventory.

How I Disrupted The Ad Industry And Grew My Business To $20M/Year -

Founder interview, self-reported

Publishers monetizing with AdSense alone, where a single buyer sets the price

What it is

MonetizeMore runs ad auctions for publishers with millions of monthly visitors, adding more bidders to inventory that previously had only AdSense, and takes a cut of the incremental ad revenue. The founder reports over $20M in annual revenue.

Real-demand verdict

Real-revenue case

How This Firefighter Invented A $3.6M Product That Prevents Kitchen

Founder interview, self-reported

Owners' reactive response to unattended-cooking fires: post-incident claims, insurance costs, and manual inspections.

What it is

A Utah firefighter built a plug-in device that cuts power to the stove when it hears the smoke alarm, and sells it mainly to multi-family and senior-living property owners; the company did over $2M in 2018.

Real-demand verdict

Real demand · It replaces property owners' reactive handling of unattended-cooking fires (claims, insurance, manual checks) with a device that cuts power automatically. The loss is real and recurring, which gives owners a reason to keep buying. The material gives no repeat-purchase or renewal data, so long-term revenue depends on new properties rather than existing customers.
