Founder interview, self-reported

Expensive traditional indoor weddings

What it is

In Tampa and Destin, Florida, two partners started as a weekend officiant-and-photographer duo and turned beach weddings into standardized packages, using their own Beach WeddingStudio configurator plus owned vans and venues to reach roughly 1,000 weddings a year and $165K in monthly revenue.

Real-demand verdict

Real demand · It replaced expensive traditional indoor weddings; the recession pushed couples toward a cheaper beach option, so demand was real and recurring. The founder reports $165K/month, but the business shut down in 2023, so it made money without proving durability.
