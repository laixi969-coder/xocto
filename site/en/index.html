First discovery

2026-09-21 · Open-source project · Finance and Investment / Personal Finance

What it is

An individual trader imports broker CSV trade records on their own machine; the tool turns scattered fills into round-trip trades and key metrics, then Claude produces a diary-style review analysis. A bitmap-to-vector tool for makers and designers that converts PNG, JPG and WEBP into editable SVG and exports DXF, EPS, AI, PDF, PNG, G-code plus embroidery formats like DST, PES and JEF, earning about $3,278 a month.
