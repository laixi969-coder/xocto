dsh-tui

Claude Code-style full-screen interactive terminal plugin, 571 stars, filling the official TUI gap. A complement for CLI enthusiasts, showing toolchain experience is becoming more layered.

Upgrades one-off multi-agent orchestration to a generative, saveable, governable workflow layer. From one-off to governable is a key step in agent engineering.
