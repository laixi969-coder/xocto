verdict_reason_en: Bachelorette parties are a recurring, high-ticket event that someone always has to
  organize; the old workflow was group chats plus spreadsheets, and BACH folds invites, polls, itineraries,
  expense splitting and booking into one app. Vendors lacked online demand and scheduling tools, so they
  give up commission for orders.
