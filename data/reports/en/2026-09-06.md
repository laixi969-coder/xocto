# AI product radar · 2026-09-06

## Daily Opportunity Flow: 2026-09-06

Today's signals cluster around AI agents shifting from 'chat' to 'delivery': shopping agents, formal math proofs, office workflow takeover, and search-driven transactions. Major vendors' open-source releases and product updates indicate infrastructure is ready; real opportunity lies in vertical deployment and delivery.

### Positive Direction

AI agents are entering a phase where they can 'deliver verifiable results' in shopping, math proofs, office, and travel. For founders, the window is: skip general assistants, cut into vertical workflows that big vendors miss, and build trust through verifiable outcomes.

### Key Projects

### commerce-agents

Anthropic's open-source reference blueprint for developers building shopping or merchant agents with Claude. Provides example code and architecture patterns for retail, e-commerce, telecom, and entertainment, covering product browsing and order lookup. This is the first time a major model vendor offers standardized agent templates for commercial scenarios, moving shopping agents from concept to replicable. For developers, it's an accelerator; for startups, a warning: the general layer is occupied, opportunity lies in vertical data and delivery.

### fermats-last-theorem

Anthropic's open-source repo with undisclosed functionality, possibly related to math proof or AI reasoning. Lacks clear product description, but combined with OpenAI's math proof repos today, the signal is consistent: AI is entering formal verification. Recommend watching Anthropic's docs for workflow and user value.

### LongGapsBetweenPrimes

OpenAI's open-source Lean formalization of a bound on long gaps between primes. Mathematicians can check theorems line-by-line or reuse proof modules. This is an early sample of AI-generated verifiable math proofs; the Lean ecosystem is shifting from labor-intensive to AI-assisted. For the math community, it's a tool; for founders, a hint: formal verification services may become a market, but payers need clarity.

### PrimeGaps186

Another OpenAI repo with a conditional Lean formalization and numerical certificate for prime gaps at most 186. Similar to LongGapsBetweenPrimes, it demonstrates reusable AI outputs in number theory. Market education value exceeds direct commercial value, but it validates AI's capability in rigorous reasoning.

### Qwen Office

Alibaba's Qwen-family AI office product. Office workers hand tasks like drafting documents and organizing data to AI, getting editable outputs. Reports don't disclose specifics, but direction is clear: model vendors are upgrading general chat to office workflow takeover. For startups, head-on competition is unrealistic; opportunity lies in vertical industries (legal, tax, real estate) with customized workflows.

### Google AI Mode

Google Search's AI Mode adds travel planning: given dates and needs, it tracks flight prices and completes hotel searches. Search moves from 'giving links' to 'direct transactions'; AI intermediaries are taking over travel's transaction entry. For startups, avoid general assistants; target long-tail services or offline fulfillment that Google doesn't cover.

### Market Context

E-commerce sales reached $340.2B in Q2, and AI shopping assistants are moving from recommendations to direct ordering, making shopping agentification an industry direction. Logistics software giant Descartes is integrating AI into existing product lines, showing accelerated AI penetration in mature industries. Insurance software vendor Guidewire shows strong product momentum but cautious outlook, with market disagreement on software spending. Together, these signals indicate: AI agent deployment scenarios are expanding, but willingness to pay remains unverified.

### Conclusion

Today's opportunity is not in general agents but in vertical 'result delivery' capabilities. Shopping, math proofs, office, and travel all show verifiable outputs. Founders should focus on industry workflows that big vendors miss and build trust through outcomes.
