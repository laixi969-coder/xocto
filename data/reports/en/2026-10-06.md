# AI product radar · 2026-10-06

## Today's opportunity surface: AI moves from talking to doing, and the friction points surface at the same time

The clearest positive direction today: AI is moving out of the chat box and into concrete workflows — driving a browser on a schedule, operating editing software, running fine-tunes in a terminal. At the same time the system layer is pushing back: Apple tightened macOS Full Disk Access specifically around AI agents reading data. Opportunity and constraint appearing together is itself the signal.

### ai-employees
It assigns 8 scheduled business roles and 60 routines to AI, which drives the browser the way a person does, while the user keeps the workflow files they own and the improved results of each run. It replaces the old behaviour of a small team clicking through back-office screens by hand. Judgement: the direction is right, but whether the workflow files can really be taken elsewhere and who is accountable when a run fails is not answered in the public material.

### BashCut
It lets coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex drive the macOS editing interface through a command line or MCP interface, so the agent does the dragging, captioning and voiceover that used to be manual. It replaces an editor's hands on the timeline. Judgement: handing the operation surface to an agent rather than adding a generate button is a trade-off closer to real workflows than most 'AI editing' products.

### RemoveMacAI
When a Mac is short on disk space and the user does not want Apple Intelligence's local models sitting on the machine, this open-source command line tool scans for and deletes the model data it occupies. It replaces manually hunting through directories for model files. Judgement: the need is real but narrow — a control-rights friction created by system-level AI shipping on by default, and Apple's permission change today shows the platform is starting to take that friction seriously.

### brewery-ai
In a local terminal, the user hands an existing dataset and base model to this console agent, which runs the fine-tuning process and outputs model weights. It replaces writing scripts and configuring environments. Judgement: lowering the barrier for non-specialist teams is the trend, but which models, data formats and delivery methods are supported is undisclosed, so whether it actually works cannot be judged yet.

### ai-security-book
A bilingual open-source book for AI security engineers and compliance reviewers, organising attack methods, defences and engineering validation approaches for generative models and embodied AI systems. It replaces scattered blog notes. Judgement: whether the content can be applied directly to engineering practice is not stated in the public material.

### Market context
Cohere signed a merger agreement with Aleph Alpha, was reported to be negotiating up to $3 billion in funding, and formed a global AI alliance with PwC starting in Canada — competition among enterprise model vendors is shifting from model capability to capital and channels. Intel and AMD are seeking long-term server CPU purchase lock-ins from Chinese customers, as the compute shortage spreads from GPUs to CPUs. Q3 global venture funding reached $159 billion across close to 6,000 funded startups, with record billion-dollar rounds — the highest quarterly level since Q2 2022.

### To watch
Buntar Aerospace raised $16 million to scale defence autonomy; Safeworld is building digital humans to verify whether generative-AI robots behave safely before they interact with real people; ChatGPT is folding try-on previews and a Favorites library into the conversation. All three offer only a positioning statement or a funding fact — inputs, process, deliverables and customers remain unverified.
