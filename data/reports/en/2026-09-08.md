# AI product radar · 2026-09-08

## A Day of Monetization Clarity: Platforms Sell Ads, Enterprises Build Capacity, Tools Go Vertical

**Direction: AI is moving from generic demos to scenario-based charging. Tools that embed into specific professional workflows with clear payment triggers are the most certain entry point right now.**

Four market contexts today paint one picture. At the access layer, OpenAI announced ChatGPT Ads, showing ads in the free tier while preserving free access for users who cannot pay—conversation attention is now ad inventory. At the capacity layer, AIStation released V6, positioned to help enterprises build their own 'Token factories', treating inference token capacity as owned infrastructure; enterprise AI is shifting from per-call external models to owned capacity. At the compute layer, AI computing concept stocks surpassed 1 trillion yuan in total revenue in H1 2026, with R&D investment around 2.6%; profitability diverges as demand expands and competition intensifies. At the tooling layer, the AI SDK released a patch with GPT-6 reasoning configuration updates. The monetization frameworks for access, capacity, and compute are all clear; the remaining gap is at the application layer—who can collect money within specific workflows.

Two temperature gaps must be acknowledged: first, AI shopping heated up during the 2026 back-to-school season, with Amazon and Google leveraging existing ecosystems for first-mover advantage, but industry discussion notes consumer adoption is slower than brand expectations; second, a stock with six consecutive limit-ups proactively warned that its AI video business revenue is under 0.01%. The gap between hype and revenue remains huge. On the demand side, there is a real gap: discussions on agents expanding to Southeast Asia note a shortage of people who understand local implementation—tools are not scarce, implementation capability is.

### Doubao Aixue: Big Tech Brings AI Tutoring to K-12

ByteDance launched Doubao Aixue, an AI tutoring app for primary and secondary students and parents: it receives problem images or text, performs step-by-step analysis and explanation, outputs solution steps and answers, and may include practice variations (details unverified). Context: AI education is shifting from question banks to personalized tutoring and explanation, raising demands on accuracy and explanation quality. Big tech entry raises the bar for generic problem-solving; independent teams' reasonable position is in vertical scenarios like mistake notebooks and learning analytics (editor's judgment).

### AI-Engineering-Lab: AI Engineering Skills Are Systematizing

An open-source hands-on course for developers transitioning to AI: 43 runnable notebooks over 24 weeks, covering ML basics, RAG, fine-tuning, agent MCP protocols, and multi-cloud deployment, threaded through a single continuous case. Its signal value exceeds its course value: learning paths are shifting from fragmented prompt tricks to system-level full-stack engineering including MCP, RAG governance, and cloud orchestration, matching enterprises' real need for implementable talent. Around the same time, OpenAI internally published early data on coding agents reshaping AI research workflows—engineering capability is becoming hard currency.

### BoardUI: Component Standards Emerge for Agentic Interfaces

BoardUI is a React design system for agentic interfaces: it offers free open-source components and a working AI chat app example that can connect to your own model key, letting developers reuse components instead of building from scratch. It signals that AI application interfaces are forming new design paradigms, and component libraries are infrastructure. Possible entries: industry-specific component packs for customer service or data analysis, or paid themes and support (editor's judgment).
