### Top 3 Worth Watching

1. **deepseek-harness**: DeepSeek's official plugin framework, 73k stars on GitHub. The 'everything is a plugin' philosophy is spawning a huge ecosystem, worth watching for standardization.
2. **dsh-tui**: Claude Code-style full-screen interactive terminal plugin, 571 stars, filling the official TUI gap. A complement for CLI enthusiasts, showing toolchain experience is becoming more layered.
3. **dsh-workflow**: Upgrades one-off multi-agent orchestration to a generative, saveable, governable workflow layer. From one-off to governable is a key step in agent engineering.
