# AI product radar · 2026-10-01

## Today's Opportunity Flow

The clearest positive direction today: the software gaps on domestic accelerators are being filled by model vendors themselves rather than waiting for hardware vendors' general-purpose libraries. DeepEP-Ascend and DeepGEMM-Ascend entered view the same day — one covering the communication layer, one the kernel layer — showing that the engineering gap between "it runs" and "it runs fast" on Ascend now has people willing to invest. In parallel, office and compliance lines are moving: office AI is shifting from personal Q&A toward shared workspaces and enterprise context, and strongly regulated offline credential steps such as seals and insurance paperwork are starting to be AI-enabled.

### DeepEP-Ascend

Engineers running distributed training or inference on Ascend NPU clusters previously had to handle inter-card communication and collective tuning themselves; this library takes communication requests from training/inference frameworks, performs high-performance communication on Ascend hardware, and delivers communication directly callable by the training or inference pipeline.

Judgment: this moves the communication layer, previously handled by hardware vendors' general libraries or in-house scripts, into model vendors' own implementations. The supported primitives, topologies and performance numbers are undisclosed; whether it has been validated by production workloads needs the repo and real measurements.

### DeepGEMM-Ascend

Engineers running model training or inference on Ascend NPUs previously had to write or tune matrix multiplication kernels to extract usable compute; this library takes matrix multiplication needs, executes efficient kernels on Ascend hardware, and delivers kernel implementations callable by training or inference frameworks.

Judgment: this is the other half of the same logic as DeepEP-Ascend — communication plus kernels together form a usable training stack. Supported matrix shapes, precisions and measured throughput are undisclosed and need verification.

### Dongma Seal

Corporate administration or compliance staff open it during seal approval and archiving, handling seal-use requests, approval records and stamp traces for physical seals. Public material only says it uses AI for seal management; what the AI receives, what action it performs and what credential it delivers are all undisclosed.

Judgment: the trend is that strongly regulated offline credential steps such as seals and contracts are starting to be AI-enabled, but public material stays at the award level and does not show which manual step it replaces.

### Qwen Office

In multi-person office scenarios, enterprise teams open it and hand over material previously scattered across documents, chat logs and internal systems, letting AI organize it and produce reusable office output in a shared workspace; per candidate material it also introduces "enterprise context" so the AI can read company information, though the integration method and delivery form are undisclosed.

Judgment: the competitive point in office AI is shifting from model capability to whether it can access internal enterprise information; a shared multi-person workspace is harder to replace than personal Q&A.

### Market Context

- A competition watchdog took enforcement action against a fake-review provider and 45 businesses that posted fabricated online reviews, moving review governance into actual penalties. For AI apps and e-commerce services that rely on user reviews for acquisition, review-data credibility and compliance costs rise.
- Mistral AI, after September 2026 funding led by Samsung, is valued above EUR 21 billion, acquired ad-tech company Pimento in cash and stock, and is exploring privacy-focused multilingual AI browsing with Mozilla.
- Google gave its most powerful AI model first to cyber defenders rather than releasing it broadly, suggesting frontier models may follow staged, industry-prioritized access in B2B scenarios.
- OpenAI introduced Dots, positioned as proactive assistants that keep working across complex projects and daily tasks; public material gives no specific task flow, deliverable or pricing.
