# AI product radar · 2026-08-30

## AI Product Radar · 2026-08-30

### Today's Opportunity Summary

Today's market signals three clear trends: AI engineering education is moving from scattered tutorials to systematic curricula; local inference and privacy protection demand is rising; AI agents are evolving from CLI to dedicated workbenches.

**Positive direction**: AI applications are deepening from general conversation to vertical scenarios and executable actions, with education, local inference, and privacy protection being the most promising entry points.

### AI-Engineering-Lab
A free 24-week AI engineering course for students and career switchers, covering Python, ML, LLMs, RAG, fine-tuning, agents, and MCP, with 43 runnable notebooks and a continuous case study, MIT licensed, no signup. Entry point: systematic curriculum, later monetize via certification or enterprise training.

### codex-manager
Developers can manage multiple OpenAI Codex sessions, models, tokens, accounts, providers, and AGENTS.md files from a local desktop control center. It centralizes the Codex runtime environment, delivering a local-first dashboard. Entry point: local control center for the Codex ecosystem, but need to validate willingness to pay for management tools.

### cover
Developers give real data to cover, which acts as a reversible privacy proxy, sending realistic fake data to external AI agents and restoring originals locally. It protects sensitive information in AI agent interactions, delivering a privacy layer. Entry point: data masking proxy layer, but need to validate adoption and complex data mapping.

### Ollama
A tool for running open-source models locally, receiving model names and run commands, starting a local inference service, and integrating with agents like Claude Code and Codex. Entry point: target enterprise intranet, offline environments, or data-sensitive industries with pre-configured model packs and ops support.

### Lovart
An AI design agent platform for creative teams. Designers upload design briefs, assets, or references; AI performs design generation, layout, or iteration, delivering editable drafts or proposals that require human confirmation. Entry point: focus on specific areas like brand design, e-commerce detail pages, or ad creatives, charging per deliverable.

### resume-evidence-workflow
Job seekers input their experiences; the workflow uses AI to dig into details, match JD, rewrite resumes, and generate interview-ready narratives. Entry point: vertical industries like tech or finance, charge per revision or coaching session.

### Rillet
An AI tool for accounting teams that automates financial reporting processes such as board updates. Users upload financial data, AI generates draft reports, and accountants review before submission. Entry point: accounting and financial analysis scenarios, offering per-report pricing, but need to validate adoption and willingness to pay.
