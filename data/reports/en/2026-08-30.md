# AI product radar · 2026-08-30

## Today's Opportunity Summary

Today's market signals three clear trends: AI engineering education is moving from scattered tutorials to systematic curricula; desktop agents are evolving from conversation to software operation; local inference and open-source model ecosystems are accelerating.

**Positive direction**: AI applications are deepening from general conversation to vertical scenarios and executable actions, with education, office automation, and local inference being the most promising entry points.

### AI-Engineering-Lab
A free 24-week AI engineering course for students and career switchers, covering Python, ML, LLMs, RAG, fine-tuning, agents, and MCP, with 43 runnable notebooks and a continuous case study, MIT licensed, no signup. Entry point: systematic curriculum, later monetize via certification or enterprise training.

### Baidu Dazi DuMate
A desktop AI agent that can see the screen, operate software, process files, and connect business systems to execute complex tasks across applications. Entry point: target repetitive vertical processes like finance or HR, charge per outcome, but watch competition from existing ecosystems like DingTalk and Feishu.

### Blaze.tech
A low-code platform for healthcare, enabling visual configuration of data models and workflows, with AI components embedded in forms, approvals, and reporting. Entry point: patient data management and clinical workflow automation with templates and compliance support, charging per app or per seat.

### headcount
An agent organization for Claude Code, structured like a company with 15+ departments and 125+ skills. Entry point: offer pre-configured skill packs for specific development stages like code review or test generation, charge per department or skill subscription.

### Ollama
A tool for running open-source models locally, receiving model names and run commands, starting a local inference service, and integrating with agents like Claude Code and Codex. Entry point: target enterprise intranet, offline environments, or data-sensitive industries with pre-configured model packs and ops support, charging per node or service.

### Keenable
A web search API designed for AI agents, returning structured, deduplicated results, having raised $26M. Entry point: search infrastructure, charge per API call.
