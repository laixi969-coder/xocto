# AI product radar · 2026-08-24

## AI product radar · 2026-08-24

Today's opportunities cluster around AI operations, formal proofs, 4D content generation, and developer tooling. Most projects are early-stage, requiring validation of effectiveness and monetization paths.

### oncall-kit
Anthropic's open-source starter kit for Claude-assisted on-call: mines incident history into triage playbooks, sets up through human-approved gates, and runs read-only in Slack, with humans deploying every fix. Opportunity lies in safe AI ops, but efficiency gains in real incidents need verification.

### ten-proofs
OpenAI released Lean certificates for ten proofs in math and theoretical CS, machine-checkable. Advances formal verification adoption, but commercialization path is unclear; suitable for research communities.

### zeta-23-lean
Anthropic's open-source project with limited info: no description, no docs, only 187 stars. Need to clarify the problem it solves before assessing follow-up value.

### 4DAnyone
Ant Research's open-source tool generates rotatable, animatable 4D humans from monocular video. Targets film/game pre-visualization; possible per-generation or licensing fees, but output format needs verification.

### Airsec
Open-source tool using coding CLIs like Claude and Codex to scan for security issues. Security-left trend is clear; could be CI/CD plugin or IDE extension, charged per scan or subscription, but remediation and integration need validation.

### Other Notables
- **clipfactory**: Generates vertical short videos from your own B-roll, fully automated, for creators.
- **benjamin-plus-skill**: JetBrains' skill reduces coding agent token costs by ~17.9%, universality needs testing.
- **ComfyUI-H3-FaceRefine**: Refines small faces in AI video, post-processing refinement.

**Validation Points**: Most projects lack user feedback and performance data; real-world testing or community validation is needed before committing.
