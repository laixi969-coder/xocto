# AI product radar · 2026-09-02

## Daily Opportunity Flow Report

### Market Context: AI Agent Governance and Healthcare SaaS Repricing

Today's market context clearly points to two directions: first, the proliferation of AI agents in enterprises creates governance and security needs, with NVIDIA and CrowdStrike strengthening agentic cybersecurity and AIR raising $50M confirming capital entry; second, healthcare SaaS pricing is shifting from per-seat subscriptions to agent-workload or outcome-based models, a structural change affecting vendor revenue models. Additionally, AI cooling technology is a new blue ocean, and the compute race intensifies with some profiting and others burning cash, presenting both opportunities and risks at the infrastructure layer.

### Key Projects

#### AIR (AI Agent Governance Platform)

AIR targets enterprise security or IT leaders. When multiple AI agents run inside a company, the platform automatically discovers them, vets their skills and plugins, and blocks unwanted behavior. It addresses the 'agent runaway' problem, a governance and compliance need. Entry into highly regulated industries like finance and healthcare is clear, but integration with existing security tools (e.g., CrowdStrike) needs validation.

#### Dongma Seal

Dongma Seal digitizes traditional physical seal management. AI receives seal usage requests and executes approval, recording, and monitoring, delivering traceable records and risk alerts. Seal management is a traditional enterprise need; AI transformation improves compliance and efficiency, but policy compliance and customer trust are concerns. Specific workflows and deliverables require verification.

#### AfterQuery

AI data analytics product, valued at $3.2B, YC's fastest unicorn. Specific features are to be verified, but the sector is hot, and vertical-specific data analytics automation is an opportunity.

### Conclusion

Positive direction today: AI agent governance and security are a clear opportunity window, and healthcare SaaS repricing offers new product design ideas. Focus on agent behavior monitoring, supply chain security, and outcome-based healthcare SaaS tools.
