# AI product radar · 2026-10-10

## Today's opportunity surface: agents start acting on your behalf, and entry points plus accounts become the new battleground

Today's signals point the same way: the value of a personal agent no longer stops at answering questions, it acts to get things done. Instinct lets individuals text or call to hand off chores such as booking a DMV appointment, sending follow-up emails or chasing a merchant's support desk; the agent takes instructions in plain text and can register accounts with its own email, send messages and handle support threads, so the user gets a task advanced or finished. That path pushes the usage threshold to near zero and moves competition from model capability to accounts and the ability to act on your behalf — whether it can register, send and negotiate for you.

Running in parallel is the entry-point race. Meta extended its Muse assistant from iPhone to iPad, where users open it for everyday questions and task requests; the candidate material only supports the iPad release, and what it ingests, does and delivers still needs verification. A big platform spreading a general assistant across devices shows always-on presence matters more than a single feature, so the startup direction is not to clone general Q&A.

There is also a vendor-side move worth watching: publishing small, low-star open-source repositories under their own organizations. amulet2 sits under the anthropics organization, and the only public facts are its name, creation date and a 0-star/1-fork activity level; no README, feature description or usage flow was found. Who it targets, at which work moment it opens, what materials the AI takes in and what it produces are all unverified. Such repos often appear before formal product docs and are a window into where a vendor will put capability next.

## Market context

Anthropic was named to Madrona's 2026 Intelligent Applications 40 in the enabler stage, where Anthropic, OpenAI and Databricks together account for about 92% of a $377B total. In October 2026, reports said AI startups drove Europe's best funding quarter in four years, meaning European AI application companies gain more capital support and product delivery and talent competition may accelerate, though a single quarter is not enough to conclude a long-term upturn. In the same period, reports said AI startups are becoming serial acquirers as M&A deals rose 14% this year, with some companies integrating technology and teams through acquisition, which may change competition in the application layer. Separately, reports said OpenAI's annualized revenue was $20B below previously reported expectations, meaning the market may be overestimating how fast leading model vendors commercialize, which in turn affects the application ecosystem's expectations on model cost and willingness to pay.
