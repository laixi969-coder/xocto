# AI adoption by industry shows what other sectors can learn

The current narrative around artificial intelligence in the workplace is largely driven by Silicon Valley. But treating the tech sector as an island misses the actual trends in technology adoption–tech is just the first industry in line. As generative AI and automation tools mature, the shifts currently reshaping software engineering and product management will inevitably filter into healthcare, education, retail, and manufacturing. The variation in adoption rates today isn’t a sign that non-tech industries are falling behind, but rather a reflection of the different speeds at which industries absorb new workflows. For leaders and their teams outside of tech, this differential pace provides a practical blueprint for how AI adoption unfolds, and where the opportunities lie.
