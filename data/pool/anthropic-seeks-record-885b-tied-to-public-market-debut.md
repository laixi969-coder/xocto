# Anthropic Seeks Record $88.5B Tied To Public Market Debut

Anthropic is seeking to secure up to $88.5 billion in its planned IPO and a concurrent private placement, potentially making it the largest-ever fundraise tied to a public market debut. On Tuesday, the AI giant submitted an updated S-1 filing for its upcoming public offering, unveiling new details including a proposed share price range offering size. The offering could set a fully diluted valuation of up to $1.4 trillion for the San Francisco company. Anthropic’s expected valuation comes in below earlier projections, with many investors reportedly forecasting something closer to $2 trillion. That said, even at $1.4 trillion, Anthropic would still be going public at the second-highest valuation in history, topped only by SpaceX ’s June debut. An IPO and a private placement The offering should also provide Anthropic with plentiful capital to pursue its expensive expansion and infrastructure investment plans. For the IPO portion of its fundraise, Anthropic plans to sell just over 800 million shares 1 at $70 to $78 each.
