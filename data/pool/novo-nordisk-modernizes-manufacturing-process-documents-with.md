# Novo Nordisk modernizes manufacturing process documents with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

Novo Nordisk (Novo) is one of the world’s leading global pharmaceutical companies, manufacturing medicines for chronic diseases such as diabetes and obesity, and delivering them to patients across many countries. Behind every product lies a manufacturing network that adheres to strict quality requirements. Every quality-relevant process step must be described, controlled, and validated through formal documentation. Keeping this documentation current and accurate is a core responsibility of the Manufacturing Technology team within the Tablet area, a responsibility that spans a large and diverse portfolio of controlled documents, including standard operating procedures, work instructions, forms, and training materials, across the global organization. Managing this portfolio presents significant challenges. Document updates are frequent, driven by regulatory changes, process improvements, equipment modifications, and audit findings. Each update demands careful review, structured approval workflows, and timely communication to all affected stakeholders. With thousands of active documents spread across multiple divisions, departments, and product programs, tracking which documents are due for revision, pending approval, or require immediate attention becomes increasingly complex. Much of this oversight currently relies on manual processes and fragmented tracking methods such as spreadsheets or disconnected systems.
