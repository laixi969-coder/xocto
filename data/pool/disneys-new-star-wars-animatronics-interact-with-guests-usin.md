# Disney’s new Star Wars animatronics interact with guests using AI

As advanced and lifelike as the animatronic characters at Disney parks have become, they typically either stick to predefined movements and routines, or there's an out of sight puppeteer helping make their behavior appear more spontaneous. That could soon change as Walt Disney Imagineering R&D has revealed three new Audio-Animatronic prototypes that rely on Amazon's AWS cloud and AI capabilities to respond in unique ways to guest interactions in real-time.
