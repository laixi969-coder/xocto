# Copilot is "the new OS for work," and now Office runs inside of it

Opening the keynote at its Ignite event, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella outlined the company's vision for its new "frontier ecosystem," announcing Tuesday the general availability of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint inside Copilot, which he described as "the new OS for work." The announcement is a striking inversion of how we've traditionally used software. Rather than an app like Word being the attraction and Clippy the assistant to help you use it, Microsoft envisions the new Copilot integrations will see agents driving things, with the software packages riding shotgun. Applications will become plugin components of the agent's work, backed by an organizational knowledge layer that draws context from emails, meetings, calendars, chats, and files. Users will now be able to create documents, spreadsheets, and presentations directly in Copilot, with the resulting files remaining editable in their respective Office applications.
