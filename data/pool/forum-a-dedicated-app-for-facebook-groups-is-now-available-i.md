# Forum, a Dedicated App for Facebook Groups, Is Now Available in Indonesia

We’re announcing the availability of Forum in Indonesia—a purpose-built app for people who want to go deeper on Facebook Groups. Forum brings your Facebook Groups into one dedicated app, making it easier to join conversations, find answers and participate in discussions that interest you. It is available to download on Android and will be available soon on iOS. Just log in with your Facebook account to find your groups and discover new ones. “Facebook has always been about connecting people. In Indonesia, we see how communities play an important role in bringing people together around shared interests, experiences, and needs. Through our conversations with local Facebook Group admins, we recognize the importance of making it easier for them to nurture these connections and support meaningful interactions. With Forum, we hope to better support Indonesia’s vibrant communities for its members and the admins who help them thrive,” said Pieter Lydian, Country Managing Director Meta for Indonesia. New Experiences on Forum Forum feed: Surfaces the most active conversations happening locally from your groups as well as groups related to your interests, including discussions from other groups you may be interested in. Posts in your Facebook groups and Forum are synced. Profile on
