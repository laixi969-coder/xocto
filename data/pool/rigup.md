Turn a gaming PC or a MacBook/Mac Mini into a local AI server with one install. A web dashboard installs and switches the models: chat and coding on both, image and video on NVIDIA. Tuned for 12 to 24 GB NVIDIA cards and 16 to 128 GB Apple Silicon Macs.
