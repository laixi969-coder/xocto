# Meta ads is now available in AWS Quick

Marketers have never had more data to act on. Take an apparel brand that advertises on Meta technologies: campaign results live in Meta Ads Manager, briefs and creative assets in shared storage, and inventory, customer lifetime value, and in-store purchases in a data lake. Analysts export reports, merge spreadsheets, and rebuild the same deck each week, and the data ages before anyone acts on it. When the team does decide what to change, someone returns to Ads Manager to update campaigns, ad sets, budgets, and audiences by hand. As a result, teams judge campaigns on metrics alone, and the path from insight to action stays slow and manual. Today, AWS Quick added a Meta ads AI connector. The connector gives Quick access to ads Model Context Protocol (MCP) server operations. Meta ads customers can use natural language to create, manage, and analyze campaigns within Quick, while staying connected to the Meta ad management network. Marketers can complete tasks with support from AI, such as pulling insights to understand ads performance, creating product catalogs, and editing existing or creating new campaigns.
