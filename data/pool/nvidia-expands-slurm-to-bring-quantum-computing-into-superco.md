# NVIDIA Expands Slurm to Bring Quantum Computing Into Supercomputing

Quantum processor deployments at supercomputing centers have grown nearly sevenfold over the past five years, with nearly 30 supercomputing centers around the world now in the process of deploying over 50 quantum processors. Many of these centers use Slurm to run scientific supercomputing workloads, so expanding its support for quantum processors builds on familiar workflows and tools, enabling computational chemists, nuclear physicists and materials scientists to bring quantum computing into their research domains without friction. In a new release, Slurm builds on previous work with IBM, Pasqal and the wider quantum computing community to allow QPU builders to create a single interface to their hardware for every supercomputing center, through a simple file. This makes it easier than ever for HPC centers and QPU builders to integrate and serve their hardware. The expansion is already being adopted by Alice & Bob , IBM , IQM , Pasqal , Quantinuum and MQSC and supports the widely used QRMI and QDMI interfaces.
