# Granola

Named to Madrona's 2026 Intelligent Applications 40 (Mid stage). Granola and Fireflies.ai all started as individual productivity tools built around meeting notes and transcription, and each is now explicitly repositioning toward becoming the enterprise knowledge layer for its customers. A second theme is voice and meeting capture as a wedge, with Wispr Flow, Granola, and adjacently HeyGen for video, where individual-productivity products are racing to become enterprise knowledge layers.
