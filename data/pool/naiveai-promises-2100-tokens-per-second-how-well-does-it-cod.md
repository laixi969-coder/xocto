# NaiveAI Promises 2,100 Tokens per Second. How Well Does It Code?

Editor’s note: With this post, we are launching a new series focused on trying new models and products with unbiased reviews. Reach out with suggestions to tips@latent.space. Naive-N0.5-Flash is a new open-weights model built for AI coding tasks and tuned to generate as many tokens per second as possible . It was developed by a Chinese company called NaiveAI , with the aim of speeding up engineering and research workflows so that teams can experiment, iterate and solve complex problems faster. The model is an evolution of Xiaomi’s MiMo-V2.5 , which NaiveAI has trained further for long-context reasoning and paired with its own NaiveRT inference engine. Naive-N0.5-Flash arrives with a bold pitch: a model built largely by AI and trained to do AI research itself, with recursive self-improvement as the long-term goal. To see what changed, it helps to know where it started. MiMo-V2.5 is a 310B-parameter sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 15B active parameters, trained on 48T tokens. Its language backbone inherits the hybrid design of MiMo-V2-Flash, which mixes sliding-window attention (SWA) with global self-attention. NaiveAI's recipe starts from those weights.
