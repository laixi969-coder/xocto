# YGO secures €8M to scale its AI-powered travel infrastructure

YGO , a company developing an AI-powered platform connecting travel companies with airlines, hotels and tour operators, has raised €8 million in a Series A funding round led by Ventech , with participation from Sake Bosch, Caesar Capital and business angels. Founded by Julian Kögel , Willi Ibbeken and Sebastian Kreutzberger , YGO provides AI infrastructure designed to help travel companies integrate AI-powered search, recommendations, customer service and booking capabilities into their existing systems. Its platform connects travel suppliers with both traditional booking channels and emerging AI assistants, allowing companies to offer their services without replacing their existing technology infrastructure. YGO's platform uses AI to organise and structure fragmented hotel and travel product information, making it easier for AI systems to understand and process available offerings. This enables personalised recommendations, semantic search and automated booking processes across different travel services, including flights, accommodation and activities. YGO aims to address the travel industry's reliance on intermediaries, including online travel agencies and metasearch platforms, which typically retain between 15 and 25 per cent of each booking. By enabling travel companies to connect directly with AI-powered booking channels, the platform is designed to help suppliers maintain control over customer relationships and improve margins.
