# CUDA at 20: How the Platform Transformed Scientific Computing

This week at SC26, NVIDIA is marking two decades of CUDA by honoring the researchers, developers and engineers who helped make GPU computing a foundation for modern science and AI. CUDA’s 20th anniversary presents an occasion to reflect across computing eras, from the people who wrote the very first CUDA programs to the AI developers building on their work today. At the platform’s core is this developer community whose experiments, tools and persistence expanded what accelerated computing could do. From SC06 to SC26 NVIDIA announced CUDA in November 2006 and brought it to the scientific community at SC06, giving scientists and developers a familiar, high-level programming model for using GPUs. It fundamentally extended accelerated computing beyond graphics use cases to accelerate demanding applications for science, simulation and more. In the two decades since, developers have used CUDA to advance Nobel Prize-winning AI research , accelerate scientific applications on supercomputers and support visual effects in Oscar-winning films . Today, more than 11 million developers build on CUDA across the computing stack.
