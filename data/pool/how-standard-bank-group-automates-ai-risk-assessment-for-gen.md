# How Standard Bank Group automates AI risk assessment for generative AI

This post is co-written with Tala Qraitem from AWS and Jörg Fischer from Standard Bank Group Having an AI policy is one thing. Proving, one use case at a time, that AI systems follow it is another. Every new generative AI application adds one more system to prove. Teams can now build one in days, but each still needs a risk committee’s approval before it reaches customers or employees. When that approval depends on reviewers reading documents and testing the system by hand, governance becomes the slowest step in the pipeline. Standard Bank Group chose to automate that proof. Working with AWS Professional Services, the bank deployed an automated assessment that scores each generative AI use case against international AI standards and its own policies. The assessment produces reports and a dashboard that show what is already in place, what is working well, and what still needs mitigation. This post explains how the assessment runs on AWS and what Standard Bank Group learned from its first runs.
