# Quoting Scott Aaronson

It’s now obvious that we’ll be getting, not one-off solutions to this or that complexity question, but a whole new continent of mathematical truth, one that we’d previously only glimpsed through fog. Picture van Leeuwenhoek first training a microscope on pondwater, or Galileo first training his telescope on the stars. That might give you some idea of how our resolution into the complexity-theoretic universe, and the whole universe of mathematical truth beyond it, has now improved. — Scott Aaronson Tags: mathematics , llms , ai , generative-ai
