# European Cities Deploy NVIDIA-Powered AI Agents to Run Smarter Urban Operations

Keeping a city running means making decisions that affect residents every day — from when to repair a road to how to use energy more efficiently. Across the EMEA region, cities and local software, infrastructure and integration partners are using NVIDIA accelerated computing to build physical AI agents and digital twins that help teams make those decisions. These applications can anticipate maintenance needs, guide urban planning and make public services more responsive. Software partners including ABA Technology , Akila , Galene AI , Honeywell Technologies , K2K , Linker Vision , Milestone , Onetera , ThinkDeep and Younite AI are using NVIDIA physical and agentic AI to develop solutions for city operations and public services. https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/linker-vision.mp4 Video courtesy of Linker Vision. NVIDIA Cosmos open models and the NVIDIA Metropolis Blueprint for video search and summarization (VSS) help agents interpret video and identify conditions, such as traffic congestion, equipment condition, underused spaces and safety hazards, that need attention. NVIDIA Nemotron open models enable developers to build agents that reason over information, respond in natural language and deploy digital twins with NVIDIA Omniverse libraries.
