# The Crunchbase Unicorn Board Tops 1,900 Companies In October, With 34 Companies Joining — Half Under 4 Years Old

A total of 34 companies joined The Crunchbase Unicorn Board in October, adding more than $96 billion in value and $14.8 billion in total funding to the board. The board also reached a new record of 1,900 private companies last month, including an all-time high of more than 1,000 such startups based in the U.S. Last month’s new unicorn cohort also highlighted how quickly this AI cycle is awarding some companies with billion-dollar-plus valuations: Half of October’s new unicorns are less than 4 years old, and nine companies that joined the board during the month did so via seed or Series A raises. Leading industries for unicorn formation last month were frontier labs, agentic AI, aerospace and semiconductors. The U.S. accounts for 23 of the companies, while China and Korea each added three. Estonia added two, a record for the nation in a single month. Singapore, Germany and Japan each added one new unicorn. The most valuable of the newly minted unicorns were electrical infrastructure company Axiom Solutions International , TypeSafe AI a model developer of Jev, and agentic AI assistant company Manus AI .
