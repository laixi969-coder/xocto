# Raising the stakes for enterprise networks with AI modernization

Enterprise networks are under severe strain. As companies rush to deploy artificial intelligence, the technology is quickly becoming the biggest source of demand on the network . Now, agentic AI is adding to the load. Tasks conducted by AI agents generate an estimated 450% more network traffic than the same tasks done by human workers. As AI inference shifts from hyperscale training clouds—the huge centralized data centers where raw AI models ingest vast datasets and learn—to real-time, latency-sensitive applications, the need for on-premises or near-premises infrastructure grows. As a result, the gap between enterprise networks that are ready to support AI and those that are not is widening. As few as half of enterprises believe their current networks can handle AI’s bandwidth and latency demands, according to a recent study .
