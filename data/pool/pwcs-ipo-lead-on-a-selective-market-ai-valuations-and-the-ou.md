# PwC’s IPO Lead On A Selective Market, AI Valuations And The Outlook For 2027

The U.S. IPO market has rebounded, but the recovery remains uneven. Companies seeking to go public face investors focused on durable revenue, profitability and valuation, while uncertainty has prompted some issuers to postpone their offerings. Mike Bellin , a partner and U.S. IPO services leader at PricewaterhouseCoopers , sees a market that remains open to companies with the right scale, financial metrics and preparation. In a recent conversation with Crunchbase News, Bellin discussed the slowdown in IPO momentum, how public investors are evaluating AI companies, biotech’s resurgence and why SPACs are attracting renewed interest. He also outlined what could set the stage for a stronger start to 2027. This interview has been edited for length and clarity. Crunchbase News: How healthy is the IPO market right now? Mike Bellin, US IPO services leader, PricewaterhouseCooper. (Courtesy photo) Bellin: A good IPO year in the U.S. is anywhere from 125 to 175 traditional IPOs, and year to date we’ve seen just south of 90 or so. The breadth of the market shows me that it’s been a healthy market. We’ve seen industrial products come back to market. We’ve seen tech, consumer markets, energy, utilities and some financial services come back to market.
