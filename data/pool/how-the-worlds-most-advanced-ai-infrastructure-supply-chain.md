# How the World’s Most Advanced AI Infrastructure Supply Chain Invents the Future

Deb Shoquist leads operations at NVIDIA, overseeing the supply chain and manufacturing behind its AI platforms. That description sums up the job, but it doesn’t begin to encompass what Shoquist has made possible over decades. Shoquist, her team and their global network of manufacturing partners enable NVIDIA to introduce world-leading technologies year-round. Much of the team’s success comes down to her deep relationships with some of the world’s most accomplished manufacturers, including TSMC, Foxconn, Wistron, SPIL, King Yuan Electronics (KYEC), Unimicron, Lumentum, Coherent and Amkor. TSMC has partnered with NVIDIA since 1998. SPIL and KYEC were assembling and testing its chips more than two decades ago. For Shoquist, those relationships grow stronger through each challenge solved together on the factory floor, as every generation of technology demands more from the people and tools behind it. Ask her about the global network she relies on, and she starts with the people. There’s the supplier in Taiwan so eager to get production moving that he calls her at 3 a.m. with a fix to the latest problem.
