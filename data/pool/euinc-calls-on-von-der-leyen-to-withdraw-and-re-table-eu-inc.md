# EU–INC calls on von der Leyen to withdraw and re-table EU Inc proposal

EU–INC and Startups for Europe are calling on European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen to withdraw and re-table the original EU Inc proposal, warning that compromises made by EU Member States threaten to undermine one of Europe’s most important economic reforms for startups and scaleups. The announcement comes as Europe’s leading policymakers, founders and investors gather at Slush in Helsinki to discuss the state of EU Inc. After two years of campaigning for a single European company standard, EU–INC and Startups for Europe remain strong supporters of the European Commission’s original proposal. The Commission delivered a solid framework that could finally give European founders a genuine alternative to incorporating in Delaware, and allow companies to start and scale under one common European standard. But the Council’s negotiating mandate of 19 October has weakened or removed some of the very provisions that would make EU Inc a true European standard: Free choice of registration – Weakened: Founders can choose where to incorporate, but national authorities have more room to challenge that choice in certain circumstances, creating legal uncertainty.
