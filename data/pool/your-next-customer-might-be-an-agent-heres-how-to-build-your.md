# Your Next Customer Might Be An Agent. Here’s How To Build Your Brand For Both Humans And AI.

By Toby Coulthard You’ve just moved into a new house. It’s unfurnished; the walls are bare. Ten years ago, you might have loaded up Google and started searching for furniture that you like, consulting interior design magazines and looking for offers on retailers’ websites. In 2026, however, there’s an entirely different option at your fingertips. You could take a photo of the empty room, upload the image to an AI agent, share your budget and style, and get a list of furniture that suits your preferences. You could even have the agent purchase on your behalf. Instead of just selling to one customer base — humans — brands are now selling to two. This second audience of AI agents makes decisions on different criteria than humans. Often, in fact, AI agents will make purchasing decisions before a human ever sees the product page. There’s a real chance that all the emotive copy marketers have so carefully crafted will get broken down and reconstituted by a machine. Just relying on the human touch isn’t enough anymore.
