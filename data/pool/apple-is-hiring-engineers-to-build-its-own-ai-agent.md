# Apple is hiring engineers to build its own AI agent

Apple is working on its own AI agent that can perform a range of tasks for you, including browsing the web, working across apps, writing code, and calling services. That's according to a job listing spotted by Bloomberg , which says Apple is looking for an engineer to lead the creation of an agentic AI assistant. An AI agent from Apple would put the iPhone maker in the running to compete with the likes of Meta's Muse , OpenAI's Dots , Anthropic's Duet , and Google's Tasks . Muse, ChatGPT, and Perplexity can already take control of a computer through their Mac apps, while Google just started letting Gemini do the same.
