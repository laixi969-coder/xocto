# Announcing AI data preparation capabilities in Amazon SageMaker: Build high-quality datasets for model development workflows

Teams that ship generative AI to production know that a model is only as good as its data. That is why preparing high-quality training and evaluation data comes first. Fine-tuning an open-weight model, running reinforcement learning, or building an evaluation suite all depend on data that is accurate, well-labeled, and representative of your task. For narrow, well-defined tasks, a smaller model customized on clean, task-specific data can often match or exceed the accuracy of a larger general-purpose model prompted into shape, at a lower cost per inference. In practice, though, preparing that data is often the slowest part of the project. Labeled examples are scarce or expensive, and privacy rules limit what you can use. Quality problems such as duplicates, label noise, and answer leakage go unnoticed until a training run disappoints, and every training technique expects data in its own format. As a result, data scientists spend weeks wrangling data instead of building models. Today, we’re expanding Amazon SageMaker AI with new AI data preparation capabilities for model development workflows.
