# French deeptech HyLight raises €13M to industrialise autonomous hydrogen-powered airships

HyLight , the French startup specialising in aerial inspection of critical infrastructure using autonomous hydrogen-powered airships, has raised €13 million from Rubio Impact Ventures , DRW, and Teampact Ventures, alongside existing investors Y Combinator and Ring Capital. Founded in 2022 and based at Air Base 217 in Brétigny-sur-Orge, France, HyLight designs, manufactures, and operates its own hydrogen-powered airships, combining high-precision infrastructure inspections with AI-powered data analysis. The Y Combinator-backed company has progressed from developing prototypes to carrying out commercial inspections for major French energy and transport operators. When I last spoke to Martin Bocken, co-founder and CEO of HyLight, the company was still focused on developing its technology. Two years later, the business has moved from R&D to commercial deployment, replacing helicopter inspections of critical infrastructure. Bocken explained that the biggest challenge wasn't simply building the aircraft, but turning a prototype into a commercially viable solution: "When you start from scratch, you think the biggest challenge is building the product. Actually, that's only the beginning." Industrialising the technology has required HyLight to tackle manufacturing, recruitment, costs, and operational scaling simultaneously.
