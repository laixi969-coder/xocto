# AI Day Seoul Brings Together Korea’s Industry Leaders, Startups and Developer Communities

Over two days at COEX, more than 2,000 attendees converged for AI Day Seoul, bringing together Korea’s industry leaders, startups and developer communities advancing physical AI and AI agents. At the plenary sessions, Deepu Talla, vice president of robotics and edge AI at NVIDIA, said Korea is “punching above its weight,” with Doosan Robotics, Hyundai Motor Group, LG Electronics and SK Group — as well as startups like Real World and Luda Robotics — using NVIDIA platforms to bring general-purpose robotics into the real world. Jonathan Cohen, vice president of applied research at NVIDIA, showed how enterprises can build agents they own and control. Breakout sessions featured speakers from Coupang Intelligent Cloud, Doosan Robotics, LG AI Research, LG Electronics, NAVER Cloud, SK Telecom and Upstage. “It was a genuinely enjoyable time to have candid conversations,” said Hyunho Yang, research engineer for omnimodal foundation models at SK Telecom. “Seeing the passion of fellow AI developers was a real source of inspiration for me.” Community leaders share how they build with open models and tools at AI Day Seoul. The Agentic AI 10x Bootcamp Community Day brought together Pseudo Lab, Python Korea, PyTorchKR, HAERAE, Hugging Face KREW, Graph User Group, Womxn Who
