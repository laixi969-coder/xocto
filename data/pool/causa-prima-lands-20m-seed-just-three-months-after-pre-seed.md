# Causa Prima lands $20M seed just three months after pre-seed round

Causa Prima , a company developing an autonomous finance platform for businesses, has raised $20 million in a seed funding round led by Base10 Partners . The round also saw participation from QED Investors and FJ Labs, alongside existing investors Creandum, Kfund and Borusan Ventures. The investment comes just three months after its $10 million pre-seed round, bringing the company's total funding to $30 million. Founded by Maex Ament (CEO), Henrik Gebbing and Philip Stanislaus , Causa Prima is developing a platform designed to automate financial processes between companies, particularly invoice approvals, dispute resolution and payments. The company focuses on an area of corporate finance that remains largely manual despite significant investment in financial software. According to a study by Atradius, nearly half of credit-based B2B sales in the US are overdue, highlighting the challenges businesses face in managing payments between companies. Causa Prima's platform uses autonomous agents to handle communications and negotiations between businesses, including disputed invoices and early-payment requests. Rather than relying on finance teams to exchange emails and follow up manually, the agents can manage these interactions directly.
