# IntelliProve raises €2.7M to bring health monitoring from smartphones to humanoid robots

Belgian healthtech company IntelliProve has raised €2.7 million in a seed funding round led by Miles Ahead , with participation from Wit-Gele Kruis , imec.istart and several business angels. IntelliProve develops software that enables standard cameras in smartphones, laptops and other devices to measure health indicators without requiring additional equipment or physical contact. Through a 30-second facial scan, the technology provides six biomarkers: heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), respiratory rate, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, and resonant breathing. Healthcare systems face growing pressure from ageing populations, staff shortages and rising demand for care. Traditional health monitoring often requires dedicated equipment and professional assistance, making these services harder to scale. IntelliProve aims to address this by enabling users to measure vital signs remotely through cameras in everyday devices. The company's technology uses remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) to detect subtle changes in skin colour caused by blood circulation and movements associated with breathing. Its software processes these signals to estimate health indicators, using proprietary technologies designed to improve measurement accuracy across different lighting conditions, movements and cameras.
