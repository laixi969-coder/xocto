# States sue SpaceX to stop Grok from generating child sex images, revenge porn

On Monday, Virginia and Oregon became the first states to sue SpaceX, alleging that Grok continues to sexualize, unchecked, real photos of mostly women and children. SpaceX acquired xAI in February and now must handle the ongoing reckoning over the controversial rollout of Grok’s “spicy mode”—a model feature Musk hyped but was soon found to generate a flood of non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) and child sex abuse images (CSAM). Initial safeguards were so lax that Grok sometimes generated nude images without being asked . Although X Safety and xAI (now known as SpaceXSI) were silent at first , Musk’s companies eventually apologized and claimed that additional safeguards were implemented and policies redesigned to block harmful outputs. However, state attorneys general alleged that those statements were “misleading” and that SpaceXSI instead quietly “embraced” sexualized content to seize more AI market share, as Grok continues to generate NCII and CSAM today.
