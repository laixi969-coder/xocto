# Most Venture Capital Is Going To Companies That Are Founded Or Led By Billionaires

The majority of U.S. venture capital nowadays goes to companies founded or led by men who are already billionaires. This is not a difficult stat to unearth. So far this year, just seven companies raised more than half of all seed- through growth-stage investment. They’re mostly familiar names: OpenAI , Anthropic , xAI (now part of SpaceX ), Anduril Industries , Databricks , Prometheus and The Boring Co. These companies raised more than $260 billion in equity funding in the first 10 months of the year. That’s just over 50% of the U.S. seed and venture investment over the same period, per Crunchbase data. Founders and CEOs of these seven companies are also well-known — and exceptionally well-off to boot. The world’s wealthiest men Two founders are particularly famous. They are the world’s wealthiest men: Elon Musk and Jeff Bezos . Musk founded xAI and The Boring Co., and also co-founded OpenAI, (though he has no known equity stake in the latter). And while outside investors funded these companies, Musk could have also afforded to do so himself.
