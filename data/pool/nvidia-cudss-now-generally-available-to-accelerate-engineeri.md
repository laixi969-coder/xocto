# NVIDIA cuDSS Now Generally Available to Accelerate Engineering and Scientific Simulations

NVIDIA cuDSS, launched today in general availability, is an NVIDIA CUDA-X library that shifts a critical part of engineering and scientific simulations onto GPUs. Complex engineering feats — from advanced chips to aircraft to industrial systems —– depend on simulation to predict how designs will perform before they’re built. As designs become more complex, simulations take longer to run, increasing computing demands and limiting the number of ideas teams can test. NVIDIA cuDSS accelerates solving large sparse systems — the math-heavy step that repeats throughout engineering and scientific simulations and often limits how many designs a team can test — by tapping into GPUs. This lets applications reduce time to results and increase simulation throughput, enabling end users to explore larger more realistic models, more accurate representations and a wider design space at a reduced cost.
