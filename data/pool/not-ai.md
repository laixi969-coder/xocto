Not Ai isn’t just about bypassing AI detectors. It puts good writing first, creating clear, honest prose shaped by human writing standards. It’s perfect for college and school projects, LinkedIn/X posts, emails, factual articles, personal essays, social media posts, and technical documentation, especially GitHub READMEs
