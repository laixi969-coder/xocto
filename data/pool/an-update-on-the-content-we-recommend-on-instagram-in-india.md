# An Update on the Content We Recommend on Instagram in India

Over the past year, we’ve strengthened the default protections behind what Instagram recommends in India to reduce unwanted content and improve people’s experience on Instagram. This work has been guided by what our users told us they want from their recommendations. Today, we’re sharing an update on this work. How Recommendations Work At Instagram, we make personalized recommendations to help people discover new communities and content. Our goal is to make recommendations that are relevant and valuable to each person who sees them. For example, if you interact with restaurants and bookstores on Instagram, we may recommend content about food, recipes, books, or reading. Recommendations include content from accounts you don’t already follow, in places like Explore, Reels and Feed , so we apply guidelines specific to them. Through our Recommendations Guidelines , we work to avoid recommending content that could be low quality, objectionable or particularly sensitive. We use technology to detect content and accounts that don’t meet those guidelines and to help us avoid recommending them. As always, content that goes against our Community Standards is removed from Instagram, and content that is unlawful in a country is restricted there.
