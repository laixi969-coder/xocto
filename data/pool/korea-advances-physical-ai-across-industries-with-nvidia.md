# Korea Advances Physical AI Across Industries With NVIDIA

Tapping into the country’s strengths in manufacturing and engineering, NVIDIA is helping advance Korea’s physical AI ecosystem. The country’s industry leaders are applying accelerated computing, AI and simulation to everything from semiconductor manufacturing to robotics and autonomous vehicles. These efforts are helping bring physical AI from development into real-world production. Samsung Advances Semiconductor Engineering With Accelerated Computing and AI Physics Samsung Electronics is expanding its collaboration with NVIDIA to advance semiconductor innovation through accelerated computing and AI physics. The work spans materials research, process development and device engineering. In technology computer-aided design, Samsung is working with NVIDIA cuISS and NVIDIA cuDSS to accelerate computations involving sparse linear systems. The collaboration also extends to GPU acceleration for process and optical simulations. In materials research, Samsung’s sMOL platform has run selected quantum chemical calculations up to 5x faster end to end with NVIDIA cuEST . Samsung is also exploring NVIDIA cuEquivariance to speed up tensor operations used in training and inference for atomistic AI models. Samsung is developing chip-scale thermal and mechanical analysis workflows with NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo .
