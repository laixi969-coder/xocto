# Gemini on Mac can now use your computer

Google's Gemini app for macOS now has computer use, meaning it can help you "get multi-step tasks done across your local files, desktop apps, and the web," the company says . Google suggests the feature might be useful for things like speeding up workflows, filling in forms, making presentations, and listing items for sale. Users will be able to decide which of your apps Gemini can access, and be able to follow along as Gemini works because it has its own background window that shows a live preview in your chat, Google says.
