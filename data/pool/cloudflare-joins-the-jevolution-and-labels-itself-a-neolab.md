# Cloudflare Joins the Jevolution and Labels Itself a Neolab

Cloudflare has been one of the essential infrastructure companies of the web for the past decade and a half. But now, it’s turning into a neolab — at least according to a presentation at AI Engineer Code Summit by Michelle Chen , who runs Cloudflare’s AI Platform on the product side. We’ll get into what exactly a neolab is in a minute (the short version: they build models and they’re not called OpenAI or Anthropic). Cloudflare is claiming to be one because it too has begun to launch models. Two System One models , to be precise: Clef and Clairic. As Chen explained in her talk, Clef is a decision model directly inspired by TypeSafe AI’s revolutionary Jev model , while Clairic is a new type of system one model that Cloudflare calls an “extract” model . In an interview with Latent Space after her presentation, Chen hinted that Cloudflare won’t just be producing system one models. There will be large language models, too. “We're not being a neolab [to] only launch system one models, even though it makes sense for our infrastructure. We've kind of entered this Pandora's box now, right? We opened it, now the possibilities are endless.”
