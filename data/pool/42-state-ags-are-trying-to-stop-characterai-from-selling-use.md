# 42 state AGs are trying to stop Character.AI from selling users’ chats

A coalition of 42 state attorneys general is trying to prevent Character.AI from selling off user data as it navigates Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings. In a deluge of nearly identical press releases, the various AGs accuse the company of planning to auction off "the private chat histories of more than 21 million U.S. accounts - more than 2.1 million of them registered to minors - with no safeguards for the people whose conversations are being sold." In October, Character.AI filed for bankruptcy following years of scandals and legal challenges , including from Disney .
