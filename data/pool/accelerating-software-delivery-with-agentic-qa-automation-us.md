# Accelerating software delivery with agentic QA automation using Amazon Nova Act – Part 3

As quality assurance (QA) automation adoption grows inside an organization, a new problem appears that has nothing to do with writing tests. A platform team stands up test automation, then a second team wants to join, then a dozen more. Soon hundreds of test cases belong to different products and squads, and everyone can see everyone else’s tests. No one can answer a basic question at a glance: is this application’s test coverage healthy right now? Test authoring stops being the bottleneck. Organizing tests and controlling who can touch them becomes the work. A second, related question follows close behind: who owns this application and its tests, and who is accountable when coverage slips? Without a clear owner, health problems have no one to land on. In the first post , we introduced QA Studio, a reference solution built with Amazon Nova Act . Teams use it to define tests in natural language and run them through AI-powered visual navigation. In the second post , we added test suites and a CLI for batch regression testing in continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines.
