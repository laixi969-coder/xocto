# owlmic

Turn your Android phone into a high-quality PC webcam & studio microphone! Stream 1080p video & low-latency Opus audio over USB (ADB), Wi-Fi & Bluetooth. Features RNNoise AI noise reduction, AEC & DirectShow virtual camera. Fast, privacy-first, zero cloud, no accounts, 100% open source. Faster alternative of DroidCam, Iriun & WO Mic.
