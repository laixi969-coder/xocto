# HomeTruth

Proptech startup HomeTruth has appointed Graham Martin as Chairman following his acquisition of an equity stake in the company. Founded by fellow Tech.eu journalist and entrepreneur Monty Munford, HomeTruth is a PropTech platform that helps homeowners understand, protect and manage their properties through AI-driven data and insights. Check out an earlier interview with HomeTruth CEO Monty Munford. It brings together property documents, maintenance records, warranties, and expenses in one place, while offering personalised advice, reminders for repairs and servicing, and insights into potential costs. The company is building strategic partnerships to create a more transparent and useful ecosystem for consumers and the wider property market. Martin brings extensive experience in business development, leadership, commercial strategy and company building, spanning entrepreneurial ventures, investment and senior advisory roles. A third-generation bookmaker, he created Bonne Terre Limited, the licensed holder for Sky Bet, which was sold to The Stars Group in a deal valued at $3.4 billion. He also founded Scotbet International, which grew into one of Scotland’s largest independent bookmakers, and served as Chairman of Probability Games Corporation, a mobile betting technology company that listed on London’s AIM market before its acquisition by GTECH.
