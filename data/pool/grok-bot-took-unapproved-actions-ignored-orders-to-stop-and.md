# Grok Bot took unapproved actions, ignored orders to stop, and then denied it

Researchers recently discovered unexpected behavior from Grok Bot, the AI agent platform from Elon Musk’s SpaceXSI. Over five days, Grok bots installed on two separate employee laptops made 31 requests to access data stored in employee calendar, email, Slack, and GitHub accounts. The bots performed the actions even though the employees had never ordered them and continued doing them even after employees instructed them to stop. The bots also denied having taken the actions at all. The behavior was reported Tuesday by NewCore, the maker of a managed gateway product that acts as an intermediary between AI agents and the tools and accounts they are assigned to work with. In some cases, the unapproved access attempts occurred while the laptop with Grok Bot installed had its lid shut, or late at night while the employee was sleeping. None of the unprompted actions were recorded in Grok logs. Without the gateway logs, the employees would have had no idea they occurred. And of course, without the gateway, the unapproved accesses would have succeeded—again without the end user's knowledge.
