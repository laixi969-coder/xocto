# British Business Bank invests £180M across AI and deeptech funds

The British Business Bank has today announced it is investing £180m across six AI and deeptech VC funds, as investors continue to pile into the two hot sectors. The state-owned investor is investing £35m in London-based Crane Ventures Partners’ Crane III fund, which backs founders from inception to seed; it is also investing £20m in London-based Connect Ventures’ Connect V fund, which backs founders at pre-seed stages; and it’s investing £22m in Matt Clifford’s Entrepreneurs First’s early-stage fund. It is also investing £55m in Intrepid Growth Partners’ Fund I, a specialist growth fund dedicated to AI; £26m in global investor Headline’s EU VIII fund; and £22m in Matt Miller’s Evantic Fund I. The new investments follow the British Business Bank's announcement yesterday that is was investing £10m in Salience Labs. The British Business Bank, which was given increased spending power last year following a government review, has also directly invested in Wayve, Kraken Techologies and PolyAI. Leandros Kalisperas, chief investment officer, the British Business Bank, said: “The UK has no shortage of ideas or talent.
