# Scarlet

Scarlet , the certification body for AI and software medical devices, today announced a $50 million Series B led by Headline , with participation from new investor Notion Capital and existing investors Atomico, Creandum, Kindred Capital, and Entrepreneurs First. Nancy Xiao Mclaughlin, Partner at Headline, joins Scarlet’s board. AI medical devices are being developed faster than certification bodies can review them. A company can train an AI diagnostic model in weeks and then wait 12 to 18 months for an assessment by experts, with another six to eight months of unnecessary delay for approval of a minor update, slowing down healthcare innovation. Scarlet is a government-designated certification body. Among the roughly 52 such organisations designated under the EU Medical Device Regulation, it is, according to the company, the only one that specialises in software and AI, and was truly built to enable AI’s iterative development. Scarlet holds designations from the EU, the UK’s MHRA, and the US FDA, where it is recognised as a Third Party Review Organisation. James Dewar and Jamie Cox founded Scarlet in London in 2021 after building AI medical devices at Babylon Health. The company spent its first three years working with regulators to obtain designation
