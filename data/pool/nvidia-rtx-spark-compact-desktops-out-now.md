# NVIDIA RTX Spark Compact Desktops Out Now

New NVIDIA RTX Spark compact desktops from ASUS, Dell and MSI are available today, bringing local AI agents, image and video generation, and model fine-tuning to a dedicated Windows PC. With up to 128GB of unified memory and NVIDIA’s AI software stack, these systems provide room for larger local models alongside RTX-accelerated creative and development tools. A dedicated desktop can keep agents and scheduled tasks running while a laptop travels or handles other work. Alongside the new desktops, an expanding software ecosystem brings more creative and development workflows to RTX Spark laptops and compact desktops. Native Windows on Arm applications from Adobe, the NVIDIA DLSS Ray Reconstruction feature in Blender and local generation workflows in ComfyUI expand what creators and developers can do on RTX Spark — all on one Windows PC. A Windows PC Built for Local AI As workflows expand to include local AI models and agents, more work can continue beyond an active editing session — from generating assets to processing long renders or scheduled tasks. RTX Spark compact desktops give developers, creators and AI enthusiasts a dedicated Windows PC for local models and always-on agents. They bring together image and video generation, model fine-tuning, creative applications
