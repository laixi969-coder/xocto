# Polish spacetech KP Labs secures €4.42M from Momentum Capital Partners to scale in-orbit AI data processing

Polish company KP Labs , which develops solutions for processing satellite data directly in Earth orbit, has received €4.42 million (PLN 19.4 million) from Momentum Capital Partners . As the space industry continues to expand, the volume of data satellites collect is growing faster than the capacity to transmit it back to Earth. At the same time, increasing satellite density in orbit and the growing importance of space for defence and security are creating a need for satellites capable of autonomously manoeuvring and monitoring their surroundings. KP Labs addresses these challenges with proprietary solutions that enable real-time data analysis in orbit, allowing critical information to reach Earth faster and more cheaply. KP Labs' portfolio includes specialised processing units, firmware, and AI algorithms for data processing and selection. Customers include space agencies, satellite manufacturers, and space hardware providers. The company works with space agencies, including the European Space Agency (ESA) and NASA, as well as leading European space companies such as Airbus Defence and Space, OHB Hellas, and Thales Alenia Space. Among KP Labs' data-processing achievements is Intuition-1, its own satellite, which operated in orbit from 2023 to 2026 and collected Earth observation data on soil conditions, vegetation, and pollution.
