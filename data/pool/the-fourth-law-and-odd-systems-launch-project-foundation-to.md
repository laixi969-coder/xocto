# The Fourth Law and Odd Systems launch Project Foundation to quadruple European thermal sensor production

The Fourth Law and Odd Systems today unveiled Project Foundation, a semiconductor initiative to mass-produce uncooled thermal sensors in Europe and the United States. The project aims to expand European thermal sensor production to four times its current level and US production to twice its current level within three years, building a new industrial base for defence and everyday applications. The primary mission of Project Foundation is to secure thermal sensor sovereignty for Europe and the United States by developing proprietary uncooled long-wave infrared (LWIR) sensors, also known as microbolometers, and the capacity to manufacture them at scale. Thermal imaging is increasingly important to modern warfare, autonomous systems, and industrial applications, enabling drones, vehicles, and robots to operate in darkness, poor visibility, and challenging environments. However, developing cameras is only part of the challenge: access to affordable sensors and reliable manufacturing capacity is equally critical. Project Foundation aims to address this bottleneck by expanding production in Europe and the US, reducing dependence on external suppliers, and making thermal imaging technology more accessible at scale.
