# Jensen Huang says he’s willing to return as Nvidia CEO

Jensen Huang, who resigned as Nvidia's CEO last week, would be willing to return as CEO if the company's board of directors wanted it, he said in a statement reported on by The Wall Street Journal . "Since my departure, I've seen an outpouring of calls from Nvidia's employees, investors, business partners, and customers for me to return as CEO," Huang said . "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO."
