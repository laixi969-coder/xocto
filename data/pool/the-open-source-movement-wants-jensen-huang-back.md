# The open-source movement wants Jensen Huang back

This story originally appeared in The Algorithm, our weekly newsletter on AI. To get stories like this in your inbox first, sign up here . On Friday, a petition to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO of Nvidia began circulating on social media, and by Monday it listed some notable signatories, including Elon Musk and several influential Nvidia researchers. But comb through the more than 115,000 names and you’ll notice a significant faction of Huang’s supporters—higher-ups from Hugging Face, a hub for sharing AI models, and venture capital firms Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia Capital—come from a particular slice of the tech world. These names have a vested interest in the development of open-source AI, models that, unlike ChatGPT or Claude, can be downloaded and run independent of large tech companies. Supporters of such models say that concentrating AI in the hands of a few powerful companies will leave everyone else worse off (a concern that wouldn’t sound out of place among the AI industry’s critics on the left, if so many open-source advocates weren’t also venture capitalists who stand to profit when startups can build on cheaper AI without paying Big Tech).
