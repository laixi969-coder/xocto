# Amazon drone crashes on garage after delivering shampoo, draws FAA probe

An Amazon delivery drone that clipped a fiber-optic line before crashing onto a garage roof is now the subject of an investigation by the US Federal Aviation Administration. It’s the latest delivery drone mishap caught on video as Amazon and other companies are ramping up such aerial delivery operations across the United States. The November 20 incident began with Jim Brennan and his wife, residents of Huntington Woods, Michigan, placing an order for shampoo and being surprised by a notification about the package arriving by drone within seven minutes, according to WXYZ 7 News Detroit . After the drone dropped the package into Brennan’s backyard, it lost a propeller when it caught on fiber-optic cable and floundered sideways before getting pinned between the neighbor’s garage roof and some power lines. “Like a sick bird, it tried to go up, it tried to get out of here,” Brennan told WXYZ 7.
