# Cognition

A total of 38 companies joined The Crunchbase Unicorn Board in September, adding $71 billion in value to the board. Semiconductors, financial services and space technology led for new counts with the remainder hailing from a broad swath of industries including biotech, AI infrastructure, AI training, security and frontier labs. Twenty-one of last month’s new unicorns are U.S. headquartered. Six new unicorns joined the board from China and three from the U.K., while Canada, Saudi Arabia, Japan, Israel, Italy, Finland, Germany and Bulgaria each added one new unicorn. Through September, 311 companies have joined the board so far this year, putting 2026 on track to be the second-highest year for new unicorn creation, following only the venture peak year of 2021, when counts topped 600. Several companies already on the board stand out for significant valuation increases via new raises last month. They include San Francisco-based Cognition , developer of AI for software engineering, which saw its value climb to $48 billion, up from $26 billion only four months earlier.
