# What’s Next For Recruiting, Job Hunting And HR? More AI, Of Course

AI agents and humanoid robots may dominate the funding headlines. But even so, investors haven’t lost interest in startups focused on finding, filling and managing jobs for actual humans. So far this year, venture backers have poured close to $4 billion into an assortment of global companies focused on recruiting, finding jobs and streamlining processes associated with HR and payroll management, Crunchbase data shows. Not surprisingly, the vast majority are also incorporating a heavy dose of AI. To get a sense where capital is flowing, we used Crunchbase data to analyze investment to employment-related categories for the past few years. Broadly, findings show 2026 funding on track to meet or slightly exceed year-ago levels, albeit with reduced deal count. As you can see, it’s a varied list, with a commonly shared theme of bringing more automation to hiring, HR and payroll tasks.
