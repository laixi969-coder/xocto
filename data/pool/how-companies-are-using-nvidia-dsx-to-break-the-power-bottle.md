# How Companies Are Using NVIDIA DSX to Break the Power Bottleneck

Companies around the world are building on the NVIDIA DSX AI Factory Platform to plan, simulate and run the grid and the AI factory as one system, from the substation to the rack. Their adoption of the platform responds to an acute problem. Power is now the biggest bottleneck in infrastructure. More than $300 billion in equipment is backlogged, and over 200 gigawatts of projects are waiting in U.S. interconnection queues. One root cause of the bottleneck is siloed systems. The grid, the facility and the compute inside have long been designed independently from one another, creating the conditions for delays, risk and stranded capacity. NVIDIA DSX Flex syncs AI factory demand with grid conditions and onsite generation, turning AI factories from static loads into flexible grid assets.
