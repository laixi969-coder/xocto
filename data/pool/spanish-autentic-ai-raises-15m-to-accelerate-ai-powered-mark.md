# Autentic AI

Autentic AI has raised €1.5 million in pre-seed funding from venture capital firms Baobab Ventures, Lanai Ventures, Acurio Ventures and Masia VC. Founded by Dani Diestre , Aniol Carreras and Jacob Bamio , Autentic AI has developed a platform that uses artificial intelligence to conduct interviews with hundreds of consumers and experts simultaneously, reducing the time required for market research from weeks or months to days. The company combines AI-led interviews and data analysis with human researchers who interpret the findings and provide context. The platform aims to address the time and cost associated with traditional market research, which can limit how frequently companies gather customer feedback. Autentic AI seeks to make research more accessible, allowing businesses to gather customer insights more regularly and use them to inform their decisions. Dani Diestre, co-founder and co-CEO of Autentic AI, commented: When a study costs €100,000 and takes three months, companies cannot run one every time they need an answer. By the time the findings arrive, the market may have moved on. The real cost is making important decisions without hearing from customers. At Autentic, AI conducts the interviews and researchers interpret what the answers mean. We want research to be something
