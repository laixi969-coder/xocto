# Leading Tech Companies Join Global Law Enforcement in Major Crackdown on West African Scam Networks

Today, Meta, Coinbase, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Starlink, Adobe, and other leading tech companies announced the results of a major joint operation with law enforcement to disrupt criminal scam networks based in West African nations, including Nigeria and Ghana. Hosted by the National Crime Agency (NCA) in London in partnership with the US Department of Justice, the FBI, and several US federal agencies, the operation marked the latest and largest instance of tech companies coming together with law enforcement around the world to share information, take down sophisticated criminals, and help protect people from online scams. This is the first joint disruption to broadly target criminal scam networks in the West Africa region. Scam centers based in Nigeria, Ghana, and other nations in the region are generally more decentralized and less industrial than those in southeast Asia. Criminals are continually seeking new havens where they believe they can operate with impunity, and it’s essential that law enforcement, industry, and government work together to disrupt these criminals wherever they may try to organize and operate.
