# Ars Live recap: Don’t expect military attacks on data centers to stop

Drone attacks on data centers. Amazon data centers came under military attack for the first time ever in the Middle East this year, and Russia and Ukraine exchanged an unprecedented flurry of drone strikes on each other’s data centers. To examine what such attacks on data centers mean for the future of cloud services that all modern societies depend upon, Ars hosted a live discussion. The dual use that I refer to is the utilization of compute functions within these data centers—within cloud services—by militaries, for example, to run battlefield management systems, to do their HR, to do their resource management.
