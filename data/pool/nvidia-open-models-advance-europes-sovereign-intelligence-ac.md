# NVIDIA Open Models Advance Europe’s Sovereign Intelligence Across Industries

NVIDIA is expanding its family of open models across language, robotics, biology, and quantum computing, giving developers, researchers and enterprises what they need to build applications tailored to local languages and industries The portfolio includes the new NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and Nemotron 3.5 Bolt models; new NVIDIA Nemotron Retriever models for embedding, reranking and document parsing; the new NVIDIA Kumo family open weight foundation models and libraries for structured business data; NVIDIA Cosmos 3.5 world models; NVIDIA Alpamayo 2 Edge for autonomous vehicles; NVIDIA BioNeMo for healthcare and scientific discovery; and NVIDIA Ising for quantum computing. The open models give organizations a foundation they can customize with local data and deploy on infrastructure they control. Nemotron models are built with data, research and expertise from the NVIDIA Nemotron Coalition , including European partners Black Forest Labs , H-Company and Mistral .
