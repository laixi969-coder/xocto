# Some Observations About Anthropic’s IPO Filing

In an S-1 filing today preceding what could be the largest IPO ever , Anthropic laid out extensive new details about its earnings, growth, key stakeholders and the risks it faces going forward. Clocking in at 335 pages, the document wasn’t exactly light reading. However, after a preliminary once-over, we highlight a few of the more intriguing disclosures below: Revenue growth is unusually rapid For the first half of this year, Anthropic reported revenue of $16.3 billion. That’s up 14x from the same period in 2025. Things aren’t slowing either. While the S-1 doesn’t forecast the second half of this year, Reuters has reported that Anthropic could reach a $110 billion annualized revenue run rate by year-end, citing analysts who met with company executives.
