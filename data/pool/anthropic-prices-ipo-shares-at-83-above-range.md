# Anthropic Prices IPO Shares At $83, Above Range

Anthropic priced shares for its upcoming stock market debut at $83 on Wednesday afternoon, solidly above the expected range of $70 to $78 laid out in its IPO filing, CNBC, Reuters and the Wall Street Journal reported, citing sources familiar with the matter. The offering promises to be the public market event of the season, with the Claude creator seeing investor demand for shares well in excess of its capital-raising goals. The stock is slated to begin trading Thursday on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT. For the IPO portion of its fundraise, Anthropic plans to sell just over 800 million shares, bringing in about $66.4 billion. Anthropic also expects to pull in another $26 billion from a private placement slated to close immediately following the IPO. Investors in this include AMD , Broadcom , Google , Micron Technology , Microsoft , Nvidia and TSMC . Put together, the IPO and private placement total more than $92 billion. It’s a record-setting capital haul for a company making its market debut. SpaceX , by comparison, raised $75 billion in its June offering.
