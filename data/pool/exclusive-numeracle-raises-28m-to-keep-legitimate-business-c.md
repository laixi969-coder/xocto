# Exclusive: Numeracle Raises $28M To Keep Legitimate Business Calls From Spam Label

Numeracle has raised $28 million from Edison Partners to help businesses whose calls are incorrectly labeled as spam or blocked by phone carriers, it tells Crunchbase News exclusively. The McLean, Virginia-based company verifies businesses and links their identities to the phone numbers they use to contact customers. It shares that information with the systems carriers use to screen calls and works to remove incorrect labels. Rebekah Johnson, founder and CEO of Numericle. (Courtesy photo) The financing brings Numeracle’s total funding to $34.8 million since its 2018 inception. That total includes a $6.8 million Series A raised in December 2023, founder and CEO Rebekah Johnson told Crunchbase News in an interview. Edison was the sole investor in the new round. Numeracle declined to disclose its valuation. The company says it has roughly 4,000 business customers and about 85 employees. It became profitable this year, sooner than Johnson had expected. Numeracle plans to use its new capital to hire and expand internationally. The company also plans to adapt its business verification tools for use beyond phone calls. For example, Johnson said the company is eyeing messaging, chat apps, marketplaces and AI agents. Its expansion comes as businesses simultaneously face two related problems.
