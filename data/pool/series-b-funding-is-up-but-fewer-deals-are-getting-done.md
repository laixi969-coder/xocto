# Series B Funding Is Up, But Fewer Deals Are Getting Done

More money, fewer rounds. That, in short, is the state of U.S. Series B startup funding in 2026, as venture capital investment continues to concentrate heavily around a handful of AI hotshots. Crunchbase data shows that so far this year, investors have poured around $48 billion into financings around the Series B stage 1 , the highest tally in years. Yet round counts are below year-ago levels and are on track for a multiyear low. Smaller Series B rounds grow scarcer One trend driving capital concentration is a sharp decline in small and mid-sized Series B rounds. So far this year, less than a third of deals in our sample set 2 were for under $30 million. By contrast, between 2021 and 2025, rounds in this range accounted for about half of deals, per Crunchbase data. The smallest Series B deals have gotten particularly scarce. This year there were just 26 rounds under $10 million in our sample set. Big rounds still a big thing On the flip side, really large Series B rounds are quite popular.
