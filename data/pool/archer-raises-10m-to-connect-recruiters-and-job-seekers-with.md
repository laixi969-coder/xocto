# Archer raises $10M to connect recruiters and job seekers with AI

Archer (formerly hackajob) has raised $10 million in new funding in a round led by Volition Capital , with participation from existing investor AVP . The company develops an AI-powered recruiting platform designed to connect employers with suitable candidates while helping job seekers discover relevant opportunities. Founded by Mark Chaffey and Razvan Creanga at King’s College London, hackajob initially operated as a reverse hiring marketplace where companies applied to software engineers. The platform grew to one million users before the company began developing Archer in 2025, adapting its approach to the growing use of AI in recruitment. Developed in collaboration with customer Sainsbury’s and publicly launched in September, Archer uses AI to automate candidate sourcing, qualification and introductions. Unlike recruitment tools focused primarily on employers or job seekers, the platform serves both sides, recommending candidates to recruiters and relevant positions to applicants. The growing use of AI in recruitment is changing the dynamics of the hiring process, creating new challenges for both employers and job seekers. As AI tools simplify job applications, application volumes have tripled, while the average likelihood of securing an interview has declined from approximately 15 per cent to 2 per cent.
