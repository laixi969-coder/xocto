# ‘Hogwarts Legacy’ Gets a Magical NVIDIA DLSS 5 Upgrade on GeForce NOW

Hogwarts Legacy receives an NVIDIA DLSS 5 upgrade with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering today, bringing lifelike lighting and materials to the wizarding world on GeForce NOW . Blizzard Entertainment’s World of Warcraft: Forever also opens a new chapter in Azeroth, with a launch-week access reward available for GeForce NOW members. Meanwhile, Ubisoft brings fresh challenges with expansions for Tom Clancy’s The Division 2 and Anno 117: Pax Romana . It’s all part of a packed month of play, with two-dozen games joining the GeForce NOW library throughout November, including eight this week. Hogwarts in a New Light NVIDIA DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering arrives in Hogwarts Legacy today, giving members a new reason to return to Hogwarts, streaming from Steam, Epic Games and Xbox with Play Anywhere support. Published by Warner Bros. Games under the Portkey Games label and developed by Avalanche Software, Hogwarts Legacy is an immersive, open-world action role-playing game set in the 1800s wizarding world. Begin as a fifth-year student, encounter magical creatures, brew potions and learn spells while exploring both recognizable and unfamiliar places.
