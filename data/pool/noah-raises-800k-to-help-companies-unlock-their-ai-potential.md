# Noah raises €800K to help companies unlock their AI potential

Antwerp-based startup Noah , which develops an AI-powered platform to help companies identify and prioritise opportunities for artificial intelligence adoption, has raised €800,000 from a group of business angels. Investors include Rudi Deruytter, former CEO of B-Bank, entrepreneur Ben Schrauwen and Nicolas Debray of Belgium Startup Ecosystem. The financing is supplemented by subsidies and a bank loan. Founded by Denis Leysen (CEO), César Van Leuffelen (CPO) and Tom De Smedt (CTO), Noah helps businesses assess where AI can deliver value by analysing internal processes, identifying potential use cases and estimating their financial impact. While companies increasingly recognise the potential of AI, many struggle to identify where to begin or how to measure its benefits. According to Denis Leysen, CEO and co-founder of Noah, traditional approaches such as hiring consultants or assigning internal teams can be costly and time-consuming, creating demand for faster ways to evaluate AI opportunities. Noah addresses this challenge through an AI interviewer that conducts 20-minute conversations with employees in multiple languages to identify opportunities for automation and process improvements. According to the company, the platform can complete up to 100 interviews within 24 hours, compared with the month a consultant would typically need to conduct 20 interviews.
