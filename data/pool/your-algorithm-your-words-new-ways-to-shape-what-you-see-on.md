# Your Algorithm, Your Words: New Ways to Shape What You See on Facebook

Facebook is at its best when it helps you find and do more of what you’re actually into. Now we’re putting you in the driver’s seat. New AI-powered tools let you tell Facebook, in your own words, what you want to see in Feed and Reels, and your recommendations respond right away. You can now fine-tune your experience and interact directly with the algorithms that rank and recommend what you see on Facebook, meaning you’ll see recommendations that are more relevant, more nuanced, and more you. We’ve updated the three-dot menu on every post to make it easier to access these new customization tools. Talk to Your Algorithm, Fine-Tune What You See We’re rolling out Your algorithm, where you can directly add and manage the topics you want to see in Feed and Reels. Think of it as a dedicated space to tell us exactly what you’re into. You can find it in the three-dot menu.
