# NVIDIA Announces New Planned Investments in Korean Startups at the MSIT-NVIDIA AI Startup Partnership Forum

Korea’s Ministry of Science and ICT and NVIDIA hosted the MSIT -NVIDIA AI Startup Partnership Forum at COEX. Ryu Je-myung, vice minister of science and ICT , joined NVIDIA leaders to meet with over 20 Korean startups across foundation models, physical AI, AI infrastructure and AI semiconductors. NVIDIA also announced an investment in Korean startup Point2 , with additional investments planned in Motif Technologies , RLWRLD and Upstage. Point2 Technology, an AI interconnect company, builds scalable, radio frequency-based interconnects that move data between chips and racks in AI factories, offering an innovative alternative to conventional copper and optical interconnect solutions for AI cluster scale-up, delivering the reach, performance and efficiency needed for next-generation AI infrastructure. Motif Technologies , a Seoul-based AI lab, develops foundation models for building AI agents that carry out complex tasks, including Motif-3, a large language model released with open weights. RLWRLD, a physical AI startup based in San Francisco, Seoul and Tokyo, develops robotics foundation models that enable humanoid and industrial robots to perform complex real-world tasks that require five-finger dexterity, such as precisely inserting components, threading nuts onto bolts and kitting parts from moving conveyors. Upstage , a Korean enterprise AI company, develops Solar language
