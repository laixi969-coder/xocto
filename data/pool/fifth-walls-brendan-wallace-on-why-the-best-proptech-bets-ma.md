# Fifth Wall’s Brendan Wallace On Why The Best Proptech Bets May Not Look Like Proptech

Brendan Wallace has spent much of the past decade investing in technology that impacts how real estate is bought, built and managed. Now, the Fifth Wall co-founder sees AI changing the kinds of companies worth backing and expanding the definition of proptech itself. Founded in 2016, Los Angeles-based Fifth Wall has grown into one of the largest investment firms focused on real estate technology, with roughly $3 billion in commitments and capital under management. Its portfolio includes Procore , OpenDoor , Blend and Hippo . Notable exits include ServiceTitan , which went public in 2024, and flexible workspace provider Industrious , which real estate giant CBRE took full ownership of in January 2025 at a valuation of more than $800 million. Major real estate and hospitality companies — including CBRE, Hines , Hilton and Marriott — are among its investors, giving the startups it backs a direct connection to potential customers. Wallace, who serves as CEO and chief investment officer, brings experience on both sides of that relationship. He worked in real estate finance at Goldman Sachs and Blackstone before co-founding workforce analytics startup Identified , which was acquired by Workday in 2014, and ridesharing company Cabify .
