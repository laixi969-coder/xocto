# How Stretto built an AI voice agent with Amazon Nova 2 Sonic

This post is co-written with Danya Gordin from Stretto and Marcelo Wzorek from CI&T. Before Stretto built an AI voice agent, consumers considering bankruptcy on its Bankruptcy.com site filled out a web form with a few fields: debt amount, attorney status, and contact information. These form-generated leads carried thin margins. The gap between a web form lead and a fully qualified lead is significant. Bankruptcy attorneys place more value on a lead that includes detailed financial data: debts, assets, vehicles, real estate, employment history, and prior filings. The web form could have been extended to capture that detail, but the longer it got, the more visitors abandoned it partway through. A phone interview would have drawn the information out naturally, because people are more willing to share financially sensitive details in conversation, but staffing and training a human intake team to run those interviews was cost-prohibitive. Stretto saw an opportunity: replace the web form with an AI voice agent that conducts the intake interview by phone. The agent would guide callers through the full bankruptcy questionnaire conversationally and extract structured data in real time.
