# Introducing Direct: An App Just for Messaging

Today, we’re introducing Direct, a new messaging app brought to you by Instagram for chatting one-on-one or in groups with friends and family. People rely on messaging to stay in touch with friends and family, and we built Direct to provide a dedicated space for these close connections. Direct has creative, customizable messaging tools made to make chats unique to you, including: Draw: Draw and sketch directly inside your chat threads. Notes: Catch quick updates from friends right at the top of your inbox. Stickers: Create custom stickers to express yourself and share with friends and family. Voice Effects: Add fun audio effects like crowd laughter or underwater sounds to your replies. Themes: Personalize your chat backgrounds with custom visual themes. How to Access Direct Direct is now testing in Türkiye and Thailand, and is available with or without an Instagram account. You can sign up for Direct by using your email or phone number. If you have an existing Instagram account, you can log in to Direct with the same username and password, and your existing chats will sync across both apps. If you use Direct without an Instagram account, only your contacts can start a chat with you
