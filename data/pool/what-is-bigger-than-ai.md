# 📈 What is bigger than AI?

In August 2024, we said GLP-1s “will be the most impactful technology in the US over the next two years, with more meaningful and measurable impacts than either artificial intelligence or renewable technologies.” Later, in October that year, we estimated its impact in the medical field alone could affect 20% of US adults, with another 6-10% using them for lifestyle reasons; in the final scenario we laid out, up to 60-70% of US adults, targeting dopamine addictions and desire regulation. That year, 3% of US adults took a GLP-1 for weight loss; that’s now up to 11% . More than twice as many Americans pay for GLP-1s as pay for AI (via ). Subscribe now Bigger than the bots By the end of 2025, GLP-1 sales exceeded generative AI revenue, reaching $76 billion, versus our $63 billion for generative AI. Revenue growth suggests this may be the crossover year. The cost collapse Semaglutide – the second-best-selling drug in the US last year – had a listed cost of $1,349 per month’s supply in the US on first release in June 2021. That hasn’t budged since. The cash price outside of insurance has dropped; Novo’s self-pay price came in at $499/month
