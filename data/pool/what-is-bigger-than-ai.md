# GLP-1 market

In August 2024, we said GLP-1s “will be the most impactful technology in the US over the next two years, with more meaningful and measurable impacts than either artificial intelligence or renewable technologies.” Later, in October that year, we estimated its impact in the medical field alone could affect 20% of US adults, with another 6-10% using them for lifestyle reasons; in the final scenario we laid out, up to 60-70% of US adults, targeting dopamine addictions and desire regulation. That year, 3% of US adults took a GLP-1 for weight loss; that’s now up to 11% . More than twice as many Americans pay for GLP-1s as pay for AI (via ).
