# Your complete guide to AWS Quick at AWS re:Invent 2026

AWS Quick is the AI workspace built only for work, where the dashboards, agents, and automations one person creates are available to everyone on the team. Built on AWS with enterprise security and governance from the start, Quick connects to the data and applications you use every day. At AWS re:Invent 2026 (November 30 to December 4), customers including Allianz, Cox Automotive, Mondelez, DXC, and Southwest Airlines, join AWS leaders on stage to share how they govern, extend and scale Quick across their teams. Whether you’re a business or technology leader, a builder, or part of an IT, security, data, or analytics team, use this guide to find the sessions that fit your goals. Explore the session catalog and learn more . Keynote Join AWS CEO Matt Garman for the re:Invent 2026 CEO Keynote on Tuesday from 8:30 AM – 10:30 AM PT as he shares the latest innovations powering the agentic era. From AI infrastructure and inference to data, agent platforms, and the software agents AWS is building, see how AWS gives you the depth, breadth, and integration to build what’s next with new announcements and stories from customers already doing it.
