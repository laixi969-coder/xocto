# How Octave built a secure multi-tenant AI foundation on Amazon Bedrock

In quality management for regulated industries, the institutional memory that resolves a problem is almost always buried somewhere in a customer’s history. A similar defect investigated last year, a Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) that fixed the root cause, an audit finding that flagged the same gap. That knowledge sits in billions of documents no human can search by semantic similarity across languages and formats, so every investigation starts from scratch. Octave Reliance has helped each organization collect documents across multiple systems to help them search more easily and keep track of everything they have. The Octave Reliance team saw unlocking this history as its single largest opportunity to help customers work faster and stay compliant. The obstacle was equally clear: complaint records, audit findings, and corrective actions carry the platform’s strictest isolation requirements, and those were exactly the records the AI would need to read. In a platform serving over 14,000 customers across medical devices, pharmaceuticals, and food manufacturing, a single inadvertent disclosure from one customer to another would carry regulatory consequences far outweighing any AI benefit.
