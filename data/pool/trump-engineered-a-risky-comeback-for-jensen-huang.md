# Trump engineered a risky comeback for Jensen Huang

After Nvidia's board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation as CEO , following reports that Huang had an affair with an alleged Chinese spy, President Donald Trump, too, appeared to submit to the new reality. Appearing on Stephen A. Smith's radio show shortly after the news came out, Trump said that Huang would "end up on his feet." "You know the expression, anybody can be replaced," Trump said, floating Apple's ex-CEO Tim Cook as a potential CEO candidate. It didn't take long for Trump to begin softening his approach, making it clear he'd welcome Huang back into the role of CEO even if Nvidia's own board wasn't ready to do so.
