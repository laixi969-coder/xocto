# Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO, board chair

Nvidia's board of directors has voted to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO and chairman of the board, just 11 days after he resigned in the wake of multiple scandals . The reinstatement, announced Monday morning , is "effective immediately" and was based in part on "input from senior levels of the United States government" that assured the board members that "Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk." That assurance comes despite Huang's admitted affair with a woman who, US intelligence sources believe, was working as a Chinese spy and allegations that Nvidia had subverted export controls meant to keep Nvidia chips from reaching China. It also comes despite multiple developing investigations from multiple different government departments into those and other alleged national security, accounting, and corporate governance failures.
