# Barcelona-based Enzo Ventures closes €20M Fund II backing Southern European founders

Barcelona-based Enzo Ventures has closed its second fund, a €20m fund which is backing Southern European founders at the pre-seed stage. Fund II follows Enzo’s strategy it began in 2023, when it invested in its first fund, which backed Southern European founders and diaspora entrepreneurs at the very start of their journey. Enzo says Fund II has been designed around a simple principle: keep doing what worked in Fund I, a €10m fund, but with more capital and a stronger platform behind it. Enzo defines diaspora entrepreneurs as those founders leaving cities like Barcelona and Lisbon to build companies in cities like London and Paris, while maintaining deep ties to the talent networks and entrepreneurial communities they came from. The firm is looking to identify these founders before the broader VC market. The new fund is deploying cheques of between €250,000 and €600,000. The new fund has already invested in three startups: London-based Cori Clinical and investments in startups in Madrid and Zurich. Enzo’s portfolio includes Orbio and Murphy , which Enzo says are now generating millions of euros in revenue.
