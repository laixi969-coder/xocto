# Solvera raises $6.3M to scale the AI-native engineering firm

Oslo-based Solvera has raised $6.3 million in a pre-seed funding round to develop its AI-native engineering firm for the construction industry. The round was led by Seedcamp , with participation from Construct Venture, Inception Fund, Momentum, Obos Ventures, PSV Tech, Sondo, and The Nordic Web Ventures, alongside angel investors including Spacemaker founders Anders Kvåle and Håvard Haukeland. Founded by Arif Mirza (CEO), Pål Rasmussen (CTO), and Thorvald Thorsnes (CCO), Solvera is building an engineering firm that combines AI technology with the expertise of experienced building engineers. Rather than offering software licences or charging for consulting hours, the company provides end-to-end engineering services, taking projects from initial requirements and constraints through to coordinated, construction-ready designs. At the centre of its approach is a purpose-built operating system that uses AI agents to automate an increasing share of engineering tasks, while human engineers remain responsible for judgement and key decisions. The system is designed to improve with each project by capturing engineering knowledge and making it reusable across future work. Solvera is initially focusing on mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) engineering, a field that involves managing complex requirements, coordinating multiple disciplines and adapting designs throughout the construction process.
