# Announcing Amazon Textract Insights: Verifiable generative AI for documents

Organizations across financial services, insurance, healthcare, and the public sector depend on document processing. They turn scanned files and paper forms into structured data their systems can act on. As generative AI has matured, these organizations want more than accurate text extraction. They want to describe the information they need in their own words and get dependable, auditable answers from complex documents. Today, we are announcing the general availability of Amazon Textract Insights , a new generative AI feature of Amazon Textract available through a new API within Amazon Textract. With Amazon Textract Insights, you describe the fields you want in natural language and submit a document or a mixed, multi-document packet. You receive structured results in which each value carries a confidence score and page-level grounding, with no model training required. The challenge of extracting data from mixed packets Many of the high-value document workloads involve diverse, mixed packets rather than clean, single-purpose files. A mortgage file, for example, typically arrives as a single scan holding a W-2, payslips, bank statements, and a page of handwritten notes. Each has its own layout and appears in no fixed order.
