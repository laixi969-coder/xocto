# Accelerating product development with AI-driven lifecycle management (AI-PDLC) in AWS Quick

This blog post focuses on how AI can improve and expedite the product development life cycle using agents built on AWS Quick, with human approval baked into the process. AI-driven software delivery is helping teams ship products faster than ever. As implementation accelerates, the upstream product development process becomes even more important. Coding agents can turn clear requirements into working software faster than before, but they can’t compensate for building the wrong product. With implementation becoming easier, your competitive advantage moves upstream to four critical activities: discovering the right customer problem, deciding which opportunity to pursue, designing the right experience, and validating it through a prototype. The signals that you need to make those decisions are scattered everywhere. Your customers leave feedback in Slack, your sales team logs insights in Salesforce, systems record incidents in ServiceNow, stakeholders document requirements in Microsoft SharePoint, engineers track work items in Jira, and teams make decisions in Outlook meetings and email threads. As your teams move between these tools, they disconnect context from the decisions it should inform, and lose more at every handoff.
