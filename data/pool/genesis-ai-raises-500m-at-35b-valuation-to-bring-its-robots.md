# Genesis AI Raises $500M At $3.5B Valuation To Bring Its Robots To Factories And Warehouses

Genesis AI , a startup teaching robots to perform physical tasks by learning from human movements, announced Tuesday it has raised $500 million in Series A funding at a $3.5 billion valuation. Premji Invest , Khosla Ventures and Eclipse co-led the round, which brings the company’s disclosed funding to $605 million since its 2024 inception. Genesis previously raised $105 million in a round announced in July 2025. The company is developing both robots and the AI software that controls them. Its approach combines recordings of human hand movements with training in simulated environments, where robots can practice skills before attempting them in the physical world. Genesis is initially targeting manufacturing, logistics and laboratories with its robot, Eno. Zhou Xian and Theophile Gervet, co-founders of Genesis AI. (Courtesy photo) “We just want to bring something more elegant, more timeless,” co-founder and CEO Zhou Xian told Crunchbase News in an interview. “In the end, this is a very full-stack company. … We have to build all different pieces across the entire stack to make sure we can tightly integrate our technology stack together, and hopefully build the most well-polished and reliable product. Other participants in the new financing include T. Rowe Price
