# Optimize AI economics on Amazon Bedrock with Accenture’s sequenced approach

As enterprises scale AI on Amazon Bedrock , token spend often grows even as per-token prices fall. Without cost allocation tags in place, your cloud invoice shows AI spend as a single line item rather than by team, workload, agent, or model. Cheaper tokens expand adoption, and agentic workloads multiply invocations. As successful AI use cases scale, consumption can rise faster than unit prices drop. The economic question is whether the business value generated grows faster than the cost of the intelligence required to produce it. The Linux Foundation has launched the Tokenomics Foundation to develop open standards for AI cost economics, a sign that managing the economics of AI is now an industry discipline. In this post, you will learn about six levers for optimizing token spend, how Accenture applied them in production, and how to apply them using Amazon Bedrock. Accenture applied these levers in four client engagements across telecom, insurance, mining, and a large enterprise deployment. The same levers are available natively on Amazon Bedrock as described in this post. Where savings were measured, token spend fell by about 40–85 percent. Results are specific to each client’s workload and may vary. Client names are withheld by Accenture
