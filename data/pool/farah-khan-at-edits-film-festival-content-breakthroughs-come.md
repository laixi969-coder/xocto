# Farah Khan at Edits Film Festival: “Content breakthroughs come from spontaneity, relatability and realness”

Today the curtains came down on the Edits Film Festival in Mumbai, in the presence of filmmaker Farah Khan, festival judges Ahsaas Channa, Dolly Singh, Nikhil Taneja and Rajeev Masand and over 100 content creators in the audience. The festival awarded its winners – Ishank Gupta ( @filmmaker.ishank ), Sanya Rohillaa ( @bunniestbunnytoeverbunnny ) and Ahsan Vazir ( @ahsan_vazir__ ) for their great storytelling and use of Edits for editing their content. Top Submissions from the festival Submissions for the Edits Film Festival commenced in August, where in a duration of 45 days, you were encouraged to submit a piece of content made from unpolished videos in your camera roll, and edited using Edits, Instagram’s video editing app. While the Official Selection played at the festival demonstrated this, the top three in particular, selected by the jury, displayed a rare sense of adventure, restraint and skill. Creators of the top submissions of the Edits Film Festival, with the jury members. The top three submissions, by Ishank Gupta ( @filmmaker.ishank ), Sanya Rohillaa ( @bunniestbunnytoeverbunnny ) and Ahsan Vazir ( @ahsan_vazir__ ), stood out for diverse storytelling, and use of Edits features in them.
