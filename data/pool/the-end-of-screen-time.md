# 🔮 The end of screen time

I live in Amsterdam, and I travel to London every couple of months for some quality in-person time with the EV crew. When I arrived at my hotel on Monday, I realized that I had left my laptop at home. A year ago, I would’ve felt completely lost without my laptop. If you do office work, you’ll probably relate. For decades, office work has revolved around computers: Slack, Word docs, Excel models, all created with the idea that I’ll be sitting at my desk, typing. That assumption is one of the fictions we built our lives around. This week, without my laptop around, I found that fiction loosening, and now I believe it will eventually disappear because of how I use AI agents. I did most of my work this week by chatting to ChatGPT on my phone. So I’m not replacing desk work with squinting at tiny text or craning my neck over a screen, but with talking and having the work talked back to me.
