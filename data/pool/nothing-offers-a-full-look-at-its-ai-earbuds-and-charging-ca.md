# Nothing offers a full look at its AI earbuds and charging case

Nothing is showing off a much more detailed look at its upcoming AI earbuds ahead of their December 8th release. The Ear Note buds can record your calls, while their charging case can record, transcribe, and summarize your conversations. Like the Ear 3 , the Ear Note's microphone-equipped case comes with a "Talk" button that you can use to record your own voice. There's also a new "Record" button that can record and transcribe your conversations. In a post on X , Nothing CEO Carl Pei says the button will allow you to record calls through the earbuds, and meetings through the case as a "first step toward a hardware context layer for AI."
