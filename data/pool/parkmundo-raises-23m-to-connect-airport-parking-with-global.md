# ParkMundo raises €2.3M to connect airport parking with global travel platforms

Travel technology company ParkMundo has raised €2.3 million in seed funding to expand its airport parking marketplace and B2B distribution platform. The investment marks the company's first external funding after more than a decade of bootstrapped growth. The round was backed by a group of angel investors with experience in the technology and travel sectors, alongside additional financing from Rabobank. The funding follows a rebuild of ParkMundo's technology infrastructure, a rebrand and the development of its B2B distribution capabilities. Founded in 2013 by Jeroen van den Eerenbeemt as Vliegen en Parkeren, the company was joined shortly afterwards by Martijn Meeuws , now co-founder and CEO. Initially operating as an airport parking comparison website, ParkMundo has since expanded into an international travel technology platform. Before this investment, ParkMundo had been self-funded and profitable. Today, the company operates across four continents and has facilitated millions of airport parking bookings through a network of more than 850 partners in 15 countries. The platform connects travellers with airport parking providers in a market that remains fragmented across local operators and booking systems.
