# 19 charts that explain the state of AI

Anthropic is scheduled to go public on Thursday in a transaction that will value the AI giant at more than a trillion dollars. OpenAI is expected to follow suit in the new year. Some people question whether these two companies could possibly be worth so much, but they just had nine of the greatest months of revenue growth in business history. It won’t be all smooth scaling, however. Earlier this month, Democrats won both houses of Congress in an election where many candidates ran in opposition to data centers. When the new Democratic majorities take power in January, they are expected to put AI regulation near the top of their agenda. AI is already having a big impact in disciplines like math, physics, and cybersecurity. Many people expect the same forces to reshape other disciplines in the coming months — and this might mean job losses across the economy.
