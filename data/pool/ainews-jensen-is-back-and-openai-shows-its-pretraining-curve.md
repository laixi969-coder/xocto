# [AINews] Jensen Is Back! and OpenAI Shows Its Pretraining Curve, joined by Microsoft

Congrats to Sierra on tripling last year’s $10B valuation , and to Z.ai for releasing GLM 5.4 , their first looped transformer following Thinking Machine’s much smaller Inkling Mini 2 from last week. However all these are outshone by today’s twin title stories… Jensen Huang is back . Eleven days after announcing his resignation , Nvidia announced his return as CEO, president and member of the Board of Directors. View post on X ↗ The board voted unanimously after more meetings with Jensen on Sunday and said it received input from senior U.S. officials (aka the Treasury Secretary and the President dialing into the board meeting ). Colette Kress remains CFO, the independent directors’ investigation continues, and Jensen’s Senate hearing is still due in 8 days. Nvidia also formed a board committee for national security and export control. As Matt Levine points out , with the firing AND the rehiring by this board with involvement from the government, the investigators are now being put in a position of having had the outcome somewhat predetermined for them, because both have demonstrated that they did not need to wait for any further evidence to come to light.
