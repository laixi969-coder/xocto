# How U.S. Hunger connects leaders and data using Amazon Quick

For U.S. Hunger, feeding families takes more than delivering food to them. It takes understanding the people behind each request, the pressures they face, and the business that makes the mission possible. That means connecting information across the organization. Its mission is to feed families today and unite them to a healthier tomorrow. U.S. Hunger reports delivering more than 250 million meals. Its work has reached 59 countries historically and is now concentrated primarily in the United States. The organization reports approximately 500 percent growth over four years, compared with only 15–20 percent headcount growth. For a lean team at that scale, every hour and every decision matters. With Amazon Quick , U.S. Hunger can explore organizational data directly, colleagues can work from shared definitions, and leaders can examine financial information alongside the circumstances families report. U.S. Hunger’s experience shows how those capabilities fit into the daily work of a lean nonprofit.
