# Researchers Are Using Meta’s Open-Weight AI Models to Transform Medicine

Open science is critical to advancing medical research. To accelerate this progress, we’ve expanded our academic partnerships , giving researchers funding and access to our AI models like Muse Glimmer, SAM3, DINOv3, and Brain2Qwertyv2. These researchers are independently adapting our models for their own investigational research. Here are a few of the projects that are benefitting from our partnership: Training Doctors in Virtual Reality At Louisiana State University, Dr. Peter DeBlieux and his team are using Muse Glimmer, SAM 3, DINOv3, and other models from Meta, along with Meta Quest 3 headsets to develop COPES, an experimental coaching platform that supports healthcare professionals with medical procedural training in virtual reality. The system will aim to provide adaptive guidance, real-time performance assessment, and personalized feedback across technical execution, patient communication, clinical reasoning, and decision-making.
