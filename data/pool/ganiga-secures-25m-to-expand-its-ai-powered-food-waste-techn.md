# Ganiga secures €2.5M to expand its AI-powered food waste technology

Italian deeptech startup Ganiga has raised €1.5 million in an equity funding round to further develop its AI-powered waste management technology and support commercial expansion. The round was led by Italian contract catering company Vivenda, with participation from London-based NextSTEP and three angel investors. Together with €1 million secured through Smart&Start Italia, an incentive programme managed by Invitalia, Ganiga's total funding has reached €2.5 million. Founded in the province of Pisa by Nicolas Zeoli , Ganiga develops technologies combining artificial intelligence, computer vision and robotics to improve waste management and reduce food waste. The company initially focused on robotic waste sorting, developing devices that identify and separate different types of waste. It has sold more than 300 devices for use in airports, shopping centres and large businesses across Italy, Japan, Australia and other European countries. Building on data collected through these deployments, Ganiga expanded into food waste management with HooolyFood, an AI-powered system designed to help catering operators monitor and reduce waste. Using cameras installed above waste containers, the system identifies discarded food without requiring weighing and provides data to help operators identify inefficiencies, improve purchasing and menu planning, and manage costs.
