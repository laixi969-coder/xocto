# Seedance 2.5 Is Now Available on Cinelyo

Seedance 2.5 is now available on [Cinelyo]( https://cinelyo.com), giving creators a practical way to generate short cinematic videos from text prompts, images, and multimedia references.

Cinelyo is a browser-based AI video generation platform designed for creative experimentation, product visuals, social media content, film previsualization, and short-form video production.

## What Can You Do with Seedance 2.5?

Seedance 2.5 supports video generation for clips from 4 to 30 seconds, with output options including 480p and 720p.

You can use it for:

- Text-to-video generation
- Image-to-video animation
- Product advertisements
- Fashion and beauty concepts
- Film and storyboard previsualization
- Social media video ideas
- Character and environment motion tests
- Architectural and interior design visuals
- Educational and explainer content

The longer output duration is useful for creating continuous scenes instead of limiting every idea to a very short clip.

## Text-to-Video

You can describe a complete shot using natural language, including:

- Subject and environment
- Main action
- Camera movement
- Lighting
- Visual style
- Sound and atmosphere

For example:

> A silver electric coupe drives slowly through a rain-soaked city at night. Neon reflections move across the bodywork while the camera tracks from a low front angle into a wide hero shot. Cinematic commercial lighting, realistic reflections, subtle rain ambience.

Cinelyo transforms this type of shot description into a generated video that can be reviewed and refined.

## Image-to-Video

If you already have a product image, character design, storyboard frame, or architectural visualization, you can upload it as a starting point and describe the movement you want to add.

This is useful when the composition, product shape, or visual identity needs to remain consistent while the camera and subject begin to move.

## Multi-Reference Generation

Cinelyo also supports a multimodal workflow for projects that require more creative control.

You can combine:

- Up to 9 image references
- Up to 3 video references
- Up to 3 audio references

For example:

- An image can define a character or product
- A video can provide movement or camera timing
- An audio reference can communicate rhythm or atmosphere

References can be identified in the prompt with tags such as:

@image
@video
@audio

## A Practical Workflow

A simple workflow for using Seedance 2.5 on Cinelyo is:

1. Start with one clear shot idea.
2. Write a prompt describing the subject, action, and camera.
3. Add image, video, or audio references if necessary.
4. Select Seedance 2.5.
5. Choose the duration, resolution, and aspect ratio.
6. Generate the video.
7. Review the result and refine one variable at a time.

For the first draft, it is usually better to focus on one primary action instead of asking the model to perform several complex events in a single clip.

## Why Use Cinelyo?

AI video generation works best when creators can iterate quickly.

A single generation may not be perfect, but it can reveal whether the composition, motion, camera direction, and overall visual idea are working.

Cinelyo keeps prompts, reference assets, model settings, and generated results in one browser-based workspace. This makes it easier to compare different takes and refine a visual concept.

## Use Cases

Cinelyo can be used to create:

- Product launch videos and ecommerce advertisements
- Short-form social media content
- Fashion and beauty campaign concepts
- Film storyboards and previsualization
- Character and environment motion studies
- Architecture and interior design presentations
- Brand visual concepts
- Music and event visuals
- Educational and explainer videos

## Prompt Writing Tips

For better Seedance 2.5 results, try to describe the shot as a short production plan.

Include:

- The main subject
- The environment
- One primary action
- The camera movement
- Lighting and visual style
- Important consistency requirements

For example:

> A matte-black fragrance bottle stands on a wet obsidian platform in a dark studio. A narrow rim light reveals the silhouette while the camera makes a slow clockwise orbit. Fine mist moves in the background and a soft violet reflection travels across the glass. End on a centered product hero shot. Keep the bottle geometry stable with no text, logo, hands, or watermark.

Focused prompts are usually more reliable than prompts that combine too many actions, camera movements, and visual effects in a single short clip.

## Review and Refine Your Results

AI video generation is an iterative process.

After each generation, review the most important variable first:

- Is the subject stable?
- Is the main action readable?
- Does the camera move as expected?
- Is the product or character consistent?
- Does the lighting support the intended mood?

Then change one part of the prompt and generate another version.

This approach makes it easier to understand which creative decisions improve the result.
