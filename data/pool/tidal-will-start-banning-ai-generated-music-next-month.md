# Tidal will start banning AI-generated music next month

Tidal is officially banning AI-generated music on December 9th. As a result of this change, Tidal will delete around 29 million AI-generated songs and reject an estimated 80,000 to 85,000 tracks per day, according to Tidal music lead Robert Andersen . The move comes just months after Tidal began labeling and demonetizing tracks identified as 100 percent AI-generated. In a post on X, Andersen says Tidal needed to take a more "drastic" measure, as AI music made up 43 percent of the tracks distributed to the platform in the last 90 days.
