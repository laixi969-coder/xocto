# How Duco transforms financial data automation with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

Financial institutions face a critical challenge: processing massive volumes of data with speed, accuracy, and compliance while operating under some of the world’s strictest regulatory frameworks. Duco is the agentic Operations platform for financial services, serving tier-one banks and other financial institutions. It processes 20 billion transactions monthly for 200+ clients, including 7 of the top 20 banks and 10 of the top 20 asset managers. To keep pace as data processing demands grew, Duco evolved from traditional machine learning models to the agentic AI capabilities of Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore. In this post, we share how Duco rebuilt its agentic AI stack on these services, cutting deployment time and onboarding costs while keeping each bank’s data isolated. The challenge: Scaling intelligence in a regulated environment Duco’s platform empowers teams to automate mission-critical data processes using AI and no-code models, handling everything from data extraction to downstream publication. It already used traditional machine learning models for predictive labeling and pattern recognition, helping banks consolidate and automate over 20,000 processes. However, these classical machine learning approaches had inherent limitations. They required extensive historical data for training, struggled with novel scenarios, and demanded significant customization for each new use case.
