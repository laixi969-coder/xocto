# Why Agents Love Supabase

I first met Paul Copplestone and Ant Wilson in Singapore, shortly after they finished YC as the hottest startup of YC S20 . Six years later, we finally got them on the podcast! This is their first podcast together, after taking an open-source, fully remote company from zero to a $10 billion valuation. The title is a question I wanted to understand for myself: why do agents love Supabase? There are plenty of managed databases. Postgres hosting is competitive. Being familiar to a model is useful distribution, but it doesn't explain all the work needed to turn a generated application into something that runs. But when I ran my frontier AEO tracker , it was an almost complete sweep of first choice for app backends (and in contention for managed db ) for every frontier model from Astra to Fable to Muse. At Select, Supabase grouped its announcements into Build, Operate and Scale. The things that stood out to me were surprisingly practical: start a local backend without Docker, keep the desired database schema in Git, and run compute beside the database. Each removes a piece of work that developers have been doing for years. Agents make that friction harder
