# How the UK Amazon Quick User Group grew in 2026

The Amazon Quick User Group – United Kingdom is a community of Amazon Quick users in the United Kingdom who meet quarterly to exchange knowledge, share best practices, learn through hands-on sessions, and discuss their experiences using the service. These user groups function as collaborative forums where members learn, connect, and innovate together. Whether you’re already using Amazon Quick or just getting started, we’d love for you to join the UK Amazon Quick User Group and become part of a growing community that’s redefining how organizations make decisions with data. In this post, we’re thrilled to share our 2026 yearly roundup, highlighting all three quarterly meetups that have taken place since the group’s launch and the momentum that has followed. The events drew more than 150 attendees, and the energy in the room, fueled by agentic AI and the potential of Amazon Quick, was clear. Across industries, customers are using Amazon Quick to turn data chaos into faster decisions, higher satisfaction, and scalable growth. Their work spans real-time incident management, eliminating dashboard sprawl with governed data layers, and automating most support workflows with AI.
