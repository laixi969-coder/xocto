# Less scrolling, more living: How Whimscope is turning everyday ideas into adventures

People often want to do more interesting things but struggle to decide what to do, find the energy to organise something, or commit to a big activity. Consumer app Whimscope aims to remove that struggle by helping people break out of their everyday routines through small, achievable adventures based on their available time, energy, and mood. Think of it as a combination of an activity discovery app, a bucket list, and a gentle nudge to do something different. Whimscope follows a three-stage process: Dream, Plan, Do. Users discover activities and experiences, choose something to try immediately or save for later, and record their completed adventures through photos, notes, and memories. These activities, known as whims, range from small everyday experiences to more ambitious adventures, such as trying a new hobby, visiting a local attraction, learning to surf, taking a spontaneous trip to a lighthouse, or planning a memorable day out with family. Some take just 15 minutes, while others might fill an evening or an entire weekend, and can be enjoyed alone or with others.
