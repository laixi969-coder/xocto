# LATO secures $4.7M to automate customer and expert interviews with AI

AI-powered market research platform LATO has raised $4.7 million in a seed funding round co-led by Air Street Capital and Axiom Partners . Its backers also include Y Combinator, Montis VC and Inception Fund, alongside angel investors including Paul Graham and co-founders of ElevenLabs, Vercel, Snowflake and Rogo. Founded by Tymek Staniszewski and Tien Chu , LATO develops AI agents that conduct customer and industry expert interviews to help investment firms assess businesses, markets and growth opportunities. Before investing in a company, private equity firms conduct commercial due diligence to assess its market position, customer relationships and growth potential. While financial data provides insights into revenue and retention, understanding purchasing decisions and competitive pressures often requires direct conversations with customers across different markets. LATO aims to streamline this process through AI agents that identify and interview customers and industry experts in their own languages. The platform combines insights from these conversations with published research and investors' internal data to help assess market conditions, competitive positioning and business assumptions.
