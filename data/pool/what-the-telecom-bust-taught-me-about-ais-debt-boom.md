# What The Telecom Bust Taught Me About AI’s Debt Boom

By David Spreng The financing of the AI buildout is shifting from equity to debt, and those who invested through the telecom bubble may recognize what that shift tends to mean. There is an old story in Stephen Hawking ’s “A Brief History of Time” about a scientist lecturing on the universe when a woman in the audience insists that the world rests on the back of a giant turtle. Asked what the turtle stands on, she replies that it’s “turtles all the way down.” Her response is what philosophers call infinite regress — a chain of claims in which each one rests on another of the same kind and solid ground never arrives. David Spreng is the founder, co-CEO and co-CIO of Runway Growth Capital. I think of it when I look at the AI buildout because its logic has taken the same shape. Valuations support borrowing, and borrowing builds capacity. The capacity then becomes proof that the valuations were right, so each claim stands on the one beneath it.
