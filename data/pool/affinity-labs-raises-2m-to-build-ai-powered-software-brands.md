# Affinity Labs raises $2M to build AI-powered software brands with creators

London-based startup Affinity Labs has raised $2 million in pre-seed funding to expand its portfolio of creator-led software brands and further develop its AI-powered product development technology. The round was led by Fuel Ventures , alongside Antler , and includes a £497,787 grant from Innovate UK , part of UK Research and Innovation (UKRI). The funding also attracted angel investors from Google DeepMind, Meta and Playfair VC, as well as content creator Marcello Ascani and Devran Amaratunga Karaca, CEO of creator economy company Kyra. Founded by Edoardo Ricci (CEO), Ahmed Eltom (COO) and Sunny Panchal (CTO), Affinity Labs develops software products in partnership with content creators. The company combines creators' existing audiences with its technology to launch and scale digital brands, with each product co-founded alongside a creator. While content creators have established consumer brands across sectors such as beauty, food and beverages, Affinity Labs is applying a similar model to software development. Its approach aims to help creators turn their audiences into digital businesses without having to build the underlying technology and operational infrastructure independently.
