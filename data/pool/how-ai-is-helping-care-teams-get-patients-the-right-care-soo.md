# How AI Is Helping Care Teams Get Patients the Right Care, Sooner

From pediatric research to smart hospitals, AI is helping care teams spend less time on routine tasks and more time with patients. Health systems and startups are putting it to work across healthcare, from matching patients to clinical trials to supporting radiologists, hospital teams and patients at home. Clinicians stay at the center, reviewing AI outputs and guiding decisions, while NVIDIA technologies provide the compute behind the applications. Read on to learn how healthcare teams are using AI to improve and speed patient care. Friday, Nov. 13, 5:00 a.m. PT Dyania Health Uses AI to Turn Decades of Patient Records Into Better Care Hospitals hold decades of patient history, most of it written in free text, as physicians’ notes, operative reports, imaging and pathology reads and discharge summaries. To understand one patient’s full story, a clinician would need to read through years of charts . Dyania Health , a New Jersey-based clinical AI company, built Synapsis AI to do just that. Synapsis AI is a clinical reasoning engine: It works across every note, report and lab result in a patient’s record, connecting findings that may be years apart to answer clinical questions the way a specialist reviewing the chart would.
