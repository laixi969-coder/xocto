# Scaleup Europe Fund leads $250M funding round in Pigment

French enterprise software startup Pigment has raised $250m at a $2.3bn valuation, it said today, in a funding round led by the EU's new €5bn ($5.6bn) investment fund aimed at scaling up future European tech giants. Paris-based Pigment offers enterprises a business planning platform, helping businesses plan finances, operations and forecasting. It competes against the likes of Microsoft Excel, SAP, IBM and Anaplan. Pigment’s Series E round was led by the Scaleup Europe Fund, managed by EQT, with participation from World Innovation Labs, a Palo Alto and Tokyo-headquartered VC fund. Existing investors, including Greenoaks, Iconiq, IVP, and Meritech also participated, along with investment from Nvidia, which has previously been announced. It follows Pigment’s Series D $145m funding round in 2024, led by Iconiq. Pigment's customers include big businesses like Siemens and Unilever as well as newer tech companies such as Anthropic and ElevenLabs.
