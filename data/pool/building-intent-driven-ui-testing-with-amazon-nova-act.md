# Building intent-driven UI testing with Amazon Nova Act

To scale intent-driven UI testing across its product teams, Epsilon , a data, technology, and marketing-services company and part of Publicis Groupe , turned to Amazon Nova Act . With hundreds of millions of consumer profiles and thousands of brand relationships, Epsilon needed UI testing that could keep pace with rapid application changes. The solution could not create maintenance overhead for every product team. Within Epsilon, the Agentic Platform team (50 engineers) builds shared infrastructure so other product teams can build, deploy, govern, and run AI agents safely. With this shared platform, teams get a skills-based agent builder. They also get federated identity and pre-flight authorization, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration, and cross-account provisioning. When a product team wants to adopt AI tooling, they get capabilities without additional configuration and without re-solving identity, security, browser infrastructure, or model integration on their own. One of those managed capabilities is UI testing . With Amazon Nova Act and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Browser (a capability of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore), Epsilon’s platform team could deliver resilient, intent-driven testing as a service to teams on the platform.
