# Amazon Quick 与 Amazon Bedrock AgentCore 合同智能方案

Manually extracting data from hundreds of vendor contracts doesn't scale, and RAG chat tools fall short on portfolio-wide questions. This post shares a contract intelligence platform on AWS that uses AI agents to extract and verify contract fields, then answers aggregate and single-contract questions through Amazon Quick analytics.
