# The five most important debates in AI right now

I’m excited to publish this post co-authored with one of my favorite writers, Derek Thompson. Derek left The Atlantic last year to write his own Substack , where he covers business, technology, science, and politics. Lately he’s been doing a lot of great coverage of AI; I encourage you to check it out . One year ago , when everybody was asking if AI was a bubble, Derek Thompson and I published a Thanksgiving playbook of the debate, with all the statistics, factoids, and studies that experts cited most frequently. One year later, the AI discourse has deepened and broadened. Yes, many people still obsess over the bubble question. The Jensen Huang saga at Nvidia has also uncorked a messy conversation about whether the US should sell chips to China. There is also an existential debate about whether AI is on the verge of “recursive self-improvement,” the threshold beyond which AI systems would be able to improve themselves without human intervention. The software outages of the last few weeks have exacerbated concerns about an AI cyber-pocalypse. So, this year we’re doing something similar but different.
