# Rabot Energy

Rabot Energy , a provider of dynamic electricity tariffs, has raised $50 million in a Series B funding round to expand its technology platform and support its next phase of growth. The round was led by Endeit, a growth capital investor focused on European tech scale-ups, alongside the DeepTech & Climate Fonds (DTCF). Existing investors HV Capital, High-Tech Gründerfonds (HTGF), Bosch Ventures, CNI, Acurio Ventures and vent.io also participated. Rabot Energy offers dynamic electricity tariffs that help customers reduce energy costs by automatically shifting electricity consumption to periods when power is cheaper, and renewable energy is more readily available. Its technology also enables connected home batteries to store electricity when renewable generation is high and release it when supply is lower, helping balance demand and reduce reliance on fossil fuel-based generation. As transport and heating become increasingly electrified and renewable energy generation expands, the company aims to make electricity consumption more flexible by connecting household energy devices and managing their consumption and storage. In June 2026, Rabot Energy launched Rabot Flow, combining a dynamic electricity tariff, a smart meter and a plug-and-play home battery.
