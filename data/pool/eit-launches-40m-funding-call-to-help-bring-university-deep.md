# EIT launches €40M funding call to help bring university deep tech research to market

The European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) has launched a new call to strengthen the capacity of higher education institutions to turn their research into innovations focused on the manufacturing and deep tech areas. the EIT Higher Education Initiative aims to help universities develop innovation and entrepreneurship skills, turn research into commercial products, and build stronger links with businesses. Backed by €40 million from the EIT, the 2026 funding round will offer up to €1.25 million per selected project over 18 months. Universities, businesses, research organisations, public bodies, and other partners are invited to apply together. The selected projects will help universities develop practical innovation and entrepreneurship skills among students and staff, particularly in STEM, manufacturing, and deeptech. They will also help universities turn research into commercial products, better manage intellectual property, and share expertise. With support from the EIT’s Knowledge and Innovation Communities (KICs), universities will build stronger connections with businesses, investors, and the wider startup community. Martin Kern, Director of the EIT, said: "Turning a research breakthrough into a successful business takes skills, confidence and targeted support.
