# UK healthtech startup Stride raises £4.6M to expand preventative health platform

UK-based preventative health startup Stride has raised £4.6 million in seed funding to further develop its diagnostics-driven health platform and support international expansion. The round includes investment from Arāya Ventures, Morgan Stanley Inclusive & Sustainable Ventures, Better Ventures, Innovate UK and an a16z Scout Fund investor, alongside a group of angel investors. Launched in May 2024 by Andrew Steele , an Olympic medallist and co-founder of DNAfit, Stride offers a preventative health membership that combines DNA, blood and gut health testing with personalised insights. The platform aims to help individuals better understand their health data, track changes over time and make more informed decisions about their health and wellbeing. Stride is focused on encouraging a shift from treating health problems after they emerge towards identifying potential risks and addressing them earlier. By combining diagnostic testing with personalised guidance, the company aims to make preventative health management more accessible and encourage long-term changes in everyday health behaviours.
