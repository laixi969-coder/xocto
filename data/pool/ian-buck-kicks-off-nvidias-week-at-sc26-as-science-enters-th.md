# Ian Buck Kicks Off NVIDIA’s Week at SC26 as Science Enters the Age of Agents

NVIDIA’s Ian Buck kicked off the company’s week at SC26 in Chicago on Monday with new tools for AI agents, software that can plan experiments, run code and check results on researchers’ own systems. In NVIDIA’s special address, Buck announced Nemotron for Science , the Rubin HPC GPU and an expanded Vera CPU platform for high-performance computing . He also detailed a new collaboration in quantum computing with IBM and Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory . “The frontier of computing and the new scientific stack is going to be simulation, as always, but combined with AI and the future of quantum,” said Buck, vice president of hyperscale and high-performance computing at NVIDIA. Buck opened the talk by looking back to his first public talk on CUDA, at an SC06 tutorial in Tampa, Florida, 20 years ago. NVIDIA now offers more than 1,000 CUDA-X libraries , he said. Open Models for Science Agents “[Agents] have been just a game changer in how we interface with our supercomputers, with our devices, our instruments and how we can do the research,” Buck said.
