# Lab-in-the-Loop at Compute Speed — Ian Quigley, Leash Bio

A few years ago I stumbled upon Leash Bio’s Substack , and was hooked. Be it their Kaggle competition that showed that no, we really haven’t solved protein-ligand binding , or their crazy discovery that knowing a molecule’s author is perhaps all you need to know to predict how good it is . Their substack — or at least Owl Posting ’s 1 deep dive on them — should be required reading for any scientist trying to do chemistry with AI. So I was super excited to finally be able to sit down with Ian Quigley from Leash Bio, and pick his brain. Leash specializes in DNA Encoded Libraries (aka “DELs”), a way of collecting ridiculously high-throughput interaction data between proteins and small molecules. 2 Before I discovered Leash, I had heard nothing but horror stories from DEL users. And yet, Ian and friends somehow found a way to tame the beast, extracting clean and reproducible data that is just perfect for large scale modeling.
