# The Jev Revolution: What Engineers Are Already Building with System One Models

Today at the AI Engineer Code Summit , the main theme was system one models — non-generative models that return structured choices, scores, or probabilities. They’re also typically much faster and cheaper than reasoning LLMs (a.k.a. system two models). And who better to give an inspirational rallying cry for system one models than Diogo Almeida , co-founder and CEO of TypeSafe AI . Almeida was lead creator of Jev , the product that kicked off this trend less than two months ago. Jev was an instant revelation for AI engineers when it was released in September. In a podcast with Latent Space on launch (which quickly became our most popular episode of the year), Almeida explained the core problem with making LLMs the heart of software: reliability .
