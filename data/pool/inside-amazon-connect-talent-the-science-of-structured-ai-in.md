# Inside Amazon Connect Talent: The science of structured AI interviews

For organizations hiring hundreds or thousands of people, every interview presents the same challenge: How do you give candidates a consistent opportunity to demonstrate their skills while gathering the evidence needed to make informed hiring decisions? Structured behavioral interviews offer a research-backed approach to this challenge. They use consistent, job-related questions and clear scoring criteria to give candidates an opportunity to demonstrate their skills. Decades of research in industrial-organizational (I-O) psychology have shown that structured interviews help organizations evaluate candidates and predict job performance (Schmidt & Hunter, 1998; Sackett et al., 2022). Yet delivering structured interviews at scale requires significant time and expertise to develop competencies, create q uestions, train interviewers, and evaluate responses consistently . Amazon Connect Talent brings established hiring science to AI-led interviews at scale. Connect Talent uses structured questions, adaptive follow-ups, and evidence-based scoring to help organizations gather consistent, job-related information while giving more candidates the opportunity to demonstrate their strengths. We built Connect Talent around three core design principles and evaluated how well the service applies them through a combination of internal research and independent expert review.
