# From Rohlik to enterprise AI: Tomáš Čupr's Duvo raises $32M Series A

Duvo today announced a $32 million Series A led by Balderton Capital, with participation from existing investors Index Ventures, Northzone, and Credo Ventures. The round brings Duvo's total funding to $48 million and will support its expansion, initially targeting retailers and consumer packaged goods (CPG) companies. Tomáš Čupr is a Czech serial entrepreneur, co-founder and CEO of Duvo, and founder and Group CEO of Rohlik Group , the European online grocery company valued at approximately $2 billion. He founded Rohlik in 2014 and expanded the business beyond the Czech Republic into several European markets. I spoke with Čupr to learn more. Duvo is an enterprise operating system that brings a company's processes together on one platform. Businesses can automate multiple processes using the same software connections, approval rules, and monitoring tools, rather than treating each automation as a separate project. How Rohlik's operational challenges led to Duvo Duvo emerged from the operational frustrations Čupr encountered while building online grocer Rohlik. Despite recognising the potential of AI to improve productivity, he found that introducing automation into an established business was far more complicated than expected.
