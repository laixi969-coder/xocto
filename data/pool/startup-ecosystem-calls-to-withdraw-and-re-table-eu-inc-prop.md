# Startup ecosystem calls to withdraw and re-table EU Inc proposal, Isomorphic Labs bags $5B, and inside Plural's €1B fund

This week, we tracked more than 65 tech funding deals worth over €6 billion and over 4 exits, M&A transactions, rumours , and related news stories across Europe. This week, I've gone straight from the scale and intensity of Slush in Helsinki to Yerevan, Armenia, where a much different startup ecosystem is taking shape. It's an interesting contrast. While conversations at Slush centred on scaling European tech companies, unlocking institutional capital and removing regulatory barriers, Armenia is focused on building the foundations of a globally competitive startup ecosystem where previously diaspora has been its main competitive advantage. AI is a common thread, but the conversations are markedly different — from infrastructure, commercialisation and technological sovereignty in Helsinki to the opportunities AI presents for smaller ecosystems to compete on the global stage. Despite their different stages of maturity, both face familiar challenges: retaining talent, accessing capital, commercialising technical expertise and building internationally successful companies without founders feeling compelled to relocate.
