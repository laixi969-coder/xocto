# What Frontier Labs Buy: A Data Deep Dive with Handshake AI — Garrett Lord & Team

This is our first Latent Space deep dive with an AI data company . In roughly a year, Handshake’s AI training business went from launch to $1 billion in gross annualized revenue . What are frontier labs buying at that scale? What evals are models still nowhere close to human level on? Garrett Lord brought the people who build these reinforcement learning environments to show us. For the first time, we are walking through them live! To cover the scope of Handshake, we had to produce a very special joint podcast across our Science/Engineering/Research/Finance podcast hosts, because Handshake’s business runs across all these and more. Watch for three segments after the general intro: Finance. In the finance demo, the agent has to find its assignment in a bank’s email, work out which files govern the deal, and deliver a workbook. Watch what happens to one line in the client’s email about existing goodwill. The agent carries the number into Python and subtracts it. Collin points out that the model can explain the tax treatment when you ask it directly. The mistake comes when it has to recognize that this particular line of code is where the rule applies. Bio. Nick
