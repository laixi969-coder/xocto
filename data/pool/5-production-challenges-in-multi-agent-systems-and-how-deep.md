# 5 production challenges in multi-agent systems and how Deep Insight solved them

Building a multi-agent system is now pretty straightforward. Taking it to production is where the real challenges begin. Thanks to open source frameworks and cloud services, you can set up an agent in a matter of days. A few tool calls, a few lines of prompt, and you have something that looks impressive. But the moment teams try to move beyond a pilot and into real business applications, they hit a familiar wall. If you’ve ever tried to put an agent into production, these frustrations will sound familiar: Why doesn’t the agent follow our business logic, skip validation steps, or catch edge cases? You tweak the prompt, add more examples, swap out the model. The same problems keep coming back. That’s because agents don’t follow a fixed sequence of instructions. They make their own judgments based on context. Instructions alone aren’t enough. You need to design how the agent thinks, when it verifies, and what it does when things go wrong. This is exactly why it’s become critical to go beyond prompt engineering alone and design the agent’s entire cognitive architecture and execution flow.
