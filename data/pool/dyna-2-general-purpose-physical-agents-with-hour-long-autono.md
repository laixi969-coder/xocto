# Dyna-2: General Purpose Physical Agents with Hour-Long Autonomy — Jason Ma, Dyna Robotics

Watch along AIE NYC and join us at AIE CODE in SF next month! This episode includes a live demo of the Taku robot that is best enjoyed on YouTube . Last month, Dyna made waves by announcing Dyna-2 , a world-action model pre-trained on one million hours of human video, claiming several new scaling laws on human data across four orders of magnitude . They have now followed up the model with a physical robot agent, Dyna-2.1 , that has to be seen to be believed: In his talk at AIE this year, Jason talked about how the real challenge for robotics in the real world is reliable, long-horizon autonomy , and how Dyna got its models to a 99.4% success rate folding restaurant napkins for 24 hours straight, including recovering after it pulled over the whole stack. From training a world-action model on over one million hours of human video to deploying robots that fold thousands of napkins daily at Din Tai Fung , Dyna Robotics is pushing physical AI beyond impressive demos and into real-world production.
