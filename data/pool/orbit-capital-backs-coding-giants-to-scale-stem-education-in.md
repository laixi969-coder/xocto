# Orbit Capital backs Coding Giants to scale STEM education internationally

Polish education platform Coding Giants has secured up to €6 million in venture debt financing from Orbit Capital to support its international expansion. Founded eight years ago, Coding Giants provides technology education for children and teenagers, with courses covering coding, artificial intelligence and mathematics. The company currently operates across 13 markets in Europe and the Americas and has reached more than 130,000 students. With demand for STEM education growing across markets, Coding Giants aims to bring its educational model to more children and teenagers internationally. Its approach combines technical knowledge with practical learning, encouraging students to experiment with technology while developing analytical thinking, creativity and problem-solving skills. The courses are designed to help students understand how digital tools work and how they can be used to create new solutions. Dawid Leśniewicz , co-founder of Coding Giants, said the company's goal goes beyond teaching children how to use specific tools: Coding, AI and mathematics develop logical thinking, curiosity and problem-solving skills. These are the capabilities young people need not only to use technology, but to understand it and actively participate in creating it.
