# Responsible AI for agentic AI: A phased roadmap for platform and application teams

Companies build agentic AI platforms to govern the applications, tools, skills, models, and storage that agents use. The nondeterministic nature of generative AI models introduces a complexity that previous software development practices didn’t anticipate. Consider a real estate app that generates property listings from retrieved data: does it accurately reflect the property, or hallucinate features that don’t exist? Or a customer service agent grounded on internal knowledge bases: can you verify that it doesn’t surface confidential information? Until recently, responsible AI (RAI) was model-centric, scoped to the machine learning development lifecycle: training, testing, and inference. Models didn’t reason, use tools, or take actions. When the conversation extended to model-powered systems, it covered statistically bounded situations you could test against known inputs. Generative AI powered applications and agents changed this. The systems now reason, act, compose tools dynamically, and interact over multiple steps, expanding both the scope and the depth of what responsible AI must cover. Organizations are turning Responsible AI, which includes the pillars of security and privacy, veracity and robustness, fairness, safety, controllability, explainability, transparency, and governance into a growth strategy by addressing its expanding scope early.
