# Generate images and video with vLLM-Omni on SageMaker AI – Part 2

Deploy two generative media models from one AWS vLLM-Omni Deep Learning Container on Amazon SageMaker AI. Generate an image with FLUX.2-klein through real-time inference, then animate it into video with Wan2.1-VACE through asynchronous inference, and retrieve the MP4 from Amazon S3.
