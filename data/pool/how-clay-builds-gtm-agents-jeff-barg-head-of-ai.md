# How Clay Builds GTM Agents — Jeff Barg, Head of AI

Open video on YouTube ↗ Clay wants you to get about the same results whether you use its own agent or point Claude Code at Clay. Usage is already split close to 50/50 between Clay’s agent and outside harnesses. In this episode, we chat with Jeff Barg, Head of AI at Clay, about what it takes to build go-to-market infrastructure that any agent can use. Jeff joined Clay when it acquired Avenue , the ops alerting startup he cofounded. At the time, Clay had about 50 people, and Avenue had five. AI was already a big part of how Clay thought about its product, but nobody there worked on AI. Since then, the business has grown roughly 10x, and the team is up to around 600 people. In September, Clay raised $115 million at a $7.1 billion valuation . By Jeff’s napkin math, it would cost about $1,500 to check all 40 million companies in Clay’s database against your ideal customer profile with a classifier like TypeSafe’s Jev.
