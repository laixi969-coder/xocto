# llm-gemini 0.37

Release: llm-gemini 0.37 Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen today - the lowest cost member of the Gemini 4 family. It's actually not too far off the price of its much bigger siblings, Carbon and Argon. I'm finding Gemini 4's pricing really confusing. Here's the pricing scheme: Model Dates Input ($/M) Cached input Output Gemini 4 Carbon Through Nov 30, 2026 $2.00 $0.10 $10.00 From Dec 1, 2026 $4.00 $0.20 $20.00 Gemini 4 Argon Through Nov 30, 2026 $2.00 $0.10 $10.00 From Dec 1, 2026 $4.00 $0.20 $20.00 Gemini 4 Hydrogen $1.50 $0.075 $7.50 Carbon and Argon are the same price , and both are scheduled to double in price on December 1st. If they're the same price, why have two different models? Google says that Argon "delivers Gemini 4 intelligence with faster responses and lower token spend per turn compared to Gemini 4 Carbon" - so it may be priced the same, but it will use less tokens per task. Hydrogen, supposedly the cheap model, is currently only a 25% discount on its more capable peers. The Hydrogen price will make more sense after December 1st, when it will represent a 75% discount.
