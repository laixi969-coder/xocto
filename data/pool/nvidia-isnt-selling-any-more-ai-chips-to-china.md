# Nvidia isn’t selling any more AI chips to China

Nvidia has stopped sending its advanced AI chips to China despite the approval it received from the White House to do so in December last year . The announcement was made by interim Nvidia CEO Colette Kress during an earnings call on Tuesday , marking a notable shift for the company that, under former CEO Jensen Huang, fought for the right to ship its powerful Hopper architecture H200 chips to China.
