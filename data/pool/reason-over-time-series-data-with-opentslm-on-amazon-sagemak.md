# Reason over time-series data with OpenTSLM on Amazon SageMaker JumpStart

Across industries, decision-making starts with understanding what is changing and why. Industrial organizations collect data that informs decisions across their operations. This data helps prevent production stoppages, diagnose vehicle faults, explain drops in data-center performance, and adjust pricing as supply and demand shift. However, interpreting that data still requires specialist time, and collecting more data does not mean teams can act on it. General-purpose language models like Claude or ChatGPT can help analyze data. However, processing long histories across hundreds of operational metrics as text or images can consume large numbers of tokens, which increases cost. To help organizations act on this data, Aionic Labs is developing temporal foundation models, including OpenTSLM , an open source family of time-series language models (TSLMs). These models interpret operational measurements, from equipment sensor data to supply and demand, alongside context such as maintenance records, market news, and an engineer’s observations. In doing so, TSLMs help systems recognize emerging problems in recent measurements. Experts can then ask follow-up questions to understand what changed and assess how to respond.
