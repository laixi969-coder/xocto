# [AINews] Perplexity hits a $1B revenue run rate, up 4x in 2026

Perplexity crossed a $1B annual revenue run rate today , CEO Aravind Srinivas told Semafor at a live event. The Information reported previously under $250M at the start of the year and over $750M by August, with Computer driving some of the growth. At usual agent lab multiples (eg Cognition also crossed $1B , with Cursor crossing $4.6B ), this would roughly mean that Perplexity is now worth $30-50B — if the unit economics hold up.
