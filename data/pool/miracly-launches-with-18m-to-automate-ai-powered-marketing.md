# Miracly launches with €1.8M to automate AI-powered marketing

AI marketing platform Miracly , developed by The Wild Ventures, the company behind Scandinavian home decor brand Wild Palace, has launched following the announcement of a €1.8 million pre-seed funding round. The round was led by Beenext Ventures and Lotus, alongside a Swedish family office, with participation from existing investors including Stefan Lindeberg (founder of Creandum), Alex Bouaziz (founder and CEO of Deel), Thomas Ekman (founder of 6GAI and former CEO of Axel Johnson, Dustin and Tele2), Hedda Baverud Olsson (founder and CEO of Lassie), and Parham Benisi (partner at Pophouse). With offices in Sweden and Singapore, Miracly has developed an AI-powered marketing platform designed to help businesses monitor, manage and improve their visibility across AI search and answer engines. The platform combines generative engine optimisation (GEO), marketing analytics and automated execution, allowing companies to move from identifying opportunities to implementing improvements within a single system. Miracly aims to address challenges associated with declining organic traffic from traditional search engines and rising performance marketing costs. Its platform provides marketing teams with tools to analyse how AI systems present their brands and implement strategies to improve their visibility and organic growth.
