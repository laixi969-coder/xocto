# Blackwall hits $2.2B valuation with $100M Series C just weeks after joining Estonia's unicorn club

Web traffic management platform Blackwall today announced it has raised $100 million in Series C at a valuation of $2.2 billion. Last month it was reported that Blackwall reached a €1 billion valuation and ranked fifth in TopTech 2026, the annual ranking of Estonia's 30 most valuable tech companies. AVP , who join existing investors Dawn Capital , MMC Ventures and Tera Ventures , led the round. The new round of financing accelerates Blackwall's objective to build a global traffic management ecosystem around its hosting-provider network. By connecting providers, infrastructure and technology, Blackwall aims to provide greater control over how internet traffic is managed, secured and monetised. Currently spanning bot mitigation, DDoS protection, web application firewall, load balancing, server-side encryption and management of agentic AI traffic, which is rapidly taking over the Internet, Blackwall is deployed inside the web hosting providers’ own infrastructure, identifying real visitors and legitimate automated traffic from malicious bots, attacks and traffic abuse, filtering billions of requests per day. "The web is becoming more automated, more distributed and more complex, but the infrastructure that powers it remains highly fragmented," said Nikita Rozenberg, Co-Founder and CEO of Blackwall.
