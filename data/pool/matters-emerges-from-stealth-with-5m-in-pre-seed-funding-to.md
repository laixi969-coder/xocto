# Matters

AI-powered personal finance startup Matters has emerged from stealth with $5 million in pre-seed funding to develop a consumer banking platform focused on helping users manage their spending and increase their savings. The round was led by Swedish angel investor Jakob Johansson, with participation from Knut Frängsmyr (former COO of Klarna), Douglas Stark (Voi co-founder), Johan Bladh (CEO of Röko), Patrik Tran (CEO of Validio), and Per Widerstrom (CEO of Evoke). Matters was founded by Per Österström with the aim of building an AI-native consumer bank that combines automation and personalised financial guidance to help customers improve their financial wellbeing. The company's planned offering combines neobank services with an AI Advisor designed to help users make more informed spending decisions, increase savings and work towards their financial goals. Customers will pay a flat monthly subscription fee, while revenue streams typically retained by banks, such as interchange revenue and foreign exchange fees, will be returned to users through an accumulated cashback system. Through this model, Matters aims to align its business interests with those of its customers, focusing on customer experience, retention and long-term loyalty.
