# Czech startup FLÆ Robotics lands over €1M to automate hotel guest services

Czech technology company FLÆ Robotics has raised more than €1 million in seed funding from Purple Ventures and Garage Angels to further develop its AI-powered hospitality platform, BE-A, and support international expansion. Founded by Filip Linek , FLÆ Robotics develops BE-A, an AI hospitality agent designed to automate routine guest communication, reservations and front-office tasks for hotels. The platform operates around the clock, handling enquiries through WhatsApp, email, web chat and phone calls. Through integrations with hotel management systems, BE-A can check room availability, manage reservations and provide information about hotel services. It also categorises incoming messages and directs requests to the appropriate teams or workflows, helping hotel staff reduce administrative work and focus on guests who require personal attention. The platform is currently used by more than 60 hotels across the United Kingdom, Germany and the Czech Republic. According to FLÆ Robotics, BE-A processed approximately 265,000 messages and voice interactions across nearly 160,000 conversations in July and August 2026, averaging more than 4,000 interactions per day. During the same period, the platform completed more than 3,400 hotel bookings without human intervention.
