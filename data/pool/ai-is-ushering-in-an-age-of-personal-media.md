# AI is ushering in an age of personal media

My mother broke her hip in late 2022, and it triggered a long process of moving out of a house she’d owned since 1987. During the final stretch of cleaning out the place in late 2024, I found myself passing time by composing nonsense songs in my head. “Nasty rotten rusty moldy trash,” I sang to myself repeatedly as I hauled bag after bag of moldy garden supplies out of her dank basement. I’ve recorded music for both personal fun and public release for decades — first using a 16-track digital recorder two decades ago , and using Logic Pro more recently. So I often keep audio notes of song scraps in Voice Memos on my iPhone for later development with vocals, guitars, piano, and drums I play or sequence myself. A man holds some trash. (Photo by Dacharlie via Getty Images) So on that day in November 2024, I set down the trash bags and took a moment to sing the song I was hearing in my head into my phone, and then went on with my cleanout duties. Many months later, in mid-2025, I decided to test the latest version of the AI music generator service Suno. Version
