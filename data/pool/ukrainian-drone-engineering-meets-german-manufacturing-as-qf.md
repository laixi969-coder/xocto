# Ukrainian drone engineering meets German manufacturing as QFI ramps up production

Quantum Systems and Frontline Robotics are expanding their industrial cooperation through a programme for long-term support capacity in Ukraine aimed at systems developed by Frontline Robotics and produced in Germany by Quantum Frontline Industries (QFI). The programme is the next stage of the partnership the two companies established in December 2025 through (QFI), their joint venture for manufacturing Ukrainian-developed unmanned systems at scale. QFI began manufacturing Frontline Robotics’ Linza 3.0 multi-purpose drone in Germany in February 2026. With its first contract for the delivery of 10,000 Linza systems to Ukraine approaching completion, the joint venture has secured a larger follow-on contract with the National Guard of Ukraine for 2027, covering both Linza multi-purpose drones and Zoom reconnaissance drones. QFI plans to expand its production capacity fivefold in 2027. “The additional commitment represents a further step to strengthen Ukraine’s domestic defence-industrial base and build long-term capacity to support Ukrainian-developed unmanned systems. Through QFI, we’ve shown what Ukrainian engineering and German manufacturing can achieve together. As our co-production scales in Germany, the infrastructure that supports these systems in Ukraine needs to grow alongside it,” said Matthias Lehna, Vice President at Quantum Systems and Managing Director of QFI.
