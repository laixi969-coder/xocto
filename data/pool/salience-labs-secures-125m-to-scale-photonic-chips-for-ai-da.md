# Salience Labs secures $125M to scale photonic chips for AI data centres

Salience Labs , a developer of photonic chips for AI data centre connectivity, has raised $125 million in an oversubscribed Series B funding round to accelerate the deployment of its optical switching technology and expand its manufacturing operations. The round was led by Bullhound Capital , with participation from AMD Ventures, Intel Capital, SBI Group, Hudson River Trading and British Business Bank. Existing investors Applied Ventures, Oxford Science Enterprises, Cambridge Innovation Capital and Silicon Catalyst Ventures also participated. As part of the investment, Callum Stewart, Principal at Bullhound Capital, will join Salience Labs' Board of Directors. Salience Labs develops optical circuit switching (OCS) technology designed to improve connectivity between GPUs in AI data centres. Its fully integrated, all-optical switching architecture is designed to connect thousands of GPUs across multiple racks, addressing growing demands on network performance, efficiency and power consumption as AI computing infrastructure expands. Built on more than a decade of research in silicon photonics, the company's integrated chip architecture is designed for high-volume manufacturing and deployment across AI data centre infrastructure.
