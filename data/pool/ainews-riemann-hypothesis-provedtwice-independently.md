# [AINews] Riemann Hypothesis proved...twice independently

Within a few hours of each other ( timeline ), two mathematicians claimed independent proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis, the most famous open problem in mathematics ( Scientific American ). Levent Alpöge of Anthropic first made his Claude-driven proof in a note released with Lean code (1.3GB worth, ~250,000 page equivalent) rather than a full paper. Elliot Glazer confirms that the formalized statement is GRH and puts the chance of a Lean bug well under 1%. So if it finishes compiling with no extra axioms, it is probably correct, and no human will ever have read all of it… …prompting Alessandro Masullo to release his 940 page, 100% human , work in progress proof he had been working on for the last 12 years (and had been undergoing secret manual review and revision per Kevin Hartnett ). This is the third in the trilogy of frontier math breakthroughs, following from the Navier Stokes solutions and the Quasi-Riemann bounding, but it doesn’t feel over. OpenAI's September Navier-Stokes claim is still the cautionary tale: critics argued that its Lean proof did not match the natural-language result.
