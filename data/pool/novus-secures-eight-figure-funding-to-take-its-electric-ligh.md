# NOVUS secures eight-figure funding to take its electric Lightbike from Germany to California

Mobility company NOVUS has closed a double-digit million funding round, led by Berlin- and San Francisco-based venture capital firm 468 Capital . The NOVUS One is a lightweight electric two-wheeler that sits somewhere between an e-bike, a scooter, and a motorcycle. Designed for everyday urban travel, it combines the straightforward operation of an electric bicycle with the speed and performance of a motorcycle, without a clutch or conventional gears.
