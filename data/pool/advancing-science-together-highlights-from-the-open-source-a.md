# Advancing Science Together: Highlights From the Open Source AI For Science Summit

From studying the brain to discovering new materials, open source AI is giving scientists new ways to tackle difficult problems. Last month, Meta’s Fundamental AI Research (FAIR) team brought researchers from universities, hospitals, and industry together at our Menlo Park, California, headquarters for the Open Source AI for Science Summit. Over two days, attendees explored how openly shared AI models, tools, and data can advance healthcare, neuroscience, chemistry, materials science, and mathematics. Hands-on workshops also gave attendees a chance to collaborate directly with the scientists behind Meta’s models. Here are a few highlights. Advancing Medical Research and Real-World Applications We announced an expansion of the Digital Brain Project , launched with the Rothschild Hospital Foundation and the University of Montreal. The project funds researchers to collect de-identified brain data and share it openly. We’re increasing the project’s data target from 15,000 hours of brain data to 25,000, to help researchers understand human intelligence and explore ways to improve neurological health. Researchers are also exploring how AI can support greater independence for people with disabilities.The University of Pittsburgh presented an assistive-robotics system built on DINOv3 and SAM 3 .
