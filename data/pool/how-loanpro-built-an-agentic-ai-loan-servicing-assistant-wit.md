# How LoanPro built an agentic AI loan servicing assistant with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

This post is co-written with Mariah Miller from AWS and Colin Terry from LoanPro Have you ever wondered what the experience is like for the person on the other end of the phone when you call your bank for support? When a borrower calls a lender, the answering agent must pull account data from dozens of fields, transaction histories, and workflow queues to orient themselves before they can help the borrower. All the while, the clock runs. Assembling that context by hand means longer calls, inconsistent service quality, and agent frustration. A loan servicing assistant built on agentic AI changes that. LoanPro , the operating system for modern lenders, set out to change that. Working with AWS Partner AllCloud and the AWS Generative AI Innovation Center , LoanPro built a multi-agent AI assistant that gives servicing agents immediate access to synthesized borrower context and actionable next-step recommendations. Early results show customer call times reduced by up to 15 percent, according to AllCloud’s published case study. In this post, we walk through the business challenge, the agentic AI architecture built on Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore , and the outcomes LoanPro achieved.
