# Slice Into GFN Thursday: ‘Phantom Blade Zero’ Launches on GeForce NOW

This Halloween week, GFN Thursday is delivering a fresh batch of gaming treats. Something wicked this blade comes. S-GAME’s Phantom Blade Zero launches on GeForce NOW today, bringing a scarily good blend of martial arts and punk fantasy to the cloud. There’s also something on the horizon for adventurers: World of Warcraft: Forever arrives Wednesday, Nov. 4 at 3 p.m. PST, with a launch-week access reward coming for GeForce NOW members. This week’s lineup also includes nine games streaming from the GeForce NOW library , alongside new mod support for Cyberpunk 2077 and The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered . That means more options to play, with no downloads or installs to haunt gamers’ devices . There’s another sweet update for gamers in India: The option to sign up for four-month Performance and Ultimate memberships is launching tomorrow. Visit the GeForce NOW website for more information. Cold Steel Meets Cloud Power Framed for his master’s murder and gravely wounded, a warrior named Soul has 66 days to uncover a conspiracy threatening the martial world in S-GAME’s Phantom Blade Zero .
