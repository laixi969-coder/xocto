# 📈 Monday data: Turbulence is getting weirder, and nobody knows why

Hello, here. I was on a long-haul red-eye flight last week that hit turbulence so bad it was hard to sleep. Being a scientist, I started looking into turbulence data to understand what it is and how it’s changed over time. What surprised me was where conditions favored clear-air turbulence, the kind of turbulence that often forms near jet streams and that ordinary onboard weather radar can’t detect. It accounted for 29% of turbulence-related accidents on US airlines between 2009 and 2018 1 .
