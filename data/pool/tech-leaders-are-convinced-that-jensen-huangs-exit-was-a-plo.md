# Tech leaders are convinced that Jensen Huang’s exit was a plot against him

Jensen Huang's stunning resignation from Nvidia after more than 33 years has sent shockwaves throughout the tech industry. Now prominent figures are calling for Huang's reinstatement, and many are also hopping on board with a theory spreading throughout Silicon Valley: that the scandal is the result of a plot against the former CEO. At the center of this theory is the reporting from The New York Times that former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, one of multiple women Huang is accused of having an affair with, is a Chinese spy.
