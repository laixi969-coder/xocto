# Wilgot raises $3M in seed funding to help brands get recommended by AI

Stockholm-based startup Wilgot , which develops technology to help brands improve their product visibility in AI assistants, has raised $3 million in a seed funding round led by Industrifonden . The round also included participation from Luminar Ventures, Foundry Ventures, Ventures Together and Inovexus, alongside angel investors Johan Bäckarlin and Johan Hjelte, founders of Voyado, and Niclas Mollin, founder of inriver and Occtoo. Founded in 2024 by Erik Wikander (CEO), Dr Mhairi Dunion (AI Scientist) and Oleg Danylenko (CTO), Wilgot helps brands adapt their product information to changing consumer shopping behaviour. Consumers are increasingly using AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Perplexity to discover products and receive shopping recommendations. Rather than searching for specific items, users can describe their needs in natural language and receive suggestions based on their preferences. This shift is changing how brands reach potential customers, as traditional product descriptions and marketing content may not provide AI assistants with the information needed to recommend relevant products. Wilgot addresses this challenge through an approach it calls Agentic Product Optimisation (APO), which focuses on improving product information rather than simply tracking how frequently AI assistants mention a brand.
