# FCC signs off on SpaceX’s plan to put up to 1 million data center satellites into space

In an announcement on Wednesday , the FCC says it authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million AI chip-equipped satellites as part of the Elon Musk-owned company's "Starmind" initiative . With several others also pursuing the idea of putting AI data centers in space , including Google and Blue Origin, this is the first approval the agency has granted for a full "constellation" of AI satellites. The FCC's approval requires SpaceX to deploy at least one satellite by 2033, and meet the 1 million satellite milestone by 2040.
