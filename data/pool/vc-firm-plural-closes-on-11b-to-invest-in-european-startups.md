# VC Firm Plural Closes On $1.1B To Invest In European Startups With Potential For ‘GDP-Level Impact’

Early-stage startup investor Plural says it has closed on its largest fund to date — just over $1.1 billion, or 1 billion euros — to lead early-stage investments across Europe. With the announcement, Plural joins a select group of European venture firms that have raised funds of a billion dollars or more in recent years. Others include Balderton Capital , Atomico and Index Ventures , which is co-headquartered in London and San Francisco. It also follows a Q3 that marked Europe’s strongest venture funding quarter in years . “Our common theme is ambition. We go after the founders that are building the most ambitious things we can humanly find,” Sten Tamkivi , a co-founder of the firm, said in an interview with Crunchbase News. “Our stated objective remains to have a GDP-level impact on Europe with the companies that we back.” Plural partners from left: Ian Hogarth, Taavet Hinrikus, Khaled Helioui, Carina Namih, Sten Tamkivi and Pierre-Dimitri Gore-Coty. (Courtesy photo) Plural’s portfolio is a mix of energy, compute, defense, robotics, health and software.
