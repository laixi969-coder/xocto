# Genjustsu-Open-Source-Workflow

Open-source AI video replacement workflow by TEIN. Combine depth maps, SAM segmentation, face meshes, and audio processing to replace characters and objects with Seedance. Includes a local web UI to prepare inputs, review masks, preview generations, and export with original audio.
