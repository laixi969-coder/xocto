# Acquirers Are Snapping Up Boom-Era Startups That Haven’t Raised Funding In Years

When a venture-backed startup goes a few years without raising a new round, it’s common to presume its odds of a sizable exit have gone down as well. But that’s often not the case, an analysis of Crunchbase data shows. This year, a number of larger acquisitions of private companies involved startups that haven’t disclosed a new investment for more than four years. Several of these acquisitions are quite recent — and in some cases, quite large. The standout is Uber ’s purchase earlier this month of ezCater , a platform for catering and workplace meals, for $2.3 billion in cash. The deal comes nearly five years after Boston-based ezCater raised its last reported venture funding round. That’s not the only one. A number of companies whose names might feature on a playlist of 2021 boom-era unicorns have sold to acquirers in recent months, including one-time highfliers like Airtable , Headspace and Miro . In many cases, companies are selling at valuations which, while not puny, are still far below their former peaks.
