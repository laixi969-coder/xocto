# Scaling Linear’s GTM to $100M ARR — Cristina Cordova, COO

Open video on YouTube ↗ Linear was founded to build a better issue tracker. Seven years and $134 million in funding later, its CEO Karri Saarinen published a post titled "Issue tracking is dead." In this episode, we chat with Cristina Cordova , COO of Linear , about how the company navigated that and how its go-to-market changed along the way. Cristina spent seven and a half years at Stripe, led platform and partnerships at Notion, and was a partner at First Round before joining Linear in 2023. Linear's founders built it after living with legacy issue trackers at Coinbase, Airbnb and Uber, and it spread self-serve through startups like Vercel and Ramp. When she joined, Linear had about 40 people and one salesperson. Since then it has shipped an MCP server, a platform for building agents into Linear, integrations with Codex, Cursor and GitHub Copilot, and its own Linear Agent. Agents created 3% of Linear's work a year ago. Now it's more than half.
