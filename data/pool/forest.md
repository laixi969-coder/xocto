# forest

FOREST is a research agent for long-horizon science, where evidence gets lost, errors compound, and goals drift. It explores ideas, reads literature, forms hypotheses, runs real experiments, analyzes results, revises paths, and builds a full paper—all in an editable graph you can inspect, branch, redirect, with claims and figures linked to evidence
