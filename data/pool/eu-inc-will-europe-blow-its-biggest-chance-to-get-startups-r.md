# EU Inc: Will Europe blow its biggest chance to get startups right?

Yesterday, the founders of EU–INC issued an urgent public appeal to Europe's Heads of State and Government, warning that months of negotiations are stripping away the very provisions that would make the proposed pan-European company framework worthwhile. Their message was blunt: "Dear Europe, why are you killing EU Inc?" In their statement, the campaigners warn: "The EU Inc you championed – one that will enable Europe to compete at scale – no longer exists." I've been covering EU Inc since the movement launched in October 2024, interviewing its founders, speaking to investors, and writing repeatedly about the obstacles European startups face when trying to scale across borders. And I have to say, I'm getting increasingly frustrated. Europe has spent years commissioning reports, hosting summits, and discussing its competitiveness problem. We know the issues. We have heard from the founders and investors affected by them. And finally, there was a proposal with the potential to make a meaningful difference. So why is it so difficult to deliver?
