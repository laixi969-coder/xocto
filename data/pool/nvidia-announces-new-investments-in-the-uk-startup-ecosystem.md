# NVIDIA Announces New Investments in the UK Startup Ecosystem

Across industries, the U.K. is putting the full stack to work. T he scale of deployment is growing fast, from enterprises transforming their businesses to startups solving some of the planet’s hardest problems. NVIDIA is putting that principle into practice, including through a five-year, $250 million compute commitment to strengthen the U.K.’s agentic safety and security research. NVIDIA’s Investments in the UK NVIDIA has been investing in the U.K. startup ecosystem, supporting companies including Basecamp Research , Ineffable Intelligence , Synthesia , Wayve and many more. Today, NVIDIA announced new planned investments in Boltz , Foresight , Hiverge, Prima Mente and Riverlane . Boltz , a London-based AI startup founded by MIT researchers, builds biomolecular models and AI agents that help scientists predict molecular structures and interactions, and design potential new medicines. Foresight, a London-based startup, helps owners of major infrastructure projects, including hyperscale data centers, predict completion dates, mitigate risks and accelerate time to revenue. Hiverge , a Cambridge-based AI startup founded by researchers from Google DeepMind and the University of Cambridge, builds an AI discovery engine that automatically writes and optimizes algorithms for tasks such as faster AI training and complex logistics planning.
