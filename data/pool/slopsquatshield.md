# SlopSquatShield

SlopSquatShield checks the packages that you or your AI coding agent install, and blocks names that don't exist or look like slopsquatting traps. It supports npm, PyPI and Maven, and it runs in your terminal, in AI agent hooks, on git commit and in your IDE.
