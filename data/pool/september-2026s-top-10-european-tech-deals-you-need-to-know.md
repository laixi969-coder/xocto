# September 2026's top 10 European tech deals you need to know about

European tech funding rebounded sharply in September , reaching €15.8 billion across 300 deals, up from €3.2 billion and 165 deals in August. Funding value increased by almost 394 per cent month on month, while deal volume rose by around 82 per cent. The UK remained Europe’s leading market, attracting €7.3 billion, or around 46 per cent of the total. Cloud was the top-funded sector with €5.5 billion, replacing AI, which led in August. Exit activity remained broadly stable, with 39 transactions compared with 37 in August. The contrast between funding value and deal activity offers a more nuanced view of where investment momentum is building.
