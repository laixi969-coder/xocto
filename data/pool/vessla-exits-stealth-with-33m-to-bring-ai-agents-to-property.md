# Vessla

Stockholm-based AI startup Vessla has emerged from stealth with a $3.3 million pre-seed funding round led by Seedcamp . Other participants included Inception Fund, Tiny VC, operators from Lovable and OpenAI, founders Lukas Saari (Tandem Health) and Fredrik Hjelm (Voi, Pit), and several angel investors. The round was more than twice oversubscribed. Founded earlier this year, Vessla is building an AI-powered platform designed to automate workflows across the systems used by residential and commercial property owners and managers. The company is already working with some of the Nordics' largest property owners and operators. Property operations involve coordination between tenants, managers, suppliers and finance teams, often requiring information to be transferred manually between different systems. Tasks such as maintenance requests can involve multiple processes, from reviewing property records and contracts to managing invoices and communicating with suppliers. Vessla aims to simplify these processes by providing a shared AI layer that connects existing business systems. Its AI agents can retrieve relevant information, apply an organisation's operating rules and execute workflows across connected software.
