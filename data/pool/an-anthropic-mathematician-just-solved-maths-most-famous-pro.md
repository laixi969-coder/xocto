# An Anthropic mathematician just solved math’s most famous problem

On Friday, November 20, Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge and NYU professor Tristan Buckmaster announced they had found a solution to the Navier-Stokes problem that was stronger than the one OpenAI released back in September. This would have been huge news if it hadn’t been overshadowed by something even more astonishing. The same day, Alpöge announced that he, working with Claude, had discovered a proof of math’s most famous problem: the Riemann Hypothesis. Alpöge did not publish a traditional paper written in English. Instead, he released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis written in Lean, a programming language that allows mathematical arguments to be expressed precisely enough to be verified by a computer.
