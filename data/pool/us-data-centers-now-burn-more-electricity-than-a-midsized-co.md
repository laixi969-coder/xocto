# US data centers now burn more electricity than a midsized country

Fossil fuels are still the biggest source of electricity for hyperscale data centers in the US, a new paper published in the journal Nature Sustainability shows. They likely burn through more electricity collectively than many countries do, resulting in a serious amount of greenhouse gas emissions. The study homes in on hyperscale facilities - the massive structures spanning millions of square feet that developers are racing to build in large part to support generative AI. Researchers say the findings show that there's a lot of work left to do to cut down pollution stemming from AI's reliance on fossil fuels.
