# OllyGarden

Observability platform OllyGarden has raised $4 million in venture capital funding to further develop its technology for improving telemetry quality. The round was backed by Next Frontier Capital, Grand Ventures and ACTAI Ventures, with existing investor DIG Ventures also participating. Observability companies Datadog, Grafana Labs and Dash0 made additional strategic investments. Launched in 2025, OllyGarden helps companies manage the quality of telemetry data used to monitor their software systems. Working with businesses across retail, financial services and enterprise software, the company addresses the growing volume of unreliable and unnecessary data, which can increase costs and make it harder for engineers to identify relevant information. Its platform continuously evaluates telemetry quality, identifies problems and turns findings into fixes at the source, preventing unreliable data from reaching observability backends. This allows engineers and AI agents to work with more accurate information while reducing the costs of storing and processing unnecessary data. Over the past year, OllyGarden has helped companies identify opportunities to reduce log volumes by up to 85 per cent. Alongside the funding announcement, OllyGarden has introduced Minimum Viable Instrumentation (MVI), a new capability for its AI OpenTelemetry Engineer, Rose.
