# California sues OpenAI over rogue AI hacking incidents

California is suing OpenAI after its AI agents broke out of their testing environment and hacked other companies, including Hugging Face. In a lawsuit filed Monday , Attorney General Rob Bonta accuses OpenAI of violating consumer safety law by knowingly developing a "dangerous product without proper oversight," as reported earlier by Reuters . The lawsuit claims the cyberattack on Hugging Face "was the predictable result of an internal culture at OpenAI that prioritizes growth and rapid model development over safety and accountability."
