# OakNorth wins US banking licence

OakNorth , the UK SME lender, has won a US banking licence, it said today, allowing it to offer services beyond lending including checking and savings accounts to businesses in the US, which now accounts for 40 per cent of its lending business. The digital bank, which is backed by Japanese investor SoftBank and GIC, the Singaporean state fund, said it’s the first foreign digital bank to secure a US banking licence, after winning regulatory approval from the Federal Reserve and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency. OakNorth applied for US regulatory approval through its acquisition of Community Unity Bank , an FDIC-insured lender in Michigan, which it acquired in 2025. Earlier this year, the Federal Reserve told OakNorth to move its domicile from Jersey to the UK to clear its purchase of Community Unity Bank. The UK challenger bank, which launched in the UK in 2015 and has lent over $24bn, first entered the US market in 2023 and has provided $3.5bn in lending to American businesses. In the US, the bank supports established and entrepreneur-led businesses typically generating $5m–$100m in annual revenues, typically providing financing ranging from $5m to $50m.
