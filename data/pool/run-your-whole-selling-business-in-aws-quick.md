# Run your whole selling business in AWS Quick

Most sellers do not need another AI product to learn. They need the tools and information they already use brought together, so they can see what is happening in their business and act on it. AWS Quick and the Amazon Selling Partner plugin do that together. Quick brings an Amazon seller’s sales data, inventory signals, and marketplace intelligence into one place, where questions become answers and answers become actions. It understands the seller’s full business context, so the seller does not need to re-explain their catalog, their margins, or their competitive landscape in every conversation. It is built on AWS, the same infrastructure Amazon uses to run its own marketplace. At Amazon Accelerate on September 23, 2026, Amazon announced the Selling Partner plugin and launched it in beta in AWS Quick. This post covers what launched, how to claim the free AWS Quick offer, and what the experience looks like day to day. It also covers how the skills and connectors work when a seller puts them to use. Limited-time offer: 12 months of AWS Quick Plus, free. Every primary selling account holder worldwide can claim a free 12-month subscription, plus access for 10 additional users you delegate.
