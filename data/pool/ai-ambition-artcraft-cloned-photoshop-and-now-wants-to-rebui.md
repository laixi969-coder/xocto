# AI Ambition: ArtCraft Cloned Photoshop and Now Wants to "Rebuild Everything"

The theme of day two of the AI Engineer Code Summit in San Francisco was increasing ambition using AI . And there have been few projects as ambitious as ArtCraft in the AI era. So far the fledgling company has released 17 open source versions of popular software tools , starting with a Photoshop alternative built from scratch in Rust called PhotoCraft . You could feel the excitement in the room this morning when ArtCraft founder and CEO Brandon Thomas presented a kind of manifesto for AI-based open source at AIE Code.
