# ClawTank

Open-source RDP server for macOS. Use your Mac mini or Mac Studio from a MacBook, Windows, iPad or Linux with stock Windows App, mstsc or FreeRDP. Hardware H.264, virtual displays at the client's resolution, audio and clipboard. LAN-only by default, no cloud. Rust (IronRDP) and Swift.
