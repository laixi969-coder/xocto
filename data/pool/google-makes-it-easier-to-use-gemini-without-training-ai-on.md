# Google makes it easier to use Gemini without training AI on your data

It can be hard to use Google products—or even the Internet itself—without coming into contact with Gemini AI. That is, of course, by design . The more you use Gemini, the more data Google collects to train and improve its generative AI chatbot. The options to limit training on your data have been scant, but Google is finally adding more controls to the Gemini app. The change currently rolling out to Gemini will separate your chat history from training, which allows you to retain basic AI functionality without handing Google quite so much control over your data. Prior to this change, the only way to opt out of contributing your chat contents (including attachments) for training was to turn off your history. That made it impossible to go back and pick up a chat down the road. As the new settings roll out, users will see separate toggles for model training and chat history. The history setting will continue to support an adjustable limit, but you will no longer have to change it to stop Google from training on your Gemini usage.
