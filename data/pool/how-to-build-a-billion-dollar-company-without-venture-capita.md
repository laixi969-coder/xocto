# How To Build A Billion-Dollar Company Without Venture Capital

By Nacho De Marco Today’s venture capital ecosystem looks markedly different from even just five years ago. Most significantly, a small number of AI companies are now attracting the vast majority of available venture capital. In Q1 2026, just four companies collectively raised $188 billion , which alone accounted for 65% of total global venture investment, per Crunchbase data. Meanwhile, in that same quarter, just 20% of global VC funding went to non-AI firms . Deal count was also down 30%, meaning that checks are being made out to fewer companies all around. So what happens to startups when the VC route is all but closed? I built a company into a multibillion-dollar business without any venture funding. Here’s a few lessons I picked up along the way: Prioritize customers and revenue, not fundraising Nacho De Marco, founding GP of BDev Ventures, and chairman of BairesDev.
