# burp-sectivai

Sectiv is a  skill-driven AI security review for Burp Suite. Pick captured traffic and a review goal (IDOR, XSS, SQLi, SSRF…),     and get a severity dashboard with description, steps to reproduce, impact, PoC, CWE/CVE and HTML/MD/JSON export. Built with     Kotlin + Swing on the Montoya API.
