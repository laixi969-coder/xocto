# Pegasus 1.5 from TwelveLabs is now available in Amazon Bedrock

On Oct 9, 2026 we announced the availability of TwelveLabs’ Pegasus 1.5 on Amazon Bedrock . Pegasus 1.5 is a video language model that turns long-form video into structured, timestamped metadata. You describe the moments that matter to your workflow, and the model returns every matching segment as JSON. Video represents a significant and complex category of unstructured data, yet most of it remains unsearchable and underutilized. Extracting structured, actionable insights from that content has historically required labor-intensive manual review or fragmented toolchains. With earlier versions of Pegasus, you can point the model at a clip, ask a question, and get an answer. Now, Pegasus 1.5 analyzes the entire video, finds every segment that matches user-defined criteria, and returns structured, timestamped metadata. It also introduces video segmentation, multimodal prompting with reference images, and on-the-fly processing. With Pegasus 1.5 on Amazon Bedrock, you can analyze videos directly from an Amazon Simple Storage Service (Amazon S3) URI or a base64 string with no pre-indexing required, and your data remains within the AWS data boundary.
