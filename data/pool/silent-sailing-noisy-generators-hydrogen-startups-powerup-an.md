# Silent sailing, noisy generators: Hydrogen startups PowerUP and H2Boat tackle yachting's diesel problem

PowerUP Energy Technologies , the Estonian company renowned for its advanced hydrogen fuel cell-based electric generators, today announced a strategic partnership with H2Boat , an Italian marine hydrogen technology startup developing integrated solutions for onboard hydrogen production, storage, and use. Check out our earlier interview with PowerUP CEO Ivar Kruusenberg. The partnership aims to give yacht owners and shipyards a practical pathway to quieter, cleaner onboard energy systems by combining complementary technologies to deliver a complete, integrated zero-emission energy solution. H2Boat, a spin-off from the University of Genoa, brings core expertise in marine hydrogen systems and proprietary metal hydride storage technology. Its solutions are engineered for on-board integration, addressing a key challenge for hydrogen in the maritime sector: storing hydrogen safely and efficiently in a form compatible with the constraints of recreational and professional vessels. The joint solution is designed to eliminate vibrations, noise, and greenhouse gas emissions, returning only pure water to the environment in a perfect circular-economy application. "As a lifelong sailor, I know firsthand the absolute peace of being out on the open water, completely in tune with nature. It's like a guillotine dropping when you start a diesel generator," said Dr Ivar Kruusenberg, CEO and Founder
