nanoMuse: an open-source personal agent for every device you own — one agent with a name and a face that does things, keeps working while the app is closed, remembers you, and asks before anything you cannot undo. In the browser, on Android, on Windows / macOS / Linux; your phone's screen and your PC as its hands.
