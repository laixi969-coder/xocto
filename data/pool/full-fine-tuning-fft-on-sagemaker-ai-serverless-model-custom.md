# Full Fine-Tuning (FFT) on SageMaker AI Serverless Model Customization

Fine-tuning is the process of continuing to train a pretrained large language model (LLM) on a company’s own data so it adapts to a specific domain, task, or style rather than relying on general-purpose knowledge alone. For businesses, the value is twofold. A fine-tuned model delivers measurably better accuracy on domain-specific work while reducing costs: a smaller specialized model can match or exceed a frontier model on your task at a fraction of the per-token inference price. Those savings compound across millions of requests. Fine-tuning has therefore become an essential process for businesses looking to get more value from LLMs at lower costs. Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) methods like Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) have emerged as a practical solution for fine-tuning LLMs to downstream tasks, reducing GPU capacity requirements by updating only a small subset of LLM parameters. LoRA aims to strike a balance between efficiency and expressiveness, however, the low-rank nature of the weight update inherently limits the representation power of LoRA fine-tuned models, potentially compromising performance on complex tasks. This trade-off hampers the ability of LoRA methods to capture complex patterns needed for diverse tasks.
