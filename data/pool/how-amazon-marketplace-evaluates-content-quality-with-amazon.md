# How Amazon Marketplace evaluates content quality with Amazon SageMaker AI

A+ Content is the rich media widget on the Amazon product detail page. Through visual assets, brands communicate unique product and brand value to help customers make informed purchase decisions. Selling partners using high-quality A+ Content boost their sales by 8–20 percent compared to those without it. A wide variety of brands, from established brands with large in-house design teams to bootstrapped brands with limited resources, use A+ Content, creating inconsistency in content quality. A typical A+ Content project combines text, images, and video into a cohesive product story. Evaluating each asset in isolation misses the quality that emerges from how those assets work together. When your content spans multiple heterogeneous modules and you need a holistic quality signal rather than per-asset checks, the multi-model fusion approach described in this post can help. In this post, we examine this problem in the context of A+ Content. A+ Content provides creative freedom for selling partners to express their authentic selves on their product detail pages, without strict content specifications such as image ratio and text word count. Although this flexibility allows compelling brand storytelling, it also creates substantial variation in content composition and quality.
