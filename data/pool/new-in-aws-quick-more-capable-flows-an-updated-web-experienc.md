# New in AWS Quick: More capable flows, an updated web experience, and agents that work together

Turn one person’s process into your team’s process, get more done in the Quick web app, and get more complete answers from agents working together. AWS Quick is the AI workspace built only for work. Today, we are releasing updates that help your team get more of its everyday work done: Build, see, and improve business processes, all through conversation. Describe a process in chat and Quick builds a flow to orchestrate agents, skills, and tools with access to structured data, knowledge, and apps. New deterministic steps help make your workflows consistent and verifiable, now with longer-running workflows and support for large data volumes. Triage your day and keep agents working in the Quick web app. The updated web app puts what needs your attention and a daily briefing on your home screen, adds the agents and skills you use on desktop, and keeps your work running after you close the tab. Complete work spanning multiple agents through a single prompt. With multi-agent orchestration, several specialized agents work on your request at the same time, and Quick combines what they find into one response. Bring more of your data into Quick.
