# Meta premieres India’s First Government-Led Microdrama Series on Scams Awareness

Meta has partnered with The Ministry of Information & Broadcasting (MIB), Prasar Bharati, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), and Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) to launch India’s first government-led public interest microdrama series on online scams. The series will premiere on Instagram and will soon be available on Waves OTT. The “Scam se Bacho” microdrama will be Waves OTT’s inaugural series with Meta that aims to educate audiences on some of the most prevalent online scams today, including fake job offers , investment and digital arrest scams, reiterating the importance of reporting suspicious behaviour online and encouraging users to call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 in case of fraud. Through an engaging, social-first approach, this initiative builds on the growing popularity of microdramas and demonstrates how innovative storytelling formats can be harnessed to make important public interest information more accessible and relatable. Scam Se Bacho: Raising Awareness Through A Whole-Of-Society Approach Building on Meta’s flagship anti-scam campaign “Scam se Bacho” – this latest initiative expands our educational efforts and reinforces the importance of tackling online scams through a whole-of-society approach by bringing together government agencies, creators and industry partners to reach audiences through India’s fastest-growing storytelling format.
