# From data to decisions: Conversational supply chain intelligence with Amazon Quick and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

Giving supply chain teams direct access to the systems behind their decisions. Every supply chain generates constant signals: orders in approval, shifting lead times, slowing ports, drifting prices, disruptions. The problem isn’t scarce data. It’s the distance between the data and the decision-maker. Answering “which top-revenue SKUs are exposed to the current port closure, and what will it cost?” used to mean knowing which system held each piece and stitching them together by hand while the domain expert waited. Could that manager instead ask in plain language and get an actionable, cross-system answer in seconds? Amazon Bedrock AgentCore is the intelligence layer, exposing enterprise Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers as a composable tool layer. Amazon Quick turns that into action for personas with no setup. Answers come from live systems of record and grounded documents, not model recall, and every change waits for a named human. In this post, we walk through this pattern end to end, from a conversational walkthrough in Amazon Quick to the AgentCore tool layer behind it, and we show you how to deploy it yourself.
