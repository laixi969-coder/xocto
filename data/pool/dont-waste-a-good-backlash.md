# 🔮 Don’t waste a good backlash

The market ran for cover after the Democrats won the midterms. More than $2.5 trillion was wiped off the Nasdaq as investors fled the picks-and-shovels part of the AI trade. The Democrats made it clear that artificial intelligence would now face the scrutiny we would expect most technologies to face. Some said it more forcefully. Those concerns are around AI safety, its impact on labor markets, the concentration of power and, of course, the pushback on data centers. The data center revolt could be the sanest thing to happen to the AI build-out this year. Voters in the US passed 43 of 44 local measures to slow data centers down. This isn’t the death knell of AI, but the price to make it better. Hyperscalers will pay almost anything for speed, and the places willing to move faster will be in the driving seat. And they should turn that leverage into lasting benefits for their communities. Data centers were not why the vote went the way it did.
