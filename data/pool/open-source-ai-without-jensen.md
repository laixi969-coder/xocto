# Open-source AI without Jensen

The resignation of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is a major setback for the US development of an open model ecosystem. Nvidia still has the financial interests in supporting open models, which are to avoid concentration in power among a few customers who would develop their own chips and reduce Nvidia’s profits, but supporting the open ecosystem is a tricky puzzle more than an engineering problem to grind out a solution on. Nvidia’s business operations are famously extremely dependent on Jensen as an operator, so it is a consensus opinion that there are challenges ahead (or even more high-profile departures through chip-smuggling investigations or plain-old sharks circling more poachable talent). Nvidia’s number one goal is to stabilize the ship, deliver to customers in the next 24 months, and prevent implosion. Open models have been one of Jensen’s more ambitious, founder-style bets.
