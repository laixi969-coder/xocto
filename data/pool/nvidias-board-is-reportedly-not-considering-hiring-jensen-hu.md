# Nvidia’s board is reportedly ‘not considering’ hiring Jensen Huang back

A source tells the outlet the board "is focused on an investigation into Huang, and is not in discussions to bring him back." Since Huang's resignation last week , tech executives, along with current and former Nvidia employees, have called for his reinstatement.
