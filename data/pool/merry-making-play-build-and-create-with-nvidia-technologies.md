# Merry Making: Play, Build and Create With NVIDIA Technologies This Holiday Season

The elves don’t have to be the only ones busy in the workshop this holiday season. With the lights twinkling and the gift wrap out, it’s the perfect time to give someone — or yourself — a spark to take a hobby to the next level. For some, that means diving into a new game. For others, it’s video editing or even building a robot. NVIDIA technologies offer something for everyone on Santa’s list. Read on to unwrap the details. Make Time for One More Game With the holiday break here, everyone can be online to score bragging rights or explore a world that’s only been seen in video game trailers. For some players, an upgrade means a desktop that brings out the detail in every scene. For others, it’s a laptop they can take along when visiting family, or a way to play in the cloud on a device they already own. The right setup depends on where and what to play: GeForce RTX 50 Series laptops and desktops power the action. Built on the NVIDIA Blackwell architecture, they feature fifth-generation Tensor Cores and fourth-generation RT Cores for AI-enhanced graphics, detailed ray-traced worlds and high frames-per-second gaming.
